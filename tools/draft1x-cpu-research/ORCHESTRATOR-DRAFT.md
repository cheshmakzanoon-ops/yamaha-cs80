## Process ownership correction
SIGALRM is blocked only during subprocess creation and handle assignment, then restored inside the cleanup-protected try. A Python exec wrapper explicitly restores the original signal mask before executing the requested command; the wrapper argv is recorded. A pending launch alarm therefore triggers group cleanup with a known handle. Timeout/exception and nonzero leader exit kill the owned process group and reap the leader; an already-exited leader is harmless. Three bounded synthetic tests pass: timeout kills leader/descendant, exit7 kills remaining descendant, and an alarm queued during actual spawn-before-assignment leaves no live descendant.
