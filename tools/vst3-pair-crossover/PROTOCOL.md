# Proposed cloud-only native CPU gate for a large idle change (v1; not acquired)

This is a prospectively specified **new** experiment. The [old interleaved control](../vst3-target-qualification/cloud-diagnostic/README.md) rejected ±2% equivalence with the same binary. It remains rejected. This design asks whether a **large** quiet change frees more than 5% of *actual callback-thread CPU* in this Linux guest. It cannot qualify DAW deadlines, physical CPUs, power, instantaneous wake latency or a portable product target. A null result or failed control is a useful terminal outcome. No governor/quota/priority/security changes, external artificial load, user device or simultaneous native timing.

## Why it differs

In the old saved data, S0 and S1 did render the same musical sample positions, but those callbacks were not adjacent: one role ran 17 consecutive callbacks before handing over. Across **6,144** matched S0/S1 callbacks the median wall-start separation was **18.30 ms**, p90 **35.56 ms**. The standard deviation of the **12 process-level twin log ratios** was 0.06326. The individual matched ratios were much noisier (log SD 0.40895). The [offline recomputation](old_design_inputs.py) is descriptive: existing numbers are design inputs, not evidence the new arrangement works.

Use two independently launched processes on **one identical logical vCPU**, each loading precisely one tested module. In measured regions a byte of execution permission passes between those two workers over their own socketpair. Balanced crossover on *matched callback indices* gives A(k), B(k), B(k+1), A(k+1) or its reverse, rather than running the two roles tens of milliseconds apart. Buffers, process contexts, event descriptors, role order and capture storage are allocated/prepared before clocks. The peer stays blocked. IPC, hashing, state reads, buffer handling and acquisition supervision stay outside every inner clock span. A second **four-callback burst** arm in the same fixed timeline is a sensitivity check for switching/cache artifacts; it is not a substitute if the adjacent case fails. Both arms record the same individual clock endpoints.

Keeping the two shared libraries in independent processes avoids accidentally resolving candidate JUCE/DSP symbols to a baseline module in the same address space. Cache eviction, ASLR, guest preemption and unobserved host frequency remain real limitations even if IPC itself is excluded from clocks. No RDTSC-to-cycles or synthetic load/throttle-to-“old CPU” translation. Do not run the arms simultaneously on hypothetical independent physical cores.

## Frozen scene geometry

Pin sample rate 48 kHz, 64 stereo frames, Circuit/Source Performance, factory strings 0/0, ordinary 8-note bank, drift .37, dispersion .18, no FX/no analog noise, inactive external input, kRealtime/sample32, matching the prior real-host fixture. Equal-velocity is MIDI7 102; the alternate eight physical velocities in pitch order 36,43,48,55,60,64,67,72 are **41,53,65,77,89,101,113,125** (each divided by 127 as Float32, no changing levels to make results easier). Observe actual input reads, outputs, full continuation audio, public state and FP. Silence is evidence about output, not hidden voices or zero FIR history.

Every ordinary window has **576 real 64-frame calls per lane**. Both lanes advance every callback, including control-extras, in exactly the same order on the individual instance.

| Scene | Real preparation before the first window call | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Fresh idle | 1,024 callbacks without a note; final 64 preparation blocks and whole window must be below 1e-6 peak | co-primary |
| Released equal | 1,024 sustained blocks starting with the equal chord, eight note-offs, then 8,192 blocks including the note-off block; same quiet guard | co-primary |
| Released varied | Same, but with the eight stated different velocities; no replacing it with equal velocities | mandatory guard and separate opportunity to prove a **broad** idle benefit |
| Held varied | 1,024 sustained blocks after eight varied note-ons, then 576 held blocks; observed window peak >1e-5 | mandatory active guard |
| Wake varied | 16 independent fresh instances; each gets 1,024 quiet blocks followed by 36 measured callbacks starting with the varied chord. Audible after wake; all 128 note-ons verified | mandatory onset-window guard, not a debit/credit for a single note-on |

After every held or quiet timed window, add a fixed **untimed continuation**: for either released scene and fresh idle issue the varied chord on the first of 32 blocks, then its note-offs on the next block after that continuation; for held/wake continue 32 blocks without new note-ons, then note-offs. Preserve state prewindow, postwindow and after continuation; check FP/errno and every stereo sample, not just silence flags. Wake uses this continuation for every placement. Do not silently call “released equal” representative of “released varied”: the shipped cutoff shortcut rarely saves work in the latter; a separate FIR change might.

## Acquisition and control size

**12 time epochs × four distinct logical vCPUs**, run **serially**, never four concurrent pairs. Choose four IDs without observing speeds: sorted allowed CPUs at indices 0, floor(N/4), floor(N/2), floor(3N/4); require four distinct IDs, and one permitted separate CPU for the lightweight supervisor. Example N=9: 0,2,4,6. The CPU order is a counterbalanced rotation, and A/B process launch order and software-to-lane assignment alternate independently by a frozen schedule. All five scenes occur in each pair of fresh worker processes; counterbalance scene order. Epochs are completed time clusters, **not** 48 independent machines. Before advancing to a new epoch, wait until at least 120 seconds have elapsed since its predecessor’s start, and at least 10 seconds since its final worker finished. Do not work around live competing intentional benchmarks. Record absolute bounds in the launch manifest: 75 minutes elapsed/40 pooled worker CPU minutes per stage, 100 seconds per worker-pair slot, and 1 GiB for retained raw data. Keep raw integer clock rows, portable schedules, acquisition receipts and controls; compare preflight PCM/FP/state bytes exactly and archive their reference. Verify the larger transient audio/state streams against this reference outside clocks (length plus SHA-256, or streaming exact comparison) before releasing the temporary memory. Do not throw away any clock row or a failed slot to meet the cap. Any missing slot, timeout or cap failure ends the stage without qualification. Keep all planned/attempted data, errors, environment and monotonic ordering. No “best core,” outlier deletion, restarting to pass, stopping once significant, or changing sample size after reading results.

Measurement is split into 16 consecutive 36-callback blocks per scene; in wake this aligns with each fresh instance. On consecutive blocks alternate burst quantum 1 vs 4 (orientation balanced across epochs and CPUs). In each block the peer switches/retains the turn in a balanced ABBA/BAAB crossover pattern. For q=4 the four calls still get their own unaltered inner endpoints; one additional 4-call visit in each 36-call block has the opposite orientation in its paired neighbor to eliminate fixed first-position imbalance. Ordinary calibration indices exclude one of nine callbacks in each group of nine. Rotate the excluded index over scene/epoch, not always at onset. Across 576 calls there are 512 common indices and 64 genuine extras. **Both** lanes really render them all; don't normalize 576 by 576 in the +12.5% workload-control contrast. The candidate contrast uses **all 576 matched calls in both arms**, not the control denominator.

Stages, with separate prospective seals:
1. **Dry/preflight, no comparative timing.** Pin checkouts, module SHA/build ID, SDK version, exact compiler/build flags, runner/analyzer/host SHA and one immutable schedule. Run zero-clock interleaved stock+stock once per scene; require exact planar PCM, FP-before/after/next, events, continuation and bounded states against isolated controls. Check callback count, quiet and audible guards. Check mocks/adversarial analyzer fixtures (missing/duplicate/forged rows, gross lane biases, misplaced extras, A/A failure, and active-only regression).
2. **Q: placebo only.** Both lanes must resolve the same physical tested stock ELF (**shipped cutoff-main 0ed90e446f1faa6153b0e2f76f074b7da62201be49f76fc9183375e9ff20edf2**, for a *new* FIR treatment on cutoff main). Hard-refuse a different hash or separate copy in this phase. No candidate processes/observations are permitted until Q completes, full raw replay passes every gate below, and a distinct candidate-stage lock is accepted. If Q fails, report **cloud native precision not established** and stop. No weaker gate may turn this Q into a pass.
3. **C: conditional only.** Lock exactly one comparison and role-to-module SHA/file identities before any candidate performance measurement. For standalone FIR attribution use cutoff-main as baseline and the matching FIR-only build; comparing route-only vs combined is a *different question* and cannot credit the FIR or cutoff individually. The existing cutoff comparison would instead require its pinned route baseline and a new Q on that baseline. Full zero-clock public parity in all five new scenes is required. Each slot includes a fresh baseline/baseline sentinel pair as well as the baseline/candidate pair, on the same vCPU; counterbalance their order and launch mapping. Noncandidate phase-Q success does not waive **live** Q controls on these sentinel pairs. Preserve all candidate raw rows even if any live gate fails. Candidate testing is not authorized by an offline/synthetic pass.

## Endpoints, estimator and strict gates

Inside each real call: CLOCK_MONOTONIC start, CLOCK_THREAD_CPUTIME_ID start, `IAudioProcessor::process`, thread CPU end, monotonic end. Preserve success and all four **integer** endpoints by scene, musical index, group, quantum, role and module identity. No subtraction of empty-clock overhead and no use of wall-minus-thread as “stolen cycles.” Outside brackets, measure same-order empty brackets and `getrusage` switches/faults by scene; capture permitted affinity start/end, loaded identity, build ID, current CPU and resource visibility. Supervisor may be pinned off the test vCPU. Window wall/CPU endpoints, actual match-to-match wall gap, and per-quantum gap are diagnostics; neither can be selected for a better result.

For each (scene, epoch, CPU cell), **sum** thread-CPU ns over the fixed indices within each lane, then form log ratios. Do not use the mean of per-callback ratios, count callbacks as inferential observations, or pool all CPUs into a fast-core-weighted raw total. Average the four log contrasts equally inside each epoch. The prescribed 95% t interval uses the **12** epoch contrasts (11 df); report exponentiated estimates, all intervals and epoch/cell totals. The gates are conjunctive; don't infer independent hardware or simultaneous coverage for every descriptive interval. Repeat identical estimator on the first and last six epochs, each CPU stratum and each switching quantum as prespecified diagnostics; don't promote any subset.

**Q and live sentinels, for each of all five scenes:**
- The 95% A/A common-512 difference interval must lie wholly within **±2.5%**. A known-work control A/B comparison measures 576 B callbacks vs 512 common A calls: entire 95% interval inside **+7.5% to +17.5%** and point within **+10% to +15%**. No latency claim follows. Rotated extras and equivalent underlying histories guard against an onset-phase ratio artifact; also report 64 extra calls by position and the corresponding all-576/common-512 ratio of A alone.
- For each quantum, A/A point within ±3%, and for each logical CPU stratum and each temporal half within ±4%. Gross disagreement or failed main interval invalidates that scene; no “valid only where it worked” release. Within **each** scene/epoch/vCPU cell, collect 128 empty brackets per lane after the window; the two lanes’ pooled mean empty-bracket CPU cost must be <2% of its genuine mean call cost and each q=1 median pair-start delay below **three** times the scene's pooled median callback wall duration, to verify prompt handoff. No row is removed for an involuntary switch.
- Every control must pass before C can start. A/A tolerances are half the smallest material 5% effect, not a retrospective rescue of the abandoned ±2% tests. The positive control is known extra **native audio processing**, not synthetic sleep or clock-frequency manipulation. Detecting a predeclared extra *quantity* of counted work does not by itself validate a 5% change to cost per callback at fixed count. The independent A/A, switching, candidate intervals and live controls are therefore essential.

**C:** saving = 1 − exp(mean log(candidate CPU/baseline CPU)). Two-sided 95% interval transformed in the correct order. Require both fresh idle and equal-release lower bounds **strictly above 5%** to call an idle win; lower bounds above **10%** to call a double-digit win. Require each of varied-release, held-varied and wake-varied lower bounds **above −3%** (non-regression), never “not statistically significant.” A **broad** idle win also requires varied-release lower bound >5%; otherwise label any qualified win only for fresh/equal-release. For both primary scenes require positive savings in both temporal halves and all four vCPU strata; both q=1 and q=4 estimates must be positive and differ by <4 percentage points. Report here only a shared-VM average for this authentic DSP workload, together with absolute callback CPU seconds / rendered audio seconds for each image. If the conservative package fails, publish the raw results as inconclusive/unadmitted, including any adverse observed effects.

The 12-epoch design does not guarantee success. From the old 6.33% triplet log SD, even 12 epochs × four CPUs predict roughly a **2.0%** nominal half-width if four cell effects within an epoch were independent; **4.1%** if perfectly correlated. Shared scheduling can be worse, hence use epoch inference and actual control gates rather than a paper power claim. C uses an additional sentinel workload; give C a separately fixed doubled envelope (150 min elapsed/80 pooled worker CPU minutes), including all sentinel cells and any new zero-clock admission. A noisy target is a reason **not** to claim the native CPU win.
