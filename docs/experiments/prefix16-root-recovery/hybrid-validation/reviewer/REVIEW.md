# Independent safeguarded interpolation review

Reviewed current 192-iteration helper only; no production changes or timing. Generic/native/ASan+UBSan permanent fixture runs: 768/768 captured states pass, including strict residual, tuple consistency, baseline reproduction, continuation and guard sentinel checks. Leak detection disabled as in earlier environment tests. Additional deterministic 100,000-equation probe: 81,244 accepted, 18,756 rejected, no assertion failures. The probe includes physical-style nonnegative coefficients, selected negative c1, tiny level, infinite z, maximum finite g and negative ff. Accepted tuples must independently meet the original strict 1e-7 residual; rejected tuples retain all five sentinel fields bitwise. This is not exhaustive proof or hardware fidelity evidence.

Acceptance uses actual residual, never weighted residual or bracket width. Both endpoint signs remain unchanged by nonnegative halving, including signed zero; failed/rounded secant proposals revert to midpoint. Infinite denominator gives an endpoint proposal and therefore midpoint; underflowed weights similarly do not falsely accept. No state/output assignment on failure was introduced. Every third iteration schedules bisection, and all valid interior proposals shrink the bracket. IEEE rounding and clamp discontinuities still preclude a universal success guarantee.

Cost bound changed: old 18 bracket evaluations + 64 bisections = 82 maximum helper evaluations; new 18 + 192 = 210. 'Retains 64 bisections' is not 'retains worst-case cost'. Also weighted values can remain scaled after side switches; they remain sign-correct candidate-selection heuristics, not real endpoint residuals or a claim of canonical Illinois algorithm equivalence.

Recommendation for further experiment: bounded interpolation prefix then a full 64-step bisection tail. 32 + 64 gives at most 114 helper evaluations; 16 + 64 gives 98. This preserves full bracket-contraction opportunity while bounding proposal overhead. Total 64 mixed iterations with only 21 scheduled bisections knowingly weakens that opportunity and needs additional adversarial recovery tests. No variant is approved purely from these arithmetic bounds; compare captured, extreme and clamp-gap outcomes before selection.
