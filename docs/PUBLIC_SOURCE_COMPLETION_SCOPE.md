# Public-source reconstruction scope

Owner clarification, 2026-11-02. This supersedes earlier completion wording that
requires access to an original instrument or newly commissioned hardware captures.

## Fidelity evidence

- Reconstruct behavior from publicly available Yamaha manuals, schematics and
  service information, published scientific and engineering literature, and
  credible third-party technical records.
- Where the public record is incomplete, implement the closest defensible
  engineering estimate. Record its sources, assumptions, alternatives,
  confidence and sensitivity; distinguish source facts from inferred laws.
- Test inferred models against independent numerical references and all relevant
  public constraints. Do not invent observations, hide contradictions, or claim
  a physical one-to-one match that has not been established.
- New owner hardware measurements and technician visits are not prerequisites
  for this project scope. Existing physical-evidence intake documents remain
  optional historical facilities, not outstanding requests to the owner.

## Acceptance requirements that do not change

The eight-voice 48 kHz / 256-frame maximum remains 0.20 times real time, and
quiet idle remains at most 0.02. Registered same-host before/after rounds,
workload identity, numerical equivalence and platform requirements still apply.
The original 885-row audio inventory, rates, musical-click limits, Legacy/V1
compatibility, source identities and numerical thresholds are not relaxed.

The manufacturer's calibration criteria remain comparison constraints. A model
prediction outside them is still a miss, even when its assumptions are declared.
Estimation is not permission to add arbitrary gain or disguise a failed score.
Intentional source-model changes require prior independent contracts, explicit
versioning and fresh full qualification; frozen historical evidence is retained.

Plugin behavior, native builds, software-host acceptance, release provenance,
licence notices and truthful documentation remain required within their stated
supported scope. Project completion is not implied by a build, an isolated test,
or a declared estimate alone.
