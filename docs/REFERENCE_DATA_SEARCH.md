# Public calibrated-reference search — 2026-10-29

## Result and scope

A bounded public-web search for original CS-80 recordings, test-point captures,
waveform measurements and response datasets found **no packet admissible under
[REFERENCE_CAPTURE_REQUEST.md](REFERENCE_CAPTURE_REQUEST.md)**. No quantitative
stage-comparison fixture was imported; M1 remains open. This is a search result,
not proof that such data does not exist.

The pass covered CS-80 measured waveform/calibration/dataset queries, IG00156
response measurements, restoration records, original emulator-developer
publications, Yamaha's sample-library documentation and repository-oriented
queries. Existing Yamaha manuals/adjustments and the Golt IC guide were not
reclassified as new measured data. No accounts, purchases, agreements, contacts,
plugin installations or sample-pack downloads were made. Links below were
opened/read on 2026-10-29; source assets are not copied into this repository.

## Candidate disposition

### Morphoice: published hardware waveform comparison

[Developer's product page](https://www.morphoice.com/unstable) links a
[comparison image](https://www.morphoice.com/images/unstable-waveforms.png)
labelled as original CS-80 versus UNSTABLE across octaves. The public page does
not supply raw hardware recordings, unit/board identity, node/probe loading,
calibration/scaling, exact acquisition settings or uncertainty. It explicitly
identifies the audio demos as software output, and describes an intentionally
altered low-pass filter. Its footer reserves copyright; no dataset reuse grant
was located on the inspected page. **Useful lead, not a numerical reference or
an independent analog oracle.** Do not digitize its curves into calibrated
fixtures or substitute the plugin's output for hardware.

### Michael Fuchs / MFox: firsthand M-board calibration account

[CS-80 Calibration Part 5: M-Boards](https://mfoxhd.blogspot.com/2016/01/calibration-3-m-boards.html)
records disagreements with printed filter alignment and owner experiments. It
also reports interaction between adjustments and explicitly cautions that its
VCA meter examples are not trustworthy tolerance references (non-TrueRMS meter).
No raw waveform/response dataset, instrument calibration traceability,
uncertainty budget or complete unit/session packet was supplied on the inspected
page. The footer identifies Michael Fuchs's copyright, without a dataset grant.
**Useful discrepancy lead for source/technician review only.** Do not replace
Yamaha's documented anchors with these alternate settings or follow this blog
as an authorized service procedure. The reported discrepancies do not by
themselves establish which revision, node mapping or measurement setup explains
the difference.

### Yamaha / Peter Jung: sample library, not calibration corpus

[YamahaSynth's library article](https://yamahasynth.com/learn/moxf-series-music-production-synthesizers/loading-motif-xf-cs80-library-to-moxf/)
confirms a library of 61 waveforms and 1,179 keybanks arranged as 128 voices.
It describes instrument sample storage/loading, not a raw measurement release.
The inspected official article supplies neither per-take original acquisition
settings nor volts-to-digital calibration, internal-stage observations,
processing history or research/redistribution permission. **Not admissible as
calibrated data.** A sample count, manufacturer provenance or a nominal playback
sample rate cannot substitute for these missing records. No pack was downloaded
or unpacked; serial/date claims appearing in third-party mirrors were not used
as verified intake evidence.

### Scott Rider / Old Crow and 16 Music Lab: expertise, not public datasets

[Old Crow's firsthand calibration cautions](https://www.oldcrows.net/~oldcrow/synth/yamaha/cs80/cautionary.html)
document practical limitations and damage hazards in following historical
instructions. They are not recordings or a session calibration certificate.
[16 Music Lab's own expertise page](https://16musiclab.fr/csseries/) states that
it developed dedicated test/calibration benches and holds original technical
documents. The inspected page offers no downloadable calibrated measurement
packet. These are potential sources of expertise, **not evidence of available
or authorized data**. No contact was attempted.

## What remains necessary

None of these sources establishes even one new *admitted calibrated stage
quantity*. Some contain nominal targets, informal measured numbers or visual
comparisons; those categories must remain separate. The first useful next input
is an owner/rights-holder-provided raw capture with its complete provenance,
settings and scaling, or access to an identified unit through a qualified
technician. The existing intake request already specifies the missing records.
Rights, acquisition review, preregistration and a reviewed hardware-ingestion
schema remain required; this memo changes no harness gate or parity claim.

Reopen this search when a new named dataset, owner response or specific original
measurement publication becomes available, rather than repeatedly searching
these same demonstration and restoration pages.
