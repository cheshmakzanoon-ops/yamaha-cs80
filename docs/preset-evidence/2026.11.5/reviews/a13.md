# A13 — wind and string performance voicing

Baseline: `8685e9ed39d6347b67401da7590b7e1e8e95472d`, factory 2026.11.4.

## Scope and changes

All **14 existing recipes in Winds.inc and 28 existing recipes in Strings.inc**
were revised. Names, categories and insertion order match the frozen baseline.
No presets were added. The independently authored modern string voices are
outside this file ownership and are explicitly excluded from this cohort.

The main audible defect was compressed effort response. Large constant rank VCA
levels left little level difference between quiet and firm playing. Spiccato
Strings had fixed levels of 1.0 and 0.90 as well as full initial-touch depth;
Tutti and several darker sections had similarly dominant fixed levels.

The revised voicing gives each instrument a smaller fixed floor and a separately
authored initial-touch and pressure range. The values differ according to the
roles of the two ranks:

- Winds retain separate body, air and tongue levels. Softer playing does not
  leave a nearly fixed loud reed or hiss underneath it. Body and air releases
  are separately shortened for tongued instruments; Breathy Flute and Shakuhachi
  retain a more cushioned onset and longer breath than Recorder or Piccolo.
- Solo strings retain their existing stable dry body and independently fading
  bow-friction rank. Their lower fixed floor allows a much stronger played
  accent, while pressure remains available for the held bow.
- Ensemble, octave, fifth and synthetic strings retain their authored geometry,
  registration, chorus, PWM and pans. Their two sections receive individual
  effort ranges instead of inheriting the same loud fixed bow.
- Pizzicato and Spiccato keep zero held sustain and an independent short upper
  attack. Their initial touch now controls amplitude more clearly.
- Swell Strings remains pressure-driven: initial-touch gain is still zero.
  Its quieter unpressed bed makes the held pressure gesture more useful.
- String Machine intentionally retains constant-level playing. Its coordinated
  onset and release are quicker, allowing successive chord shapes to separate
  without changing its divide-down-style velocity behavior.

No Engine, body-model, parameter-schema, master-trim, CMake or unrelated family
files were changed by this assignment. A07/A08's shared body improvements were
already integrated in the candidate engine used for these measurements.

## Measurements

These values are actual rendered stereo RMS ratios at 44.1 kHz. Notes remain
held for 2.4 seconds, followed by 0.4 seconds of key release. The effort range
is the velocity-0.90 level minus the velocity-0.20 level over 1.8–2.3 seconds;
Pizzicato and Spiccato instead use their sounding 15–180 ms onset. Thus a
vanished detached note cannot pass as a quiet sustained body.

The table is MIDI 60. It describes gesture response, not a sound-quality score.
The pressure-only Swell Strings and constant-level String Machine are deliberate
exceptions to the ordinary effort-range gate.

## Limits and follow-up

The recipe changes remove demonstrated effort compression and improve the
specified articulation. They do not establish a measured match to a physical
wind instrument or string section. Bow/bore spectra remain authored models;
no recorded reference instrument or blinded listening panel was used here.

### Spiccato level correction

The first dynamic revision exposed a concrete gain problem: its fixed master
trim was already 1.0, yet the actual four-note library phrase reached only
−34.16 dBFS in the loudest 50 ms. The required bank window starts at −30 dBFS.
Raising the fixed floor alone restored loudness at the expense of the intended
effort range, so that approach was rejected.

The final recipe retains a fully modeled main bow, reduces the main HP cutoff
to preserve its low body, and gives the growing bow partials a 0.65-second
amplitude T60 instead of 0.30 seconds. Sustain remains zero and key release
remains 65 ms. A 95/5 rank balance keeps the main bowed stroke dominant over
its brief upper edge. The main fixed floor is a measured compromise of 0.20,
with full initial-touch depth.

| Spiccato measurement | Baseline | First effort revision | Final recipe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| C4 firm onset RMS, 15–180 ms | −39.86 dBFS | −45.78 dBFS | −38.42 dBFS |
| C4 soft-to-firm onset range | 2.91 dB | 9.46 dB | 7.59 dB |
| Four-note phrase loudest 50 ms | −28.22 dBFS | −34.16 dBFS | −28.78 dBFS |
| Four-note phrase peak | −14.18 dBFS | −20.12 dBFS | −17.84 dBFS |
| Eight-note stress peak | −8.48 dBFS | −14.12 dBFS | −11.47 dBFS |

The final effort ranges are **7.50, 7.59 and 8.71 dB** at MIDI 48, 60 and 72.
The calibration measurement is the unchanged `LibraryAuditTests::measure`
function with its original four-note phrase, pressure ramp and stress chord;
no master-trim override or relaxed acceptance gate was used. The broader
bank's final gain qualification remains the parent's responsibility.

The initial category-wide diagnostic additionally identified compressed effort
response in Phaser Strings, PWM LFO Strings and Ensemble Plus from Modern.inc.
Those findings were sent to the parent for their separate modern-family task;
this report makes no claim to have modified them.
