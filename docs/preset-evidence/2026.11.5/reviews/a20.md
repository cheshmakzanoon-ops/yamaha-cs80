# A20 — Lead recipe refinement

## Scope and authored behavior

All **30 existing presets in `src/core/curated/Leads.inc`** received individual sound and performance changes. No names, categories, tags, ordering, oscillator-footage identities or preset addresses were added or removed. The shared `lead()` initializer remains byte-for-byte identical to the baseline because Modern presets also use it. Each named Lead now owns its final VCA balance explicitly.

The earlier recipes generally combined fixed rank levels of 0.36/0.30 with substantial initial-touch levels. That fixed contribution compressed the difference between quiet and firm notes. The revised voices use smaller individually chosen floors, stronger initial-touch contribution where appropriate, and separate body/accent envelopes. Bright, agile voices have shorter upper accents and cleaner releases; Noir, Neo-Noir and Soft Lead retain their slower entrances and more connected endings. Fifth Lead and Octave Lead keep a clearly sustained upper interval rather than letting the named layer disappear into a weak accent. Deliberate Detuned Lead beating, Hollow Lead pulse motion, the 280 Hz Ring Lead carrier and the modulation routes remain intentional rather than generic random drift.

### Role-specific exceptions

- **Aftertouch Vibrato Lead** retains a quiet, stable playable center. Strike velocity has a smaller role, while pressure supplies substantially more level and its named vibrato. The unchanged pitch-depth route produces about a quarter-tone peak-to-peak in the actual output.
- **Pressure Lead** retains zero fixed and initial level. It remains silent without pressure and is independent of strike velocity at equal pressure. Faster body/edge entrances, a slightly more open passband and separately shortened releases make its pressure gestures more immediate.
- **Sine Lead, Whistle Lead and Ether Lead** retain their single bypass-sine topology. Their audible changes use the VCA onset, release and touch balance. Sine Lead receives a rounder onset, Whistle Lead a quicker breath-like entrance and shorter release, and Ether Lead a softer entrance with stronger pressure range. No inactive cutoff change is counted as an audible refinement.
- **Brass Lead** blends a 35% modeled harmonic lip spectrum into its circuit body, with a separate brighter saw edge and short breath-like releases. A larger initial body blend was rejected after its measured phrase level proved too quiet at maximum volume. The final blend preserves the lead/circuit edge and satisfies the existing loudness gate with margin.
- **Reed Lead** combines a clarinet-like harmonic body with a quieter modeled double-reed/pulse edge. Wider body passbands preserve the authored harmonic spectrum, while shorter tongue and breath envelopes keep dry phrases distinct.
- **Neo-Noir Brass Lead** receives restrained modeled lip support beneath its slower saw-and-pulse bloom. **Contour Lead** uses a deliberate upper brilliance increase with a high-key level trim.

## Actual Engine verification

`tests/LeadRefinementTests.cpp` renders the complete factory programs at 44.1 kHz. There is no post-render level normalization. It covers all 30 at MIDI 67, ten additional register cases at MIDI 48 and 79, pressure behavior, the three pure-sine entrances/releases, and audible controller/interval identity.

- Final suite: **234 checks, zero failures**, local elapsed runtime **35.36 seconds**.
- Same suite linked against the exact frozen 2026.11.4 source/build: **234 checks, 38 failures**. The failures expose the former compressed velocity response and limited Aftertouch Vibrato pressure range.
- Existing `OriginalArticulationTests.cpp`: **63 checks, zero failures**, including the original-bank Frozen/Soft pure-sine finishes and paced sequence accents.
- Source comparison: all 30 names, categories and ordering preserved; the shared `lead()` body unchanged.
- Scoped whitespace/diff validation: passed.

Register `cs80_lead_refinement_tests`, linked to `cs80_core`, with a **180-second timeout**. Parent integration owns CMake registration, final bank-wide calibration and native platform/host validation.

### Touch and pressure response

Soft/firm velocities are **0.20/0.90**, measured over the held **1.1–1.65 second** window. At MIDI 67 the 28 velocity-led voices increase from **5.59–10.58 dB** to **10.03–12.44 dB** of contrast. This leaves quiet gestures room to recede while preserving a firm, sustained body. The two pressure-led voices are deliberately excluded from this range.

At velocity 0.55, Aftertouch Vibrato Lead's full-pressure versus unpressed held-level span improves from **3.54 to 9.73 dB**. The revised emitted pitch varies only **0.19 cents peak-to-peak** without pressure and **46.67 cents** with full pressure. Ether Lead's pressure span increases from **8.35 to 9.44 dB**. Pressure Lead produces less than 1e-6 RMS without pressure and remains within **0.1 dB** across different strike velocities at equal full pressure.

All 30 released phrases fall more than 16 dB below their preceding held body in the test's 350–600 ms post-release window. This broad gate protects termination; individual authored release lengths remain intentionally different. The original pure-sine finish regression separately checks the more cushioned inherited attacks and longer endings against their revised source recipes.

## Phrase level and stress evidence

All 30 were rendered with the unchanged `LibraryAuditTests::measure` implementation: G4 at velocity 0.9, pressure ramp after the attack, key-up at 1.2 seconds, plus an eight-note maximum-velocity/full-pressure stress chord. The final Brass Lead value was measured again after the initial full pass.

The table below records the **captured pre-calibration trims**, so it is not a substitute for the final combined bank audit. Twenty-nine recipes already meet the unchanged −30 to −12 dBFS loudest-50-ms window at those trims. Reed Lead reaches **−30.50 dBFS at its inherited 0.667 master volume**. Its separate actual maximum-volume render reaches **−26.986 dBFS RMS**, with **−21.593 dBFS phrase peak** and **−7.914 dBFS stress peak**, providing ample room for the parent's ordinary calibration. Brass Lead reaches **−27.879 dBFS at the allowed maximum master volume 1.0**, with **−19.616 dBFS phrase peak** and **−5.789 dBFS stress peak**. No volume beyond 1.0 or weakened audit threshold is required.

The largest pre-calibration eight-note stress peak among these recipes is Ether Lead at **−0.853 dBFS**: below full scale, with the parent's final calibration still responsible for its preferred −1.5 dBFS safety target. All initial phrase and stress renders are finite.

## Limits and handoff

These are individually authored synth lead and modeled-body recipes. They do not establish exact acoustic-instrument equivalence or a blinded listening preference. Their timbral roles, dynamic range, pressure behavior, interval/gesture identity and phrase levels are supported by software renders. Root must retain the final integrated capture/report, inspect native CI results, and publish only the builds produced from the verified source revision.
