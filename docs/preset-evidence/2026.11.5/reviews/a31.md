# A31 — Accented brass and bow level correction

## Scope and outcome

Among the integration owner's 17 full-bank low-level failures, six arose from three existing recipes: Sforzando Brass / Neon Brass 18, Brass Stab / Electric Brass 22, and Marcato Strings / Neon Strings 26. All six already used master volume 1.000; raising the master control was not available within its published range.

A31 changed only these three named recipe blocks in `src/core/curated/Brass.inc` and `src/core/curated/Strings.inc`, plus this report. Names, categories, ordering, tags, body models, body-mix controls, body geometry, effect settings and master trims remain unchanged. No global engine, threshold, calibration table, shared initializer or original-bank finish was edited. The original addresses inherit the corrections through their existing mappings.

All six now pass the exact production level measurement with more than 1 dB of margin above its −30 dBFS floor. The full existing BrassOrganRefinementTests passes 200 checks; the exact Marcato response function passes 21 checks across three registers. A further 33 selected complete-patch gesture checks pass.

## Cause and correction

The integration audit measures a four-note phrase at velocity 0.9 with a pressure ramp, then an eight-note maximum-velocity/maximum-pressure chord. The corrected voices were finite and had substantial headroom; their modeled main body was too weak in the completed phrase.

A causal first probe reduced main-rank filter-envelope and initial-touch brilliance. That barely affected the two brass levels and slightly weakened their color, so those filter changes were rejected. Rebalancing toward the modeled main ranks was more effective. The brass voices also needed a modest restoration of fixed and initial effort, while preserving the substantial touch improvement over the published .4 recipes.

| Recipe | Final source correction |
|---|---|
| Sforzando Brass | Rank-II share 0.34 → 0.08; main fixed VCA contribution 0.070 → 0.180; main initial level 0.950 → 1.000. Its original 0.35 main sustain, envelope times, upper accent and both brass body models remain intact. |
| Brass Stab | Rank-II share 0.31 → 0.10; main fixed contribution 0.045 → 0.140; main initial level 0.950 → 1.000; main HP 28 → 20 Hz. Both ranks retain zero sustain and their existing decay/release times. |
| Marcato Strings | Rank-II share 0.22 → 0.10 and main HP 70 → 20 Hz. All fixed/initial/aftertouch controls and envelope times remain unchanged. The lower HP preserves the modeled bowed foundation beneath the separate short upper accent. |

The body-mix controls within each rank are unchanged. Weighting the main ranks more strongly therefore retains their modeled character. A trial 0.45 Sforzando sustain was discarded in favor of the original 0.35, giving a clearer measured accent-to-body relaxation.

## Gesture and articulation evidence

The unmodified existing `BrassOrganRefinementTests.cpp`, linked against the private current factory object and current core, passes **200 checks, zero failures**. At C4, Sforzando's measured soft/firm contrast is 8.330 dB and Brass Stab's is 8.982 dB. The previously published .4 values retained in [A14](a14.md) were 4.358 and 6.722 dB respectively. The correction gives up some of the intermediate, under-level recipes' contrast while preserving a substantial improvement over the published bank.

The selected gesture probe uses the exact BrassOrgan renderer at velocities 0.25 and 0.85, with pressure zero and all factory effects enabled. It covers MIDI 48, 60 and 72. Contrast uses 0–1.9 s RMS. Sforzando relaxation compares the 0.8–1.2 s held body against the first 150 ms; Stab's late level compares 1.5–1.9 s against the first 200 ms.
