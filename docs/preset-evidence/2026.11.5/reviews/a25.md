# A25 — Preset guide and description accuracy review

## Scope and outcome

Reviewed the working 2026.11.5 preset guide and revision report against the actual recipe source, the current pre-calibration 523-program inventory, the audition selection code and the preset-browser recall implementation. This is a source and documentation accuracy review, not a new listening assessment or native build qualification.

Three concrete wording/count discrepancies were reported to the integration owner and corrected during this review. A related browse-tag correction was also applied and verified. No remaining guide behavior discrepancy was found in the reviewed scope. The reviewer changed only this report; all source and guide fixes belong to the integration owner.

## Verified user-facing contracts

### Material and vocal variants

The guide's material combinations match the source and inventory: Xylophone uses Marimba plus Xylophone bar; Steel Drum uses Vibraphone plus Steelpan shell; Choir Pad uses Organ plus Choir ah on both ranks; Dark Choir uses Organ plus Choir oo. Dark Choir retains the documented 16′ lower body and 8′ supporting rank. The guide correctly says the variants are authored synthesis, not recordings, and that incompatible model/variant combinations use Standard.

The twelve saved body controls, next-accepted-note behavior for Model/Partial decay/Resonant size/Variant, live Mix/Brightness behavior and retained 0–19 Model selector agree with `Params.h`, `InstrumentBodyVariants.h`, `EvidenceRegister.h` and the implementation reports. The guide correctly distinguishes sustained wind/bowed/brass/organ mode behavior from struck partial decay. It does not imply that an unchanged saved parameter set freezes the old DSP audio.

### Pressure, keyboard and organ exceptions

Both Pressure Lead and Electric Leads 136 have zero fixed and initial VCA level on both ranks, with nonzero aftertouch level. Their pressure-required instructions therefore match the programmed signal path. They are not mislabeled as velocity-playable sounds. The guide also correctly keeps ordinary organ registrations and quill keyboards comparatively velocity independent. Harpsichord recipes retain only small initial-level terms; the standard organ helpers remain steady registrations. The pressure-led and deliberately synthetic exceptions are acknowledged in the revision report.

The six tremolo rates in the guide match their recipe calls. These recipes use the original SUB path: the inactive extended `sub_speed` field is not the authored rate, so its retained default must not be interpreted as a mismatch.

Mandolin retains mild beating from two closely tuned string courses while its old 12 Hz amplitude-modulation depth is zero. Its advice to perform repeated picked notes rather than rely on automatic flutter remains appropriate.

### Environmental effects and recall

Wind's separate upper-air/low-bed entrance, Surf's later spray and one-shot decay, Thunder's crack and low roll, and Clockwork's retriggered ticks agree with their current recipe descriptions and controls. The revision report accurately distinguishes natural irregular motion from intentional siren, rotor and alarm periodicity. No source claim of exact acoustic-recording equivalence is made.

`PresetBrowser::loadEntry` invokes the selected factory recipe directly; double-click and Return call the same load path. Audition selection can skip an entry already marked loaded. Revert restores the captured opening sound. The guide's explicit LOAD/RELOAD instructions, fresh-note advice, old-session distinction and warning that Revert is not a factory update therefore remain accurate.

### Counts, links and configured auditions

An independent JSON count of the current inventory agrees with all 15 rows of the guide's family table: 523 entries in total, including the two Init entries. There are 26 modern-tagged programs in the curated 301-address collection.

A local Markdown-link scan found **99 existing local targets** and no missing paths. A separate heading-fragment scan checked **seven local Markdown anchors**, all resolving to headings. Historical .4 links that remain in the guide point to the specifically described earlier filtering/size work; they are not presented as new binary qualification.

Executing the audition script's `selections()` and `roster_summary()` in a private Python namespace independently returned:

- 40 unique groups;
- 171 requested WAVs;
- 81 distinct existing programs;
- 1,033.2 seconds of configured audio;
- exactly seven positive-pressure examples, all in `pressure-full`.

These are configured requests, not proof that a native CI archive completed. The guide and current revision report preserve that distinction and direct readers to the eventual source-bound download qualification.
