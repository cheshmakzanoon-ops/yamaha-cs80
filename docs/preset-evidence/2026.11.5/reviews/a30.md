# A30 — Independent wind identity review and integrated level correction

## Scope and outcome

Reviewed all fourteen existing `Winds.inc` recipes, then rendered Flute, Clarinet,
Oboe and Bassoon at matched sounding fundamentals. During the review, the parent
integration audit found six genuine upper-register level failures and delegated
correction of those six recipes to this assignment. The correction is now frozen;
no other source file, calibration table, parameter definition or engine code was
modified by A30.

**Final results:** the six actual factory recipes pass the unchanged production
level measurement; `WindStringRefinementTests` passes **502 checks with zero
failures**, and a focused G4 effort/pressure control passes **54 checks with zero
failures**. The complete final matched-pitch sample set is finite and unclipped.
No recorded-instrument or native-plugin equivalence is claimed.

## Real failure and causal correction

The first integrated production audit used its established Winds phrase:
played MIDI 67, velocity 0.9, pressure ramping from zero to one over 0.3–0.6 s,
key release at 1.2 s, a 1.9 s total render, and the separate eight-note full-effort
stress chord. The six failures below were reproduced with the exact `measure`
function included from `tests/LibraryAuditTests.cpp`. Master volume was already
1.0 in every case. They were not repaired by relaxing a test or exceeding the
published control range.

An independent filter-control sweep tested a possible cause identified in the
brass/string integration: reducing main HP to 20 Hz and removing redundant main
filter-envelope/initial-brilliance movement. For these winds that recovers only
**0.05–0.50 dB** of phrase-window energy, insufficient for their deficits. At
about 0.987 s, the actual original main HP values span 45.97–179.76 Hz. The
filter envelopes have already settled to zero. The existing wind spectral
contours were therefore retained.

The correction instead strengthens initial effort and the independently played
breath contribution. Main initial-touch depth is 1.0; main held sustain is 0.98
(0.99 for Soprano Reed). Main pressure depth is individually 0.65, 0.72, 0.85,
0.70, 0.72 and 0.92 in table order. Their smaller supporting-rank pressure depths
are 0.40, 0.45, 0.52, 0.48, 0.45 and 0.60. Low constant amplifier floors and
compact key damping remain unchanged.

| Existing program | Before correction window RMS, dBFS | Final window RMS, dBFS | Final phrase peak, dBFS | Final eight-note peak, dBFS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Clarinet | -30.685 | -28.428 | -23.215 | -4.766 |
| Bass Clarinet | -31.139 | -28.590 | -21.873 | -3.533 |
| Oboe | -32.150 | -28.665 | -19.424 | -2.576 |
| English Horn | -31.520 | -28.685 | -21.613 | -4.132 |
| Bassoon | -31.115 | -28.412 | -21.076 | -3.629 |

All six exceed the requested −29 dBFS margin and retain the production phrase
and stress headroom. The private parameter-control experiment and a freshly
compiled final `FactoryPresets.cpp` object produce the same six results. Root
still owns final whole-bank recalibration, re-audit and native qualification.

The focused G4 check measures the complete held preset at velocities 0.20 and
0.90 over 1.8–2.3 s. The six contrasts are **10.497, 10.326, 11.593, 11.078,
11.085 and 11.665 dB** in table order. A separate held-pressure gesture grows
the body by **6.371–8.120 dB** and returns it by **6.361–8.051 dB** after pressure
eases. These measurements confirm that the level correction retains useful
strike response and gives breath pressure an audible, reversible role.

## Matched-fundamental spectral probe

The probe renders the complete Flute, Clarinet, Oboe and Bassoon patches at
48 kHz, sounding MIDI 60 and 72. Bassoon is played at MIDI 72 and 84 because its
main and support ranks are 16′; the other three are played at the sounding
note. Every condition uses velocity 0.25 or 0.85 and pressure 0 or 0.8, held for
2.4 s. No source, rank, effect, filter or factory trim is removed.

Three independently linked versions were captured: frozen 2026.11.4; the
integrated .5 source before this six-program correction; and the final corrected
recipes on the same current core. That is **32 conditions per version, 96
captures total**. The final 32 conditions are all finite; their largest sample
peak is **−20.630 dBFS**.

Analysis uses stereo-averaged Welch power over 0.8–2.2 s, Hann windows of
16,384 samples with 8,192 overlap. Harmonic windows allow the authored pitch
movement; the first twelve harmonic-band powers are normalized. For pairwise
identity comparison the fundamental is then excluded and the remaining powers
renormalized, preventing a common strong fundamental or master gain from being
mistaken for an individual harmonic shape.

At every one of eight matched note/velocity/pressure settings, all six pairs
of instruments remain spectrally distinct. Across **48 pair comparisons**, the
smallest upper-harmonic Hellinger distance is **0.08661**, for Oboe/Bassoon at
sounding C5, firm velocity and zero pressure. The corresponding pre-correction
minimum is 0.08601. This is evidence against the four being simple gain copies,
not an acoustic similarity or preference score.

At sounding C4, firm velocity and no pressure:

| Preset | Upper odd/even energy, dB | First-twelve harmonic centroid, Hz | Residual 100 Hz–8 kHz energy outside harmonic windows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Flute | -11.185 | 273.416 | 0.15677% |
| Clarinet | 13.593 | 370.185 | 0.01660% |
| Oboe | -1.626 | 743.429 | 0.00733% |
| Bassoon | -6.367 | 419.793 | 0.01208% |

Upper odd/even excludes the fundamental: odd harmonics 3/5/7/9/11 are compared
with even harmonics 2/4/6/8/10/12. The residual-band number includes pitch sidebands
and other nonharmonic energy; it is **not** an isolated measurement of breath
noise. Clarinet’s authored register transition changes its odd/even result to
about −1.89 dB at C5, while retaining a distinct full harmonic distribution.
This is consistent with its source design; this probe does not validate the
exact register behavior of a measured physical clarinet.

## Reproduction, provenance and limits

Private proof material is under `/tmp/cs80-a30`: `probe.cpp`, `analyze.py`,
`recipe-levels.cpp`, `effort.cpp`, their outputs, three capture directories and
`evidence-files.json`. A private copy of the current core archive prevents
concurrent parent rebuilds from changing a running test. The final recipe object
is linked ahead of that archive. The baseline uses its own frozen headers and
core archive, never the new 202-parameter header with the old 200-parameter ABI.
The full wind/string regression was linked to the same final recipe object.

Compiler commands use `g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Isrc ... -pthread`; the frozen build
uses `-I../baseline-2026.11.4/src`. The production level helper includes the
actual LibraryAudit implementation with only its command-line `main` renamed.
All four final verification processes exited 0. Scoped `git diff --check`
passed. Final `Winds.inc` SHA-256:
`97aec97d480995a3db46b00dc9ca16e678397bd38996a86a93c05ac4aa678420`.

These are software response and identity measurements, with no human listening
approval, recorded-instrument fit, or final native plugin claim. The spectral
probe complements the playing-response suite; it does not turn either one into
a claim that these synthesized winds are indistinguishable from acoustic winds.
