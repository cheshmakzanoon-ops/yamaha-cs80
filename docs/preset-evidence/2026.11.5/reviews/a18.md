# A18 — Existing FX recipe refinement

## Scope

All **24 existing curated FX programs** are edited in `src/core/curated/Fx.inc`. Names, categories and insertion order are unchanged; no program is added. All parameter vectors remain within the existing controls. The only newly used body variant is A16's open ah voice in Robot Voice. No Engine, body implementation, parameter declaration, master trim, build configuration or original-bank source was edited by this assignment.

Wind and Thunder now carry truthful `modern`/`lfo` tags because they use the existing smooth random LFO. Surf's obsolete `sub-mod` tag becomes `brilliance-swell`. Clockwork keeps `modern` for its existing arpeggiator but drops `lfo`, because the old audible square LFO is disabled. All named intentional periodic effects retain their audible SUB/ring roles.

The modern LFO enumeration was checked in `ModulationLfo.h`: **smoothRandom is selector 6**, with seven total choices. Original SUB noise is not a low-rate random controller, and the original SUB rate cannot fall below .70 Hz. Wind/Surf/Thunder therefore do not attempt to disguise that oscillator as weather. These recipe improvements do not claim exact correspondence to a recording, natural event, singer or original hardware unit.

## Actual Engine verification

`tests/FxRefinementTests.cpp` renders factory settings at 44.1 kHz, MIDI 60, velocity .90. It measures full output without post-render normalization. Causal controls retain the same deterministic noise carrier and remove only the modulation or voiced body under test. The Rain control replaces raised-cosine interpolation with sample-and-hold, keeping the rest of the voice fixed.

The suite checks Wind's audible uneven motion, Surf's rise/drain and separate foam/low-wash decay, Thunder's crack-to-rumble band change and irregular roll, Rain's modulation continuity, Clockwork's within-tick decay and silent gaps, Breath FX pressure range, and Robot Voice's integrated voiced-material contribution. It does not substitute parameter equality assertions for rendered evidence.

Suggested target: `cs80_fx_refinement_tests`, source `tests/FxRefinementTests.cpp`, linked to `cs80_core`, 180-second timeout. Final suite: **30 checks, zero failures**, measured runtime **12.06 seconds**. All 24 source name/category/order identities are unchanged, all 24 recipes differ, scoped `git diff --check` passes, and the existing `usesModernLayer` predicate matches the `modern` tag on all 24 recipes. The same behavioral suite against frozen 2026.11.4 gives **30 checks, 8 failures**: both Wind motion contracts, separate Surf foam fall, irregular Thunder roll, Rain continuity, Clockwork's within-tick fall, Breath pressure range, and voiced Robot material.

## Limits and integration

These are deterministic software render checks and authored synthesis choices, not listening-panel validation or a claim of hyperrealistic equality. Wind, Surf, Thunder and Rain remain generated, keyboard-filtered noise models. Robot Voice is a synthesized vowel under ring modulation. Shorter articulated effects and stable machinery differ intentionally from pads and deliberately periodic sirens/alarms. The test locks their measurable behavior while leaving native host/plugin validation to parent integration.
