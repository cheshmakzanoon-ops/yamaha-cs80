# A32 — Corrected Funk Bass and Metallic Keys level balance

## Scope and outcome

The final integrated bank audit exposed three related failures at the unchanged
−30 dBFS loudest-window floor: Funk Bass, its inherited Electric Bass 82
variation, and Metallic Keys. Their master trims were already 1.000, so output
calibration could not correct them. This assignment edits only the Funk Bass
recipe in `src/core/curated/Bass.inc` and the Metallic Keys recipe in
`src/core/curated/Bells.inc`. Names, descriptions, categories, tags, order,
models and program addresses remain unchanged.

All three now clear the requested −29 dBFS development margin in the exact
production measurement. No engine, global helper, trim, audit threshold,
arpeggiator, other recipe, CMake or historical evidence file was changed.

## Recipe corrections

Funk Bass restores the low foundation without changing its zero-sustain
amplitude contours or turning its fingered-string snap into a held pad:

| Control | Before A32 | Final |
|---|---:|---:|
| Main sine contribution | 0.18 | 0.30 |
| Main high-pass cutoff | 22 Hz | 20 Hz |
| Main fixed VCA contribution | 0.065 | 0.090 |
| Rank mix, upper-rank share | 0.23 | 0.10 |
| Main string partial decay | 0.38 | 0.52 |

The main modeled-body blend remains 0.63, its brightness remains 0.62, and its
initial-touch amount remains 1.0. The upper clav-like snap keeps its independent
200 ms amplitude decay, 35 ms release and existing filter contour. Both ranks
still have zero amplitude sustain. The slightly longer independent string
losses support the low note after the pick; the outer envelope still dries it
out. Electric Bass 82 inherits the correction through the established original
bank recipe path, retaining its own finish.

Metallic Keys brings its main pitched strike forward while retaining the
explicitly synthetic metal/ring character:

| Control | Before A32 | Final |
|---|---:|---:|
| Main reed-body blend | 0.77 | 0.60 |
| Rank mix, upper-rank share | 0.23 | 0.15 |
| Ring amount | 0.29 | 0.24 |

The reed and bell model identities, brightness, independent partial losses,
filter contours, touch controls, zero sustain, releases and 277 Hz ring carrier
remain unchanged. The larger residual saw contribution and stronger main-rank
weight improve the pitched center. The ring amount remains substantial, with
the same carrier and envelope behavior.

## Exact production level measurement

The private probe directly includes `tests/LibraryAuditTests.cpp` with its
entry-point renamed and calls its unchanged `measure` function. Thus it uses
the actual 44.1 kHz category phrase, velocity 0.9, pressure ramp, 1.2-second
key-up, loudest 50 ms RMS, and eight-note full-velocity/full-pressure stress
chord. No substitute level fixture or gain normalization is used.

| Existing program | Before window RMS | Final window RMS | Final phrase peak | Final eight-note peak |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Funk Bass | −30.855232 dBFS | **−28.588118 dBFS** | −18.628094 dBFS | −9.373806 dBFS |
| Electric Bass 82 | −31.084688 dBFS | **−28.721357 dBFS** | −18.794510 dBFS | −9.462251 dBFS |
| Metallic Keys | −32.267573 dBFS | **−28.803022 dBFS** | −15.158397 dBFS | −11.254872 dBFS |

All three master trims are **1.000 before and after**. All measurements are
finite; neither phrase nor stress approaches full scale. The initial probe's
out-of-range 14 Hz high-pass candidate was sanitized by the Engine to its 20 Hz
minimum; the shipped recipe explicitly uses the valid 20 Hz value. A separate
Metallic Keys filter/brightness candidate added only 0.045 dB and was not used.

The raw probe prints `baseline` for its first three measurements in every
invocation. In `probe.log` those rows use the copied pre-correction core/factory;
in `final-audit.log` they use the final private factory object. That label is
an invocation phase, not a claim that the two files contain the same source.

The exact existing suites were compiled against the final private factory
object and the same copied current core archive:

- **BassPluckedRefinementTests: 149 checks, zero failures.** Funk Bass keeps
  **8.708 dB** of soft/firm contrast in that suite's 48 kHz MIDI-48 gesture,
  exceeding the unchanged 7.2 dB requirement.
- **MalletPresetRefinementTests: 136 checks, zero failures.** Metallic Keys
  keeps **7.398 dB** at MIDI 60, exceeding the unchanged 6.5 dB requirement.
- Scoped `git diff --check` passes. The before/after source diff contains only

The balance correction gives up some of the immediate pre-correction contrast
(Funk Bass was 9.144 dB; Metallic Keys was 8.49 dB) while retaining substantial

not presented as improved contrast over the immediately preceding working
recipe. Metallic Keys still substantially exceeds the earlier frozen .4
contrast of 3.44 dB recorded in A12's matching gesture.

## Held decay and release-floor diagnosis

A bounded additional probe used complete factory patches at 48 kHz, velocities
0.25/0.85, a 2.5-second hold and 0.6-second release. Onset RMS is measured over
10–250 ms; held decay over 2.0–2.3 seconds; release over 2.9–3.1 seconds.

| Program and MIDI note | Touch contrast | Held level relative to onset | Released level relative to onset |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Funk Bass, 36 | 9.522 dB | −49.441 dB | −140.389 dB |
| Funk Bass, 48 | 8.716 dB | −50.703 dB | −146.521 dB |
| Funk Bass, 60 | 8.834 dB | −51.888 dB | −150.981 dB |
| Metallic Keys, 60 | 7.398 dB | −34.161 dB | −37.358 dB |
| Metallic Keys, 72 | 6.867 dB | −35.637 dB | −38.159 dB |
| Metallic Keys, 84 | 6.711 dB | −37.480 dB | −39.236 dB |

The initial diagnostic recorded **30 checks, three failures**: its assumed
−55 dB relative release threshold failed in the three Metallic Keys cases.
Those failures are retained, not relabeled as passes. A subsequent causal
control identified the residual as the existing modeled ring circuit's idle
carrier feedthrough, rather than a continuing note:

- Independently rendering the complete preset without pressing any key gives
  **−77.193044 dBFS** over the same late window.
- All three released-note windows equal that idle level within
  **0.000002 dB**. The played note has therefore fallen to the observed driver
  floor.
- Disabling only ring amount yields released levels of **−166.263, −169.202
  and −173.659 dBFS**. Its independently rendered idle signal is zero within
  the measurement's −300 dB floor.

`RingModulatorCircuit.h` explicitly includes a signal-offset term that leaks
the carrier. The production DSP and its intended ring behavior were not
changed to satisfy a noise-free assumption in a private diagnostic. The
measured held decay confirms that neither corrected mixed-body recipe becomes
a static sustain bed.
