## Wording corrections

### 1. Identify the duration of the bank stress measurement

The revision report currently says that the maximum eight-note stress peak is
**−1.5 dBFS**. That number is correctly transcribed from the final bank audit,
but the sentence omits its time window. The audit uses the fixed eight-note
chord at full velocity and pressure, at **44.1 kHz**, over a nominal **one-second**
window. Its 256-frame processing blocks render 44,288 frames, approximately
1.0043 seconds. This is not a maximum over every possible held chord.

Suggested replacement:

> Its loudest 50 ms RMS windows span −29.9 to −18.0 dBFS, maximum phrase peak is
> −6.2 dBFS, and the nominal one-second, eight-note stress audit peaks at
> −1.5 dBFS across the bank (rounded).

The existing later paragraph correctly identifies the separate **12-second**
upper-register chord checks for Cathedral Pad and Golden Pads 66, at **48 kHz**.
Their **−1.789 / −1.688 dBFS** peaks and **0.650** output trims must remain scoped
to those two measured programs. They are not a new all-bank 12-second maximum.

### 2. Identify the duration of Rain's reported maximum

The report's **1.1204 → 0.0407 dB** comparison is the real A18 result, measured
with the 2.5-second FX fixture at **44.1 kHz**, using adjacent nominal 2 ms
windows from **0.2 to 2.5 seconds**. A44's later, longer measurement at **48 kHz**
finds a maximum of **0.060951 dB** over **0.2–36 seconds**. These are different
windows and do not contradict one another. The shorter maximum should not be
presented as a universal bound.

Suggested replacement:

> In the 2.5-second FX regression, Rain's largest adjacent 2 ms modulation-level
> change falls from 1.1204 to 0.0407 dB. A separate longer review measures a
> 0.060951 dB maximum over 0.2–36 seconds; both results describe their specific
> rendered gestures.

The weather descriptions otherwise match the source and long-duration review:
Wind/Rain use changing random contours, Surf/Thunder use separate one-shot
contours, and Clockwork/Helicopter/Siren intentionally retain periodic behavior.
They are not described as recorded acoustic scenes.

## User instructions and saved-session behavior

The README's **Library → FACTORY LIBRARY → select the entry → LOAD (or RELOAD,
if shown)** route agrees with A45's source review. It correctly explains that an
old project can show the same name while restoring old controls, that clicking
an already-loaded row with Audition enabled can preserve edits, and that REVERT
or A/B can restore an older snapshot. Releasing held notes/pedal and playing
fresh notes is included in the README and preset guide, matching note-on capture.
The application version is kept distinct from the factory-library revision and
compiled source identity.

The preset guide also correctly says that retaining the values of an older
body-enabled saved patch does not freeze its old rendering: the revised body
code can still change it. Missing variant fields default to Standard. This
avoids confusing parameter compatibility with an audio-identical old-session
promise.
