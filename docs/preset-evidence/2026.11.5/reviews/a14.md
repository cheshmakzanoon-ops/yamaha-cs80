# A14 — Brass effort and organ keying

## Scope

Edited every existing recipe in `Brass.inc` (32) and `Organ.inc` (14). Names,
categories, order, footage, and program addresses remain unchanged. Original
library programs inherit these recipes through their existing named mappings.
No engine, model, parameter layout, global level calibration, or other family
recipe was changed by this assignment.

The brass recipes had large fixed amplifier levels beneath initial touch. That
made a horn played at 25% velocity much closer in level to an 85% note than its
instrument role suggests. The refinement assigns each voice a small fixed
foundation and a larger played-effort contribution. Solo, accented, section,
pad, and pressure-led voices retain different balances. Fourteen natural or
accented voices also have shorter breath endings, while named long swells keep
their long phrases. Pressure Swell Brass retains a quiet initial bed and uses
pressure as its main expressive axis.

Organ stops keep zero initial-touch routing. Their straight registrations no
longer inherit oscillator dispersion/drift. Named chorus, vibrato and tremulant
remain explicit effects. Individual lower/upper pipe speaking times and dry
key damping now distinguish pipe, flute, reed, percussion and electronic
registrations. Percussive Organ keeps the 4-foot octave percussion, with a
shorter decay and fast key damping. No new organ timbre was substituted.

## Rendered verification

`tests/BrassOrganRefinementTests.cpp` renders complete actual Engine patches at
48 kHz. All 32 brass and 14 organ recipes are exercised at MIDI 60; five named
horn/mute/tuba/pipe/flute voices are additionally checked at MIDI 48 and 72.
The suite checks headroom and non-silence, effort range, pressure range, held
stab/sforzando articulation, organ velocity invariance, sustained stop level,
and key damping. Brass effort compares velocities 0.25 and 0.85 over 0–1.9 s;
organ invariance compares 0.20 and 0.90. Notes release at 2 s. Organ key-damping
figures compare the 75–105 ms post-release window with the final held level.

- Current integrated Engine/recipe render: **200 checks, 0 failures**.
- Identical suite linked against frozen 2026.11.4: **200 checks, 37 failures**.
- Existing preset voicing regressions: **284/284 passed**, 81.3 s of audio.
- Existing preset precision regressions: **285 checks, 0 failures**, 230.4 s of audio.
- All fourteen organ registrations show **0.000 dB** soft/firm difference at
  printed precision. Their held stops remain audible; percussion remains separate.
- Pressure Swell Brass pressure range at velocity 0.25 grows from **17.787 dB**
  to **21.867 dB** for pressure 0 to 0.8.

Candidate builds use the parent's current 202-parameter Engine objects plus
this assignment's freshly compiled FactoryPresets translation unit. Baseline
builds use the complete frozen 2026.11.4 core. These before/after results include
the concurrent captured body-articulation improvement; they are not an isolated
A14-only engine ablation. Final loudness calibration and native plugin tests
remain the parent integration's responsibility.

## Integration and limits

Register `cs80_brass_organ_refinement_tests`, link `cs80_core`, and use a
120-second timeout. The percussive upper attack is expressed at the public
1 ms parameter minimum; Engine's onset minimum already imposed that value.

These results establish stronger played dynamics and more distinct dry keying.
They do not establish acoustic or commercial-instrument equivalence, perceptual
perfection, real-player evaluation, polyphonic stress qualification, or all-rate
native-host behavior. No source claims were widened to imply those outcomes.
