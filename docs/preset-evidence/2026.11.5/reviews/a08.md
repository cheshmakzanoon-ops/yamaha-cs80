# A08 — captured body articulation

Baseline: `8685e9ed39d6347b67401da7590b7e1e8e95472d` / factory revision 2026.11.4.

## Change

`BodyArticulation.h` supplies a small, fixed note-captured profile for the
optional instrument body. Its inputs are the accepted body model, sounding
register after footage/tuning, strike velocity and onset body mix. The profile
changes the rank's **amplitude** ADSR; the filter contour keeps its authored
timing.

- Piano, tine/reed EP, plucked strings and bars retain a somewhat longer bass
  decay and more compact upper-register decay/damping. The curves are
  continuous and bounded: full-body decay scales remain 0.65–1.55 and release
  scales 0.72–1.40. C4 is neutral for those register adjustments.
- Bowed, woodwind and brass bodies have modest strike-sensitive attack timing.
  A firm gesture speaks sooner; this does not add a pitch randomizer or LFO.
- Organ, Off, policy-masked bodies and bodies whose onset mix is zero retain
  the neutral profile. Partial onset mixes interpolate toward neutral.
- The profile survives live model selection and envelope edits. It participates
  in note onset, live timing refresh, direct/pedal release, and cancellation of
  master-sustain tails.
- Explicit KEY RELEASE and MASTER SUSTAIN remain unscaled release minima.
  The profile scales only the recipe's release before taking that maximum.

The ordinary ADSR implementation, parameter definitions, program identities,
filter arithmetic and circuit-only signal paths were not changed by A08.
These are authored performance curves, not fitted physical-instrument data.

## Verification

New test source: `tests/BodyArticulationEngineTests.cpp`. Standalone GCC 14.2
C++20 `-O2 -Wall -Wextra` build against `Engine.cpp` and `Ig00156Batch.cpp`:

- **284 checks passed; zero failures** in the candidate.
- Rebuilding against the integrated 202-parameter headers preserves that result;
  the existing `InstrumentBodyEngineTests.cpp` also passes **18,156 checks with
  zero failures** against the same freshly rebuilt engine.
- The **same final tests** against the frozen 2026.11.4 engine produce **24
  failures**, specifically the newly required register/attack response, retained
  bass scaling after a live timing edit, and restored register release after
  master-sustain cancellation.
- No existing test thresholds were changed. The new suite renders actual
  engine audio and examines realized envelope state, rather than checking
  profile constants.

At a 120 ms authored attack, velocity 0.95 versus 0.2 raises the realized
envelope at 60 ms by factors 1.219–1.311 across the six expressive body models.
Level-normalized early/held audio also rises; for bowed strings, the ratio is
0.29258 at the soft strike and 0.39964 at the firm strike.

The suite also checks 44.1/48/96 kHz, equivalent sounding pitches from different
footage, exact zero-mix circuit audio, exact organ touch invariance, model
selection lifetime, continuous live envelope edits, explicit release overrides,
CC64 hold/pedal-up, master-sustain cancellation, scalar/batched agreement and
host-block partition invariance.

An initial new footage-tail waveform assertion found a **pre-existing** small
pitch discrepancy after release: physical C3 at 8′ and C4 at 16′ produce
130.810867 and 130.811829 Hz in the retained key-node model. Both old and new
engines showed this; it is not an articulation change. The final test therefore
requires exact equality of the realized release envelopes and less than 0.1%
tail-RMS difference, while retaining the held-audio waveform comparison. It
does not claim exact post-release pitch equality.

## Related piano diagnosis passed to the preset work

A frozen-baseline Stage Keys probe confirmed that the 7.6-second amplitude
decay is actually respected. Its envelope is 0.914 at 0.1 s, 0.401 at 1 s,
0.161 at 2 s and 0.0644 at 3 s. The corresponding 100 ms output RMS windows
fall from −32.9 to −48.3, −62.4 and −72.8 dBFS. The effective HP remains near
24 Hz and LP near 10.8 kHz: this loss is not a filter closing on the body.
The outer RC decay and the model's own modal decay multiply. Improving the
long piano aftersound also requires deliberate recipe work; the C4-neutral
register profile alone cannot solve it.

Probe and raw local logs during development: `/tmp/cs80-a08/`.

## Limits

The profile is captured with the note, including its initial mix. Moving the
live mix later does not rebuild the envelope. A partially blended body shares
its rank's amplitude ADSR with the residual circuit voice; the mix weighting
limits that effect but does not create a second independent VCA.

This change does not implement partial-specific damper contact, sympathetic
resonance, undamped piano treble strings or measured instrumental equivalence.
Those claims cannot be inferred from these response checks.
