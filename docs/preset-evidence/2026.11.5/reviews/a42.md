# A42 — Independent lead phrase and controller review

## Result and scope

No production correction is recommended from this bounded review. It adds
whole-preset phrase and controller checks beyond the short single-note renders
in [A20](a20.md), while retaining the original musical identities documented by
[A22](a22.md) and the matched-output evidence in [A33](a33.md).

The review identifies source
**6094bce18c8b19da804c2fbc9a00e31969a7f579**. Its core archive was copied to a
private directory before the parent's subsequent pad-level work, and its
SHA-256 matches the parent's recorded final core at that source:
**0daeb24d43130bd852d31655289322a1cb39e46f4f8e4d99163457d8fe3a58d8**.
This does not qualify a native build or a later source commit.

The first diagnostic recorded **1,374 checks, with 392 failed assertions**.
Those failures were two incorrect diagnostic assumptions about pressure, not
note-ownership or audio-safety failures. They remain in the original source and
log. A separate causal follow-up passed **118 checks, zero failures**; the
findings and limitations below explain precisely what was established.

## Existing lead identities

An independent binary32 comparison of the frozen preceding inventory with the
inventory read directly from commit 6094 confirms that all **56 existing
lead-category programs** retain their ordered identity and both oscillator
footage selections. The shared lead initializer is byte-identical to the
preceding source; the revised recipes own their individual final sound
controls. No preset was added, renamed or moved.

The source and saved controls preserve the meaningful role distinctions:

| Existing voice | Retained role |
|---|---|
| Fifth Lead | An 8′ foundation and a 5⅓′ upper rank, at a harmonic fifth |
| Octave Lead | An 8′ foundation and a sustained 4′ upper rank |
| Whistle Lead | A single 4′ bypass sine; no lower octave is mixed in |
| Sine Lead / Ether Lead | One 8′ bypass sine, with distinct onset, release, glide and pressure roles |
| Glide Lead / Glissando Lead | Smooth 0.22-second portamento versus the authored 0.19-second stepped mode |
| Ribbon Glide Lead | Short portamento with a live relative-frequency ribbon gesture |
| Pressure Lead / Electric Leads 136 | Zero fixed and initial VCA level on both ranks; pressure supplies their sound |

These are source/control observations. A20 remains the emitted-audio evidence
for the fifth, octave, trill and stepped-glide measurements. This review does
not repeat those measurements and count them as new results.

## Repeated and overlapping phrases

Ten complete factory voices were rendered twice: **Velocity Lead, Brass Lead,
Reed Lead, Whistle Lead, Fifth Lead, Octave Lead, Glide Lead, Glissando Lead,
Noir Lead and Ribbon Glide Lead**.

Each repeated-note phrase contains fourteen notes at 320 ms spacing, alternating
velocities 0.35 and 0.90, with a 220 ms gate. Each overlapping phrase uses the
same fourteen-note schedule with a melodic line and a 390 ms gate, so adjacent
notes overlap by 70 ms. Channel-pressure events change during the phrase.
Every phrase continues through its normal release without resetting the Engine
or forcibly muting the voices.

All **634 held-note ownership checks** in the complete initial diagnostic
passed. No note-off released the wrong continuing note, and every render
finished with zero active voices and zero held keys. The twenty musical
phrases remained audible. All twenty-five initial renders, including the
separate pressure and ribbon cases, were finite and below full scale. Their
maximum peak was **−12.413653 dBFS**.

The phrases exceed eight note assignments and therefore exercise slot reuse;
they do not claim fourteen simultaneous voices. The lead recipes also retain
the existing polyphonic engine behavior. An instrument's lead name is not a
claim that a new monophonic legato-priority mode was implemented.

## Per-note pressure isolation and modeled fade

Pressure Lead and its existing inherited variation, Electric Leads 136, were
each tested with two held keys. MIDI 60 receives pressure first; an unpressed
MIDI 67 is added while MIDI 60 continues sounding. The second key is pressed
later, after the first key's pressure is released.

For each preset, a separately rendered one-key control uses the same event
boundaries and first-key gesture. During the **2.2–2.8 second** comparison
window, adding the unpressed second key produces **exactly zero PCM
difference**: both the relative RMS residual and maximum sample difference are
zero. The initial unpressed phase is also exactly silent. Each independently
pressed key subsequently produces an audible signal.

Two initial assertions incorrectly required the pressure-off output to be below
−120 dBFS only 0.4–0.9 seconds after the gesture. The actual readings were
approximately −78 and −69 dBFS, already substantially below the pressed tone.
The unchanged touch-circuit model gives the aftertouch path a **100 ms
low-pass**, so pressure release is a fade, not an instantaneous digital mute.

The follow-up keeps both keys held for longer and measures the settled result:

| Existing program | First pressure-off window, 1.0–1.4 s later | Fall from pressed level | Second pressure-off window, 1.5–2.0 s later | Fall from pressed level |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pressure Lead | −121.279570 dBFS | 94.034288 dB | −168.030685 dBFS | 142.039385 dB |
| Electric Leads 136 | −121.156647 dBFS | 94.035161 dB | −167.787347 dBFS | 141.924293 dB |

Both pressure-only voices therefore return to negligible output while the keys
remain held. Ordinary later note-offs also retire all voices.

## Non-MPE channel-pressure contract

The other **390 initial failures** came from assuming a newly accepted ordinary
non-MPE note inherits the previous channel-pressure event. The source instead
resets that new note's local pressure to zero. A subsequent channel-pressure
event updates the active addressed notes. MPE member-expression initialization
is a distinct existing path and is outside this diagnostic.

The follow-up directly exercised twelve accepted/reused notes in Velocity Lead
and twelve in Pressure Lead. All **24** started with local pressure zero after
a prior channel event, then reported exactly 0.6 after the next channel event.
Their audio remained finite and unclipped, and all voices retired normally.

Four relevant pressure-routing/reset blocks and the entire touch-circuit
header are byte-identical to the frozen baseline. This behavior was not
introduced by the preset refinement. The initial failing assertions are
retained, rather than rewritten or counted as successful executions.

## Ribbon return

A complete Ribbon Glide Lead holds MIDI 60 while the ribbon moves to +0.30,
returns to center, moves to −0.25, and returns again. Normalized autocorrelation
of emitted stereo audio, with sub-sample interpolation, measures:

| Gesture | Measured frequency |
|---|---:|
| Before movement | 261.573011 Hz |
| +0.30 displacement | 340.041023 Hz |
| First return to center | 261.530932 Hz |
| −0.25 displacement | 196.183150 Hz |
| Final return to center | 261.570108 Hz |

The positive and negative movements follow the existing 1.30 and 0.75 frequency
ratios. The two center returns differ from the preceding center by only
**−0.278524 cents** and **−0.019213 cents**, respectively. The note then releases
normally. These observations establish the existing modeled gesture; they do
not measure a physical CS-80 ribbon.

## Retained evidence and limits

The initial and follow-up runs together rendered **11,019,840 stereo frames at
48 kHz**, or **229.58 seconds**, across 29 complete performances/control
renders. No post-render normalization was applied. The initial diagnostic
exited 1 because of its retained incorrect pressure assertions; the follow-up
exited 0. Do not summarize the initial run as 1,374 passing checks.

The [structured review](../verification/a42-lead-review.json) retains all
per-case peaks, pressure comparisons, ribbon measurements and counts. The
[build record](../verification/a42-build-inputs.json) identifies the private
core, source inputs, exact commands and executables. The
[retained-file manifest](../verification/a42-retained-files.json) hashes the
authored evidence files.

These are local behavioral checks and source comparisons, not a listening
preference test, an acoustic-equivalence score, or native release
qualification. No production source, preset recipe, calibration table, shared
build, workflow, commit or remote ref was changed by A42.
