# A33 — Complete matched-bank PCM comparison

## Result and scope

All **523 existing program identities** were compared using complete stereo
factory-patch renders with matched note, rate, velocity, pressure, hold, release
excerpt and rank settings. Each version contains **103,153,374 stereo frames**,
or **2,149.028625 seconds** of audio. Every sample is finite and within full
scale. Both initialization programs have **byte-identical PCM** to the frozen
2026.11.4 baseline.

All **519 musical programs audible at zero pressure** have changed PCM.
**Electric Leads 136** and **Pressure Lead** remain exactly silent in that
gesture, as their designs require pressure. Separate matched pressure=1
captures of both programs are audible and changed. This accounts for all
**521 musical programs** without counting the initialization sounds as
revoiced programs or treating intended silence as evidence of an improvement.

[Complete per-program measurements](../verification/a33-matched-bank-audio.json) ·
[Comparison implementation](../verification/compare-matched-bank-audio.py) ·
[Pressure-control settings and hashes](../verification/a33-pressure-controls.json)

These measurements establish signal changes, preserved identities and specific
rendered behavior. They do not establish human listening preference, acoustic
equivalence or native plugin qualification. A single played gesture does not
cover every register, chord, controller position or long release.

## Inputs and checks

The baseline inventory is the exact retained 2026.11.4 inventory from source
`8685e9ed39d6347b67401da7590b7e1e8e95472d`, with SHA-256
`0545f9a6ede82c72f4bbecc0e032cbc08f4de257b496f6b763b1911d607f9611`.
The revised inventory is the final calibrated inventory after the 17 integration
level corrections. The revised capture uses `candidate-final-audio`, whose
completion manifest and stable executable hash are required by the checker;
the earlier pre-correction candidate is excluded.

The checker independently validates:

- All 523 ordered names, categories and host indexes, the published
  200-parameter prefix, and the two appended parameters.
- Exact note/rate/velocity/pressure/hold/tail/rank agreement between each pair.
- Every CSV settings digest against the inventory's actual binary32 parameter
  values: 200 values for the baseline, 202 for the revised version.
- Exact expected WAV filenames, absence of duplicate files, IEEE float32
  stereo format, 48 kHz rate, canonical RIFF chunks, `fact` frame counts and
  data lengths.
- Every actual sample for finiteness and full-scale bounds, and five CSV
  peak/energy measurements recomputed from the PCM within 0.000001 dB.
- Exact baseline metrics identity and equality between the revised completion
  manifest's executable hash and the capture executable being reviewed.

All input hashes and per-WAV/PCM hashes are recorded in the JSON result.
The pressure supplements used the separate frozen and revised executables;
their hashes were checked before and after capture. No 200-parameter headers
were linked to a 202-parameter core.

## What the signal differences show

Equal-RMS analysis divides each complete stereo clip by its own RMS, then
measures the residual without time or phase alignment. It does not modify or
normalize a delivered WAV. For spectral comparison, the checker averages
stereo power from sixteen equally spaced 4,096-frame Hann windows within the
held interval, excludes DC, and normalizes total spectral power. The reported
Hellinger distance is zero for equal distributions and at most one.

| Scope | Raw PCM changed | Equal-RMS residual above 0.001 | Spectral distance above 0.01 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 519 musical programs audible at zero pressure | 519 | 516 | 188 |
| Two pressure-only programs, pressure=1 | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Two initialization programs | 0 | 0 | 0 |

The two thresholds are descriptive counts, not listening thresholds or quality
gates. A changed envelope, tuning relationship, modulation trajectory or effect
can alter the waveform without substantially changing its average spectrum.
Preserving a useful existing spectrum is also compatible with improving touch
and articulation. The complete result retains small differences rather than
rounding them up to a claim of spectral redesign.

Across the 519 audible musical zero-pressure comparisons, the median complete-
clip RMS change is **−0.992696 dB**. The highest captured single-note peak is
**−13.400014 dBFS** in the revised set, versus **−13.537388 dBFS** before. These
single-note figures are separate from the production chord/stress level audit.

The matched pressure controls have equal-RMS residuals of **0.098927** and
**0.100468**, with spectral distances **0.015083** and **0.015565**. Neither is
counted from its silent zero-pressure file.

## Three voices whose change depends on playing effort

Noise Pad, Ether Pad and Sub Bass have equal-RMS residuals below 0.001 at the
bank capture's single 0.8 velocity. Their raw differences in that particular
gesture are almost uniform gain changes. Additional soft/firm captures expose
the intended change in their playing response.

These captures use velocities 0.25 and 0.85, zero pressure, a five-second hold,
and a one-second release excerpt. Noise Pad and Ether Pad use played MIDI 60;
Sub Bass uses played MIDI 48. Contrast is measured in the held 4.0–4.5-second
window, after their entrances have settled.

| Existing voice | Frozen complete-patch contrast | Revised complete-patch contrast | Old/new controls on one engine, common master 0.5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Noise Pad | 3.095326 dB | 6.915161 dB | 3.095326 → 6.915161 dB |
| Ether Pad | 2.649972 dB | 6.821344 dB | 2.649972 → 6.821344 dB |
| Sub Bass | 7.454694 dB | 9.381205 dB | 7.454694 → 9.381205 dB |

The separate common-master check uses the old and new complete parameter
vectors on the same current Engine, with both master controls forced to 0.5.
It reproduces the complete-patch contrasts within **0.00000002 dB**. Different
published output trims therefore do not explain these touch-response changes.
All common-master renders are finite and unclipped.

## Reproduction and retained controls

The comparison command is:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 \
  docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.5/verification/compare-matched-bank-audio.py \
  --before ../baseline-audio --after ../candidate-final-audio \
  --before-inventory docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.5/baseline/inventory.json \
  --after-inventory docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.5/inventory.json \
  --after-executable build-preset/cs80_preset_capture \
  --pressure-before /tmp/cs80-a33/pressure-before \
  --pressure-after /tmp/cs80-a33/pressure-after \
  --out NEW_COMPARISON_REPORT.json
```

The helper refuses to overwrite existing evidence. Its focused self-test
passes gain-only, polarity-inversion, silence and distinct-spectrum controls,
a valid float WAV, and six malformed-WAV negative cases.
[Actual self-test output](../verification/a33-comparator-self-test.log)

The common-master diagnostic builds against the current core:

```sh
g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Isrc \
  docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.5/verification/a33-touch-equal-trim.cpp \
  build-preset/libcs80_core.a -pthread -o /tmp/cs80-a33/touch-equal-trim
/tmp/cs80-a33/touch-equal-trim
```

The diagnostic source contains the exact old/new inventory vectors used by the
comparison, with zero-default appended fields for older controls. Hashes of the
retained reports, pressure-control CSVs and diagnostic files are listed in
[the retained-file record](../verification/a33-retained-files.json). Private
supplemental WAVs remain under `/tmp/cs80-a33`; the main matched WAV sets remain
in the two directories passed to the comparison command.

No production source, preset data, shared build, calibration table, CMake,
commit or remote ref was changed by A33.
