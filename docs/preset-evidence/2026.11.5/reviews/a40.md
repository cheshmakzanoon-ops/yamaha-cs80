# A40 — independent choir/pad identity, long holds and output margin

## Outcome

The reviewed choir paths retain distinct open **ah** and covered **oo**
responses, captured geometry and controlled pressure routes. No defect in
their fixed-resonance mechanism, ordinary key-release lifecycle or pad
identity was found in this bounded review. Thirty recipes owned by
`src/core/curated/Pads.inc` retain their previous names, categories, tags and
host order; the complete identity list is retained in
[a40-review-results.json](../verification/a40-review-results.json).

The longer performance test did expose **narrow output margin** in Cathedral
Pad and the related original program Golden Pads 66. None of the measured
cases exceeded full scale, but their dense upper chords approached it within
0.24 dB. A40 recommends an output-volume cap of **0.650 for both existing
programs**. The parent accepted these caps and owns their production changes,
calibration preservation, regression registration, rebuilt inventory and
native qualification. A40 did not edit production source, preset tables,
shared builds or workflows, and did not commit or push.

All audio measurements below use the immutable local core from source
`6094bce18c8b19da804c2fbc9a00e31969a7f579`, with only the explicitly stated
volume overrides in the proposed-cap probes. They are not claims about an
already qualified final native binary.

## Review coverage and remaining risk selected

The review first read [A16](a16.md), [A17](a17.md), [A21](a21.md) and
[A23](a23.md), together with `InstrumentBody.h`, `Engine.cpp`, the pad recipes,
`VocalBodyTests.cpp`, `PadPolyRefinementTests.cpp` and the saved-state paths.
Those earlier checks already cover direct vocal pitch/formant tracking,
velocity and pressure spectra, short recipe gestures, missing-field state
contracts and endpoint safety. A40 did not repeat their full sweeps.

The untested integration risk selected here was a **fully developed,
eight-note held chord**, including pressure rises well after note onset and
the complete ordinary key-release tail. The existing bank audit's stress
chord lasts one second; several pad ranks have attacks longer than that.
Consequently its reported stress maximum must remain scoped to that window.

### Choir signal path

Both ranks of Choir Pad select Organ model 15, variant 3, at body mix 1.0.
Their sizes are 1.02 and 0.95. Dark Choir selects variant 4 on both ranks,
also at full mix, with sizes 1.14 and 1.03 and a 16-foot/8-foot registration.
Choir Pad's two ranks are both 8-foot. The second rank is a lighter, later
color, not an added octave stop.

The vocal source contains harmonics of the played pitch. Broad tract
responses are evaluated at each harmonic's absolute frequency and corrected
when pitch moves. Their authored first two centers are 760/1180 Hz for ah
and 330/850 Hz for oo at size 1.0; size divides the effective center
frequencies. A common gain bound preserves their relative response while
limiting modal amplitude. The existing control-rate interpolation and
periodic oscillator-magnitude normalization remain in use. This is a
source/filter construction, not an organ registration merely relabeled
“choir.”

The Engine filters the body through its effective panel HP/LP path before
the rank VCA. The choir recipes use broad, low-resonance passbands so these
filters do not deliberately replace the vowel regions with a narrow
keyboard-following peak. The panel filters still affect the complete sound.
Pressure reaches intrinsic body effort as `touchPressure * afterBrill`,
while `afterLevel` independently drives the rank's level. It does not bypass
the selected destinations as an unconditional raw-MIDI pressure path.

Free oscillator drift is zero in both choir recipes, but rank detune and
restrained chorus remain. Therefore the ensemble output should not be
described as entirely free of pitch modulation. The body itself adds
spectral motion and breath without adding a new pitch-jitter source.

The two variant selectors remain appended controls with Standard defaults.
`normaliseRestoredState` supplies declared defaults for missing fields;
the corresponding old-session, old-user-preset and inactive-A/B test
contracts specifically replace a receiving instance's live vocal choice
with Standard. Incompatible model/variant pairs also resolve to Standard
at note onset. This portion is a source and test-contract review, not an
independent native APVTS execution.

## New long-chord measurement

`a40-long-chord-probe.cpp` links a private immutable copy of the existing
core. Each case uses the complete factory controls at 48 kHz, MIDI notes
**36, 43, 48, 55, 60, 64, 67, 72**, velocity 1.0, and a 20-second hold.
Pressure is zero from 0–4 seconds, one from 4–8, zero from 8–12, and one
thereafter. Ordinary note-offs occur at exactly 20 seconds, followed by a
12-second tail. There is no audio normalization, reset, all-notes-off mute,
or parameter override in these original-source cases.

The seven cases render **10,752,000 stereo frames**. All **686 checks pass**:
correct per-window sample counts, finite samples, no full-scale crossings,
an audible final held window and no remaining active voices at the end.

| Existing program | Maximum peak, dBFS | No active voices observed by this time after key-up |
|---|---:|---:|
| Choir Pad | −6.303948 | 3 s |
| Dark Choir | −5.335563 | 3 s |
| Slow Pad | −3.489896 | 6 s |
| Evolving Pad | −3.089677 | 7 s |
| Cathedral Pad | **−0.435907** | 9 s |
| Arctic Pad | −3.116293 | 5 s |
| Pressure Pad | −3.748791 | 4 s |

The end times are observations at one-second boundaries, not exact envelope
time estimates. The last two seconds contain residual output around
−81.6 to −83.1 dBFS despite zero active voices; this review does not claim
bitwise silence or attribute that residual to a specific circuit element.
It is 60.5–69.7 dB below the preceding held windows in these cases.

## Follow-up and exact cap recommendation

The Cathedral result justified a small additional matrix, rather than a
second whole-bank sweep. The follow-up uses twelve-second full-pressure
holds with the wide chord above, a close middle chord
`48,50,52,53,55,57,59,60`, and a close upper chord
`60,62,64,65,67,69,71,72`. The original volume is also checked with velocity
0.8 and with pressure zero. The exact existing
`LibraryAuditTests::measure` function supplies the published phrase-window
and one-second stress comparisons.

An initial proposed Cathedral volume of 0.685 keeps every sample finite and
within full scale, with an audit window RMS of −20.005133 dBFS. Its close
upper chord still peaks at −1.333909 dBFS, so the recommendation was refined
to 0.650 and that exact case and audit measure were executed again.
The separate 32-second 0.685 run also passes all 98 checks, peaking at
−2.187914 dBFS. Those original proposal results are retained, not replaced.

The parent identified the related original **Golden Pads 66**, host index
109. Its actual recipe was measured independently: it has additional
filter, envelope and touch differences, so its behavior was not inferred
from Cathedral's trim ratio. Its current-volume 32-second performance
peaks at −0.410462 dBFS, remains unclipped, and releases every active voice;
all 98 checks pass.

| Existing program | Host index | Source volume | Source close-upper peak | Proposed cap | Measured close-upper peak at cap | Exact audit window RMS at cap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Cathedral Pad | 303 | 0.863 | −0.237805 dBFS | **0.650** | **−1.789454 dBFS** | **−20.460678 dBFS** |
| Golden Pads 66 | 109 | 0.854 | −0.235365 dBFS | **0.650** | **−1.688353 dBFS** | **−20.374090 dBFS** |

At these exact caps, the phrase-window levels remain 9.54 and 9.63 dB above
the unchanged −30 dBFS floor. The ordinary one-second stress peaks are
−7.420103 and −7.159827 dBFS. The nominal master reductions are approximately
2.46 and 2.37 dB, respectively.

Output-stage peak behavior is nonlinear: these maximum peaks were measured,
not obtained by subtracting the nominal volume change from an old peak.
The caps lower output drive while leaving the body, envelopes, touch and
pressure control settings intact. They do not establish a guarantee over
every possible chord, expression setting or arbitrary performance sequence.

Across the original/proposed long performances there are **882 passing
checks**. The eleven twelve-second exploratory chord cases and nine
32-second performances total **20,160,000 stereo frames**, excluding the
five calls to the existing audit measure. No sample in this bounded matrix
is nonfinite or crosses full scale. The final 0.650 proposals were directly
measured on the worst observed dense upper chord and audit phrase; A40 did
not rerender every other chord or the full bank at those final caps.

## Limits

This review establishes bounded long-performance stability, ordinary voice
release, explicit recipe distinctions and measured margin for the proposed
caps. It does not establish perceptual indistinguishability, native host
qualification, a realtime CPU budget, or arbitrary-event safety.

The vocal profiles are authored broad resonances with a 24-harmonic source.
Very low notes cannot cover every upper region, and high notes sparsely
sample the lower regions. They do not model words, consonants, individual
singers or singer-specific resonance retuning. The parent retains the
all-platform build, host, validator, package and final-source publication
gates.
