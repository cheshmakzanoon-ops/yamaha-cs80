# A15 — Bass and plucked-string performance refinement

## Scope

Reprogrammed the existing **24 Bass** and **16 Plucked** recipes in `src/core/curated/Bass.inc` and `src/core/curated/Plucked.inc`. No program was added, renamed or moved. Original-bank voices inherit these named source recipes. This assignment did not edit the engine, body implementation, calibration tables, or other recipe families.

The preceding source is the isolated **2026.11.4** baseline (`8685e9ed39d6347b67401da7590b7e1e8e95472d`). Candidate measurements use the integrated 2026.11.5 engine, including the independently reviewed struck-body normalization and captured register articulation. Absolute levels below were measured before final bank calibration; relative touch and decay measurements distinguish behavior from a simple master-volume change.

## Programming decisions

- **Touch:** individually reduced fixed VCA floors on all 23 touch-sensitive basses and all 16 plucked voices. Initial touch now owns most of the played level. The floors differ by role: dark/round/pressure basses retain more low-velocity support than bright pick or squelch accents. The final master-level pass remains separate.
- **Natural string aftersound:** Guitar I, Guitar II, Nylon Pluck, Steel Pluck, Harp, Koto, Mandolin, Zither and Dulcimer Pluck use fully decaying main bodies with individually voiced, small outer-envelope pedestals. The body modes themselves continue losing energy, while the external amplifier no longer prematurely erases every slow partial. Coupled Mandolin/Zither courses retain separate timings.
- **Key damping:** guitar/nylon/steel strings now have shorter, deliberate key-release contours. Harp, Zither and Dulcimer keep their deliberately open ringing endings. Bass aftersound remains separate from key release.
- **Picked Bass:** preserved its 6.4-second, zero-sustain main contour and existing held-note decay contract. Improved touch, shortened key damping and reduced the brief auxiliary pick-noise amount; the new register law supplies the lower-register extension. The detailed existing bass regression remains unchanged and passes.
- **Fretless/Upright:** small body-driven aftersound and shorter key damping let the lower string ring while keeping phrase endings controlled. Pluck Bass remains a zero-sustain mixed circuit/body voice.
- **Muted and nasal plucks:** Muted Pluck and Banjo retain zero sustain and compact body decay. Their modest timing extensions preserve a pitched body behind the contact sound; they still disappear on held keys. Funky, Drone and Pressure voices retain the circuit behavior described by their names.
- **Mandolin:** removed the continuous 12 Hz amplitude flutter. Paired course tuning remains; deliberate tremolo picking comes from repeated notes. The description/tag now matches that behavior.
- **Pedal Bass:** left the two sine registrations, tuning and velocity neutrality intact. Coordinated, shorter key releases improve low-note separation; rounded onset remains.

### Concrete behaviors

Pedal Bass remains velocity-neutral within 0.1 dB; its 80–120 ms post-release window is **−55.52 dB** relative to the held note, versus **−41.30 dB** before. The bass precision suite retains a disappearing Rubber Bass and a ringing-but-decaying Picked Bass: Picked's 0.8–1.1 second body relative to the 20–80 ms onset is **−8.46 / −8.68 / −9.51 dB** at MIDI 36/48/60. Its intentionally shortened controls are **−15.75 / −16.44 / −17.79 dB**; all held-tail gates pass.

Nylon Pluck retains useful string overtones at MIDI 48/60/72 and velocities 0.30/0.85: the existing spectral test measures **−8.08 to −11.00 dB** overtone/body ratios. A sine-dominant control remains below −15 dB. Thus touch/aftersound changes did not erase the string spectrum or merely substitute a sine.

## Limits and integration

These tests establish audibly scaled dynamic contrast, independent decay roles and preserved note identity in the authored synthesizer. They do not establish equivalence to a measured bass, guitar, harp, banjo or kalimba, nor replace human listening. No sampled instruments or new physical measurement data were introduced.

Parent registration: `cs80_bass_plucked_refinement_tests`, source `tests/BassPluckedRefinementTests.cpp`, linked to `cs80_core`, timeout 120 seconds. Tests use heap-owned Engine instances and 128-frame blocks. All source-family names, ordering and program addresses remain unchanged. Final all-bank calibration and native platform validation are parent integration work.
