# A39 — sustained bow lifetime and repeated-note ownership

Source reviewed: `6094bce18c8b19da804c2fbc9a00e31969a7f579`, factory revision
2026.11.5. Comparison inventory: `8685e9ed39d6347b67401da7590b7e1e8e95472d`,
factory revision 2026.11.4.

## Outcome and scope

No production correction is warranted by this review. The revised string
recipes retain an audible long bow, return to their quieter held level after
pressure is eased, preserve independent note ownership through overlapping
lines, and retain effort contrast through repeated reuse of the eight voice
slots. All 28 whole-factory renders are finite and below full scale; all finish
with zero active voices and zero held keys.

The initial diagnostic has **560 checks, including five failed release-floor
assertions**. Those failures are retained in its source and log. A separate
causal diagnostic passes **69 checks across 12 renders** and identifies the
residual as the existing chorus output floor, not a continuing bow. Neither
production code nor factory controls were changed to satisfy the initial
noise-free assumption. The initial result must not be summarized as a
560/560 pass.

This is an independent, selected review of nine existing recipes: Solo Violin,
Solo Cello, Chamber Strings, Slow Strings, Bowed Swell Strings, Tutti Strings,
Marcato Strings, Swell Strings and Spiccato Strings. It supplements the broader
family coverage in [A13](a13.md), the final Marcato level correction in
[A31](a31.md), and the generic body lifetime contracts in [A08](a08.md).
It does not repeat their full family or calibration sweeps.

## Source findings

The final inventories preserve the selected names, categories and host indices.
The whole Strings category contains 59 existing programs, including 31 curated
programs; this report's new rendered checks cover the nine recipes named above.

`Strings.inc` retains deliberate differences between instrument roles:

- Solo Violin is an 8′ modeled main body with a short, independently decaying
  noise/bow-friction rank, no chorus, no rank detuning and centered pans. Solo
  Cello has a 16′ main rank, a larger modeled body and a separately fading
  friction rank. These are dry solo-instrument colors; their names do not
  switch the engine into a new monophonic legato mode.
- Chamber Strings uses two closely tuned modeled ranks without chorus. Tutti
  Strings combines a 16′ main body and 8′ upper section. Slow Strings and
  Bowed Swell Strings retain their separately timed long entrances and
  releases.
- Swell Strings retains zero initial-touch gain on both ranks and a quiet
  pressure-responsive bed. Spiccato retains zero amplitude sustain; Marcato
  retains its independent upper accent followed by a sustained main body.
- The wider source family still includes intentional circuit-based synthetic
  strings and a constant-level String Machine. A realistic bowed-body model
  has not been indiscriminately applied to every name containing “Strings.”

These underlying body identities largely predate .5. The new recipe work
chiefly reduces the large fixed VCA contributions and increases individually
authored initial-touch and pressure ranges. For example, Solo Violin's main
fixed level changes from 0.44 to 0.095, initial-touch level from 0.64 to 0.88,
and aftertouch level from 0.28 to 0.38. Solo Cello's corresponding values change
from 0.45/0.60/0.30 to 0.11/0.86/0.40. These changes apply to the sounding main
ranks; they are not master-volume adjustments. The retained evidence lists
every changed existing non-volume control for the nine selected programs.

The shared `BodyArticulation.h` now captures the bow attack with each accepted
note and weights it by the onset body mix. A firm strike advances the attack;
it does not add pitch randomness or convert the bow into a decaying struck
body. The bowed model retains sustained harmonic excitation, separate
partial-growth times, fixed body/formant response during pitch motion and
bounded spectral movement. Those are authored synthesis mechanisms, not a
measured acoustic-equivalence claim.

### Twelve-second held bows

Eight sustained recipes are rendered at played MIDI 48 and 84. Velocity is
0.80, pressure rises from zero to 0.90 at 5 seconds, returns to zero at
8 seconds, and the key is released at 12 seconds. Rendering continues to
16 seconds. Four additional runs use the complete eight-note section
`48, 52, 55, 59, 62, 65, 69, 72` for Chamber, Slow, Bowed Swell and Tutti Strings.
The 16′ cello/tutti main ranks sound one octave below the played key; the low
violin case is a synthetic-range stress check, not a violin-range authenticity
claim.

Earlier unpressed RMS is measured at 3.5–4.5 seconds; pressed RMS at
6.5–7.5 seconds; returned unpressed RMS at 10–11.5 seconds. All 20 returned
bows remain audible under the diagnostic's −100 dBFS floor. Their returned
level differs from the earlier steady bow by at most **2.166415 dB**, comfortably
inside the private ±6 dB continuity bound. This bound checks loss or runaway
of the held body, not a timbral match.

### Repeated and overlapping phrases

Solo Violin, Solo Cello, Spiccato and Marcato each receive 12 repeated MIDI-60
strokes at 250 ms spacing and 150 ms hold. Velocities alternate 0.25/0.90.
This runs past the eight physical voice slots and checks whether a later
strike remains playable after earlier voices have been reused. All 24
soft/firm pairs retain audible output and more than the diagnostic's 3 dB
contrast requirement.

| Existing recipe | Minimum measured repeated soft/firm contrast | Maximum |
|---|---:|---:|
| Solo Violin | 8.044 dB | 8.401 dB |
| Solo Cello | 7.725 dB | 8.002 dB |
| Spiccato Strings | 6.356 dB | 6.935 dB |
| Marcato Strings | 5.189 dB | 11.148 dB |

Each of these four recipes also receives 13 ascending semitone steps, starting
at MIDI 55, with 180 ms spacing and 240 ms holds. The resulting 60 ms overlaps
exercise release of the previous key while the next key sounds. Every step
retains audible output. Ten milliseconds after each MIDI event, the actual
held-note set and held-voice count exactly match the requested keys. All
**280 ownership checks** pass across the held, repeated and overlapped runs.
Every initial run finishes with zero held keys and zero active voices.

## Five initial floor failures and causal resolution

The first diagnostic expected a released tail to be at least 40 dB below the
quiet held body, and expected short completed phrases to fall below −100 dBFS.
The low Bowed Swell case, both pressure-only Swell cases and both Marcato
phrase types fail those private assumptions. Their voice ownership and
retirement checks still pass. The initial source and failed log are preserved.

The follow-up independently renders each affected factory program without ever
pressing a key, then compares it with a played/released render over the same
5.5–5.95-second window. A second pair changes **only** `chorus_on` to zero as a
causal diagnostic control. This does not modify the factory bank.

| Existing recipe | Released complete-factory RMS | Never-played complete-factory RMS | Never-played RMS with only chorus bypassed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Bowed Swell Strings | −82.335426 dBFS | −82.335426 dBFS | −102.982588 dBFS |
| Swell Strings | −82.130446 dBFS | −82.130446 dBFS | −102.982588 dBFS |
| Marcato Strings | −82.033736 dBFS | −82.033736 dBFS | −102.982588 dBFS |

All six released/never-played comparisons match to the printed
**0.000000000 dB**. The chorus-only bypass lowers the idle floor by
**20.647–20.949 dB**. All 12 follow-up renders finish with zero active voices.
The follow-up passes **69 checks with no failures**.

`BbdChorus.h` explicitly supplies independent packet noise and residual clock
feedthrough. Its bytes are identical in the baseline, pinned source and current
workspace; SHA-256 is
`380733392e63e8ae9b183ad6816f9f626702824875928a9ef689321a25f4d0f2`.
The affected recipes' chorus on/mode/speed/depth, analog-noise and master-volume
values are also identical to baseline. The five failures therefore do not
identify a newly stuck note or a new .5 output-noise processor. A quieter
pressure-only held bed merely makes a fixed absolute output floor a larger
fraction of the note's level. No production threshold or DSP was changed.
