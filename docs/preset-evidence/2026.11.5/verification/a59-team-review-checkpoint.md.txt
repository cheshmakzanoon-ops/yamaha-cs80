## Supporting execution and integrity boundaries

The accepted screenshot execution remains source 09af. Its original artifact,
UI smoke result and two golden comparisons do not become a c76 execution by
renaming a file. The original filter matrix also remains source 09af. Each needs
an explicit relevant-input extension to the final candidate. The changed
capture Python fixture is not automatically treated as a UI or filter input;
its exclusion must follow the executed target/configuration dependency review.
The replacement DSP workflow, including document-dependent fixtures, must be
qualified from actual c76 results.

The output-manifest review checks the declared retained files and local links.
Source/input snapshots in earlier reviews intentionally describe earlier Git
objects or working-tree observations. They are not blindly compared with
subsequently edited documentation. File-hash agreement establishes identity,
not the truth of a measurement or the success of a native run.
[A59 integrity checkpoint](preset-evidence/2026.11.5/reviews/a59.md)

Objective software checks establish defined signal, compatibility and packaging
behavior. They do not establish a human listening panel, acoustic
indistinguishability, a calibrated physical CS-80 match, or universal host
compatibility. Actual installation bytes, validators, original process exits,
public download responses and retention dates remain separate release gates.
