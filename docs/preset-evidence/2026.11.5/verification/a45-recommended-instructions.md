After installing the updated build and rescanning or reopening your host, open **Library → FACTORY LIBRARY**, select the factory entry, and press **LOAD** (or **RELOAD**, if that is the label shown). Do this even if the same preset name is already displayed. Let held notes and the sustain pedal release, then play fresh notes; the body model, decay, size and variant are captured at note onset. Save the project to keep the revised settings. Older projects, saved user patches and A/B slots retain their stored controls. **REVERT** restores the sound present when Library opened, which may be the older recipe.
