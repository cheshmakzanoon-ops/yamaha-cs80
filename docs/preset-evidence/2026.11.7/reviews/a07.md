# A07 — low-register choir harmonic coverage

## Change and acoustic boundary

The previous 24-harmonic choir bank stops at 1,569.8 Hz on C2. Its authored
Ah regions at 2,800/3,500 Hz and Oo regions at 2,400/3,200 Hz therefore had no
voiced harmonics there. A direct pre-change PCM probe confirmed that Ah C2
h43 was only 4.52e-6, comparable to nearby breath noise at 3.78e-6.

`InstrumentBody.h` now gives **only Ah/Oo** a captured onset budget
`clamp(4400 / (bodySize * keyHz), 24, 48)`. The existing fractional final-mode
fade preserves continuity. Fixed-formant history expands to the existing
48-mode bank capacity; no oscillator capacity, parameter, preset, model ID,
sample-loop allocation, or live bank rebuilding is added. Standard-model
budgets and the separate piano course allocation are unchanged.

Additional modal phases extend the original PRNG sequence, after which the
breath stream resumes from its original position after harmonic 24. This
preserves both the original high-register PCM and breath continuity when a
nearly silent additional mode appears. Absolute modal-sum normalization and
host-rate antialiasing retain their existing bounds.

Coverage remains finite: default-size C2 now reaches 3,139.5 Hz, restoring the
2,800-Hz region but still truncating the 3,500-Hz region. E2 reaches 3,955.5 Hz;
the complete 4,400-Hz target becomes available around F-sharp2 (91.67 Hz).
The original 24 modes suffice above approximately F-sharp3. Body size shifts
both boundaries. A subsequent bend keeps its captured bank, while every
admitted harmonic continues to cross fixed-Hz formants.

The [UNSW voice introduction](https://www.phys.unsw.edu.au/jw/voice.html), read
for this change, supports the qualitative separation of harmonic source and
vocal-tract resonances. The existing profiles, 4,400-Hz coverage target and
mode limit are authored engineering choices, not a fit to singer recordings.

## PCM evidence

[LowChoirSpectrumTests.cpp](../../../../tests/LowChoirSpectrumTests.cpp) passes
**1,342 checks, zero failures**. The exact pre-A07 header passes only 908 of
1,000 executed checks, failing **92**; missing modes prevent later eligible
partial comparisons, so totals intentionally differ. At 44.1/48/96 kHz, the
new C2/E2/C3 upper harmonic groups stand **40.559–55.291 dB above adjacent
nonharmonic noise**, and −37.939 to −21.553 dB relative to their fundamentals.
The old group measurements are noise, not restored harmonics.

Across upward, downward and fractional bends, added harmonics match direct
target-vowel captures within **0.445880 dB** after fundamental normalization.
Ah/Oo spectral contrast remains **22.675–28.180 dB** across sizes 0.75–2.0.
At budget boundaries 24/25/32/40/47/48, the largest whole-waveform relative
difference at size ±1 ppm is **7.351e-6**; every case converges as the parameter
distance shrinks. This also rejects a breath reseed at a budget crossing.

The suite checks finite bounded PCM at host rates 8–96 kHz, internal factors
1/2/4, MIDI 0–127, extreme sizes/velocities, and exact deterministic replay.
No 49th voiced harmonic appears in the measured low-register spectra.
All **21** before/after PCM hashes match for the 19 standard models and both
high-register choir variants at MIDI 60. The unchanged **VocalBodyTests**
suite also passes **895 checks**, with 0.039162 dB worst original tracking
error. An optional sanitizer run was not qualified because LeakSanitizer
terminated in this host's ptrace environment; no sanitizer pass is claimed.

## Cost and reproduction

The actual onset budgets at default-size MIDI 36/48/60 occupy **48/34/24**
modes. A seven-run, alternating, single-process `-O3 -DNDEBUG` wall-clock
probe measured the following medians at 96-kHz internal/48-kHz host rate:

| MIDI note | Before, ns/sample | After, ns/sample | Ratio |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 36 | 76.090 | 137.368 | 1.805 |
| 48 | 70.175 | 101.623 | 1.448 |
| 60 | 71.453 | 66.688 | 0.933 |

These are informational shared-host timings; separate-process timings varied
substantially. **A11 owns the rigorous combined CPU qualification.** The
48-mode maximum is structural, independent of those timing measurements.
