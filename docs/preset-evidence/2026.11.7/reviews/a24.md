## Intended articulation, ring and tempo behavior

[`Engine::updateArpeggiator`](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) schedules a gate lasting **58% of the period**, or **45% in Random mode**. Numbered recipes’ [`paceSequence`](../../../../src/core/OriginalCircuitLibrary.inc) shapes attack, decay and release against that gate and the remaining gap. The upper accent follows the rate more strongly than the body. Ringing main bell ranks retain their independent decay and echo tail. This avoids imposing generic pluck behavior on the intentional bell sequences.

The seven bell recipes use **ring amount 0.022, speed 211 Hz and depth 0**. Ring depth controls the carrier-frequency envelope, while ring amount controls the wet blend. Therefore these are a fixed-carrier ring contribution with **no frequency-envelope sweep**, even though ring attack/decay parameters are stored. [`RingModulatorCircuit::frequency`](../../../../src/core/RingModulatorCircuit.h) returns the speed value exactly at zero depth. The other 20 sequences have zero ring amount. The bell echo is approximately 375 ms; the zero-depth distinction prevents mistaking stored envelope controls for audible frequency motion.

The [factory capture CLI](../../../../src/tools/preset_capture.cpp) holds one source MIDI note and leaves the preset’s arpeggiator enabled. That capture can contain generated notes, but its generic audio metrics do not establish multi-key order, gate spacing or transport behavior. The fresh probe instead observes generated held-note transitions under an actual three-key phrase.
