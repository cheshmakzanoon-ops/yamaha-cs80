# A04 — piano principal partial-tail repair

Baseline: `18711f93`. Implements the bounded mechanism repair recommended in
[A01](a01.md). A04's production edit is confined to the piano loss condition in
`InstrumentBody::makeHarmonicModes()`. No presets, parameters, modal counts,
course geometry, attack constants, or sample-loop operations were added.

## Mechanism and scope

The former principal-mode surcharge stopped at harmonic 6, although the base
string-loss law is continuous. The seventh and subsequent principal partials
therefore escaped the prompt damping imposed on the first six. The low
partials already have separately authored slower course components.

The repair applies the surcharge to every piano principal mode and caps its
index at 5:

```cpp
if (model == piano)
    loss += registerLossScale(0.25)
          * (0.78 + 0.04 * std::min(harmonic - 1, 5));
```

For h1–h6 this is exactly the former expression. For h7+ it extends the h6
surcharge without introducing a growing high-frequency penalty. The second
course, stiffness, phases, normalization, velocity response, and partial
onset constants are untouched. The source remains model-gated.

## PCM regression

Added `tests/PianoPartialDecayTests.cpp`. It renders only the public body API;
it does not inspect private modes or assert source literals.

- C1 / MIDI 24 and C2 / MIDI 36 at host rates 44.1, 48, and 96 kHz, each with
  the production 2× internal rate.
- Two velocity/brightness pairs: `(0.35, 0.45)` and `(0.95, 0.80)`.
- Three hammer seeds: `123`, `0x7812a3`, and `0xeff00d33`.
- Decay-control endpoints 0.25 and 4 at 48 kHz, both bass registers, and two
  seeds, using proportionally scaled analysis times.
- Hann projections at the stretched h6/h7 frequencies and nearby off-partial
  probes. Both early partials must exceed `1e-4`; both late partials must
  exceed `3e-6`; each band must beat the off-partial probes by more than 8 dB.
  Every capture must also be finite, sounding, and below 0.80 absolute peak.

The two early windows start at 0.10 and 0.40 times the decay control. Three
late windows start at 2.20, 2.60, and 3.00 times that control. Window length is
`max(0.25, 0.25 * decay)` seconds, retaining frequency resolution for C1 at
the shortest control. Within each early/late group, squared projection
magnitudes are averaged before taking the h7/h6 ratio. This avoids dividing
by a single h6 course-beat null. The test independently checks every seed.

The maximum permitted early-to-late h7/h6 rise is **3 dB**, approximately a
factor of sqrt(2) in amplitude ratio. This allows course interference and
finite-window variation. A01's proposed +1 dB single-window limit is not used:
the broader pooled C2 measurement still rises by up to +1.87 dB after the fix.
Requiring every neighboring partial or every window to be monotone would
misdiagnose the intended beating. The old defect exceeds the pooled limit
in every one of the 44 rendered cases.

## Limits

The expected listening effect is less isolated upper-partial persistence in
low piano tails. That is an inference from rendered spectra. No reference
piano recordings or listening panel establish acoustic equivalence or a
preference. Full Stage Keys / Soft Keys filter, VCA, pedal, and release phrase
auditions remain integration work; this assignment tested the body mechanism
and existing body contracts. The repair adds no sympathetic resonance,
damper noise, or nonlinear string coupling. Non-piano identity above applies
to this isolated repair, not to the aggregate concurrent branch changes.
