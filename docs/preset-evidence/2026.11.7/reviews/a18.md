# A18 — Strings family, complete-preset texture review

**No Strings production change is requested.** The corrected bowed texture survives the actual solo recipes at HQ, with preserved native output and ample measured headroom. This is numerical source-build evidence, not a listening claim.

## Structural coverage: all 59 identities

[Structural coverage](../verification/a18/structural-coverage.json) compares every Strings identity, description, tag, address and complete parameter vector with baseline `18711f93`: **all 59 are unchanged**, comprising 28 original-bank and 31 curated programs. Each row records its recipe and complete-vector hash.

The actual recipes divide into **4 solo bowed identities**, **42 modeled ensembles/hybrids**, **2 detached bowed identities**, **1 plucked program**, and **10 circuit-only synthetic programs**. The four solos include the original-address Soft Strings 36 and Midnight Strings 45 finishes. Pizzicato Strings uses nylon/steel bodies; Spiccato Strings and Neon Strings 40 remain bowed strokes with zero held sustain. PWM, Thin, Analog Orchestra, Synth Strings Saw, String Machine and their original finishes retain circuit identities. Air Strings, ring shimmer, the crystal upper glint, Phaser Strings and Ensemble Plus remain distinct hybrid/modulation roles; category membership does not imply the same acoustic instrument.

Solo Violin retains body mix **0.98**, size **1.0**, and a brief separately filtered noise rank. Solo Cello retains mix **0.99**, size **2.1**, a 16-foot foundation and its lower friction band. Cellos retains two differently sized bodies (**2.05/1.9**) and a shorter upper section. Their authored filters, touch, contours, gains and effects are unmodified.

## Texture, articulation and headroom

Hann-windowed 1–12 kHz nonharmonic power, excluding ±20 Hz around harmonics, gives these held-window HQ-minus-native differences:

| Complete program | Baseline | Corrected |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Solo Violin | −6.054 dB | −0.258 dB |
| Solo Cello | −6.050 dB | −0.228 dB |
| Cellos | +0.144 dB | +0.218 dB |

Doubling harmonic exclusion to ±40 Hz leaves corrected differences within **0.362 dB**. The solo results demonstrate that authored filtering does not erase the recovered held texture. Cellos contains circuit, detuned and chorus energy, so its nonharmonic bands cannot isolate bow noise; A10 supplies that causal component evidence. Onset HQ/native differences are −0.102 dB for violin and +0.625 dB for cello, where their independent friction accents also contribute; no isolated-noise tolerance is imposed on those complete attacks.

Pressure increases held RMS by approximately **2.38 dB** (violin), **2.67 dB** (cello), **1.98 dB** (Cellos) and **1.62 dB** (PWM). Register, bend and returned-pitch estimates remain within **1.476 cents** of intended pitches, including authored detuning. Sizes remain fixed across these register changes; this is not a causal size-parameter sweep.

All probe samples are finite and unclipped. Worst peak is **−8.776 dBFS**, the pressured PWM chord; the Cellos chord remains below **−16.12 dBFS**. Largest baseline/current peak change is **0.01082 dB**. Early release reduces the solo 0.8–1.3-second window by over **102 dB** versus held controls; final solo tails are below **−159 dBFS**. All final tails are below **−80.49 dBFS**.

Structural coverage is 59/59; fresh rendering covers the four named programs, not every original finish, plucked accent, effect chain or host rate. No production files, identities, thresholds, commits or remote state were changed.
