# A16 — Brass family, 2026.11.7

## Finding and coverage

**Dry acoustic breath balance and live fixed-formant bending survive the complete recipes. Delay Brass retains a measurable quality-dependent effects contribution.** No production edits are proposed by this review.

Structurally reviewed **59 programs: 26 original addresses and 33 curated**, including Delay Brass. Every parameter vector exactly matches the supplied .6 baseline. [The complete inventory](../verification/a16/brass-inventory.csv) records each name, source recipe, classification, footages, oscillators, filters/envelopes, body settings and touch routes. Intent classification gives **12 acoustic-inspired, 36 hybrid and 11 electronic**; these are programming categories, not claims of acoustic equivalence.

Six named programs received new complete-patch PCM coverage: **French Horn, Muted Trumpet, Trombone Glide, Pressure Swell Brass, Synth Horns and Delay Brass**. The remaining 53 received structural coverage only. This review makes no listening claim.

## Recipe distinctions

Acoustic-inspired horn recipes combine large, dark bodies with open main passbands and restrained upper ranks. French Horn inherits Covered Horn with its authored finish. Muted Trumpet combines a small, bright brass body with the second rank’s narrow nasal circuit edge; its HP cutoffs distinguish it from the horn foundation. Trombone Glide uses two 16′ ranks, unequal body sizes, a short scoop and portamento. Electronic recipes deliberately retain circuit identity: Synth Horns has no body model and combines saw/pulse movement with staggered filter bloom. Reed Brass and its original derivative combine brass and oboe bodies; pads, swells, ring/tremolo and delay programs retain hybrid intent.

Footage pairs are **47 × 8′/8′, four × 8′/4′, three × 16′/16′, four × 16′/8′, and one × 8′/5⅓′**. Tuba’s second rank is mixed out. Upper octave/fifth layers have subordinate articulation rather than replacing the played root.

Velocity and pressure retain separate level/brilliance routes. Pressure Swell Brass has low initial-level amounts (0.060/0.080) and strong after-level amounts (0.92/0.96); the rendered pressure gesture adds **18.150 dB Standard / 18.123 dB HQ**. French Horn adds 4.169/4.132 dB; Muted Trumpet 2.444/2.435 dB; Trombone Glide 2.377/2.376 dB. These measure pressure response, not a new velocity audit.

## Rendered evidence

Captured **257.4 seconds in 78 runs**, at 48 kHz, MIDI 60, velocity 0.8, three seconds held plus 0.3-second release, 113-frame blocks. Telemetry confirms Standard 2× and HQ/offline 8×. Factory settings remain intact except capture quality; explicitly labeled diagnostic copies are separate. Baseline linking uses exported `18711f93` headers for ABI compatibility.

An isolated core zeros only brass `airAmount`. Subtracting that output from the normal complete patch measures the causal sustained-air contribution through the original filters/VCA and output chain. It avoids conflating breath with analog noise, modulation leakage or total RMS.

| Program | Air contribution: HQ − Standard | Air/full-output ratio: Standard → HQ |
|---|---:|---:|
| French Horn | −0.0017 dB | −63.54 → −63.71 dB |
| Muted Trumpet | +0.0057 dB | −56.73 → −56.21 dB |
| Trombone Glide | +0.0132 dB | −60.18 → −60.16 dB |
| Pressure Swell Brass | +0.0006 dB | −68.86 → −68.59 dB |
| Synth Horns | Exactly zero contribution | Body model off |
| Delay Brass | +1.5522 dB | −58.45 → −55.79 dB |

These low-level contributions are signal measurements, not audibility judgments. Delay Brass’s diagnostic with only FX1/FX2 wet mixes zero reduces air variation to **+0.0061 dB**, locating the additional variation in the effects path. Its approximately −1.105 dB whole-output HQ/Standard difference already exists in the baseline. A universal complete-patch 0.5 dB equivalence claim is therefore unsupported.

All six current Standard captures exactly equal baseline PCM; all six HQ/offline pairs exactly match. All captures are finite; maximum peak is 0.14435.

For the three acoustic representatives, a separate isolated core disables only brass live formant correction. With the original filters/VCA, a **+2-semitone** bend changes normalized upper-partial balances by **0.625–0.912 dB** against that control; settled pitch estimates are within 0.32 cents of target. This confirms the fixed-Hz correction remains effective through the recipes, without asserting measured acoustic formants.
