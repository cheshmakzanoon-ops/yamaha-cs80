## HQ contact and headroom

Agogo at explicit HQ (8×, 384 kHz internal) retains the full-patch onset: old/new 1–20 ms RMS differs by only 0.000072 dB. The small change is concentrated at contact; maximum later sample difference after 250 ms is 9.32e−10.

A separate causal diagnostic silences modal oscillators after note-on while retaining authored Agogo contact, onset, filter and RNG. Across 128 seeds, HQ/reference contact RMS changes from **−5.880 dB before to −0.043 dB after** on both ranks. Reference-rate contact RMS is unchanged. This isolates the compensation rather than mistaking dominant pitched modes for noise strength; the diagnostic is separate from untouched-patch captures.

All 15 material/register renders are finite and below full scale (highest peak −23.17 dBFS). Six-note velocity-1 chords peak at −13.51 dBFS for Agogo and −16.44 dBFS for Gong. This leaves substantial measured headroom without claiming exhaustive polyphony coverage.
