## Complete family and character review

The actual category contains **56 Leads: 24 original-address and 32 curated
programs**. Drive Lead and Space Lead are included from `curated/Modern.inc`.
Every complete record—name, category, address, description, tags and all 204
values—matches committed `.6` source
`18711f93a39aa49f3d6f9a41784be456224facbe`: **11,424 exact parameter values**.
Each retains **5–37 changed prior non-master controls** relative to the
authenticated published `.4` inventory. All 56 non-master vectors remain
distinct. These are cumulative changes, with no new `.7` recipe edits.
[Full per-program audit](../verification/a23/structural-audit.json).

Fifty programs retain electronic circuit excitation. Sine/whistle, square and
narrow pulse, PWM, resonant, interval, ring-sideband, pressure and controller
roles remain distinct in the programming. Six add acoustic-inspired body
colors: Brass Lead, Reed Lead, Neo-Noir Brass Lead and their original-address
counterparts Amber Leads 115, Dark Leads 126 and Amber Leads 129. Their body
assignments are brass or clarinet/oboe; these remain authored hybrid leads,
not measured acoustic replicas. All Material selectors remain neutral.

Those six are structurally exposed to `.7` breath/contact noise normalization,
including sustained air/flutter. The family has no `.7` piano-decay,
standard-string-formant or low-choir exposure. Eligibility does not establish
that every note or internal sample rate changes.

**Mono priority is inapplicable:** all 56 default to eight voices. The actual
voice modes are 8 voices, 16 voices, and unison x2/x4/x8. There is no monophonic
last/low/high-note-priority mode. The source and UI mode list were checked;
“Mono (legato)” in the modulation editor concerns its envelope trigger, not
voice allocation. The overlap checks below therefore assess polyphonic note
ownership, not a nonexistent mono mode.
