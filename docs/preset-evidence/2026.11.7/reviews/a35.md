## What changes, and what the register limits mean

[A07](a07.md) expands only the two choir variants' onset budget to

`ceil(clamp(4400 / (bodySize × capturedBodyHz), 24, 48))`.

The last admitted mode can be fractionally weighted. Existing mode-sum bounds,
host-rate antialiasing and the **48-mode ceiling** remain in force. The prior
24-mode phase sequence is retained and the independent air stream resumes from
its prior position after mode 24. The change therefore does not require a
breath reseed at a fractional mode boundary.

The [Engine](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) captures body pitch with footage,
tuning reference, master tune and rank detune already included. At nominal
440-Hz tuning, a played C4/MIDI 60 into a 16-foot rank therefore captures a
C3/MIDI 48 body. The following counts are **derived nominal onset counts**, not
an assertion that all admitted harmonics have equal amplitude or survive the
complete patch's filters.

| Recipe group, including every finish | Played MIDI 36 | Played MIDI 48 | Played MIDI 60 | Played MIDI 72 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden Pads 52, Choir Pad: Ah, 8′ / 8′ | 48 / 48 | 33 / 36 | 24 / 24 | 24 / 24 |
| Blade Choir, Midnight Pads 55, Dark Choir: Oo, 16′ / 8′ | 48 / 48 | 48 / 33 | **30 / 24** | 24 / 24 |
| Hollow FX 141, Robot Voice: Ah first rank | 48 / — | 33 / — | 24 / — | 24 / — |

Every vocal rank can therefore enter the changed region at low notes. The
three Oo programs also enter it in the bank's ordinary played-MIDI-60 scene.
Conversely, a high held note keeps its original 24 modes; the new code is not
an unconditional addition of upper harmonics to every vocal performance.

**The deepest 16-foot rank remains deliberately incomplete.** At played MIDI
36 its fundamental is approximately **32.70 Hz**, so 48 harmonics stop near
**1.570 kHz**. The Oo rank's authored upper centers, after its size 1.14 scaling,
are about **2.105 and 2.807 kHz**. Neither can be reached by that rank's voiced
bank in this scene. Its 8-foot partner contributes a different, higher spectrum;
that does not remove the foundation's limit. The 8-foot Ah ranks at MIDI 36
reach their approximately 2.8-kHz regions, but their highest authored regions
can still lie above the 48-mode endpoint. Additional filtering and the lighter
upper rank further shape what reaches the output.

The [vocal profile](../../../../src/core/InstrumentBody.h) places Ah centers at
**760/1,180/2,800/3,500 Hz** and Oo centers at **330/850/2,400/3,200 Hz**, before
body-size scaling. These are authored broad resonances. The physically
motivated distinction is that harmonic pitch moves through a fixed tract
response; the numerical centers, widths and 4,400-Hz coverage target are not
fits to recorded singers or a phonetic corpus. A more complete harmonic bank
does not demonstrate universal vowel recognition or natural vocal realism.

## Brightness, touch, breath and pitch routes

The source preserves separate controls for intrinsic body color, effective
panel filters and VCA level. The body receives smoothed pressure multiplied
by the recipe's `afterBrill`; it adds **0.24 × that smoothed effort** to vocal
color, plus small bounded nonperiodic color motion. That motion does not alter
pitch. The effective HP/LP path then filters the complete body excitation.

| Recipe group | Velocity brightness I / II | Velocity level I / II | Pressure brightness I / II | Pressure level I / II |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Golden Pads 52, Choir Pad | 0.020 / 0.025 | 0.94 / 0.80 | 0.14 / 0.18 | 0.41 / 0.46 |
| Blade Choir, Midnight Pads 55, Dark Choir | 0.015 / 0.020 | 0.88 / 0.76 | 0.10 / 0.14 | 0.42 / 0.44 |
| Hollow FX 141, Robot Voice | 0.050 / 0.100 | 1.00 / 0.95 | 0.24 / 0.31 | 0.85 / 0.29 |

The FX table's rank-II values act on its **nonvocal** consonant; they are not
a second Ah effort route. All seven vocal bodies also capture strike velocity
for their harmonic excitation and onset. Their quiet intrinsic air amount is
`0.0012 + 0.0008 × (1 − strike)`, with a short velocity-dependent onset and
band-shaped noise. This is a modeled breath texture, with no independent
saved MIDI breath-controller assignment in these recipes. Pressure affects
effort, filtering and level through the routes above; it should not be
described as a dedicated breath-noise controller.

Both Ah pads map **CC1 to filter brightness at 0.05**. The other five programs
have no wheel-to-filter mapping. All seven have zero saved wheel-to-SUB pitch,
SUB pitch, pressure-to-SUB pitch, initial bend, portamento, modern LFO depth
and modulation-matrix amounts. Thus none has a programmed pressure vibrato
route. Each retains the ordinary **±2-semitone pitch-bend range**, with MPE off
and saved ribbon at zero; live ribbon pitch is supported by the Engine, but
no large ribbon or MPE gesture is newly qualified here.

During bends, the Engine supplies the live sounding rank frequency to the
body. Admitted harmonics cross the same size-scaled formants without resetting
phase. **The onset bank remains captured:** a downward bend does not create the
extra modes that a newly struck lower note might receive. A footage or size
change cannot be treated as a fresh voice-size admission until the relevant
next onset. These qualifications apply to the descriptions' pitch-stable
vowel claim.

Robot Voice and Hollow FX 141 additionally apply the authored fixed **73-Hz
ring carrier**, with ring amount **0.74**. Their output is a mechanical vocal
effect with sidebands and a quiet carrier floor, not an unprocessed singer.
A25 measures the retained **−67.427 dBFS** carrier floor; it is not silence,
delay echo, or evidence of an indefinitely held vocal voice.
