The attack values above are authored envelope settings, not measured physical

routes. Fourteen have no authored aftertouch routes; Tremolo LFO Organ alone
has rank-II aftertouch level `0.22`. Registration sustain is unity except
the deliberately decaying percussion rank. Authored detune, drift and voice
dispersion are zero throughout this family.

excitation and its seed independently of velocity and applies no struck-body
partial loss to sustained models. The rank envelopes supply key damping.
This supports the intended sustained electronic registrations without imposing
hammer dynamics or an acoustic-instrument equivalence criterion.

## Fresh representative measurements

The [probe](../verification/a20/organ_probe.cpp) rendered the untouched full
presets at 48 kHz Standard in 96-frame blocks. Single notes use MIDI 60,
velocity 0.85, a two-second hold and 0.4-second release. Eight-note chords use
MIDI **48, 55, 60, 64, 67, 72, 76, 79**, velocity 0.85 and pressure 0.8.
Only the LFO registration intentionally responds to that pressure. No output
normalization or preset override is applied. There are **18 current and 10
baseline captures**, with raw float PCM kept temporarily outside the repository.

Key damping compares RMS 75–105 ms after note-off with the preceding held
window. All 28 captures are finite and have zero samples above full scale.
The five single-note held-window changes from 0.5–1.0 s to 1.5–2.0 s lie
between **−0.022 and +0.019 dB**. This confirms stable held output for these
examples; it does not imply an unmodulated waveform in the LFO/chorus cases.
[All measured rows](../verification/a20/metrics.csv) ·
[PCM hashes](../verification/a20/pcm-manifest.json).

**Velocity and pressure.** All five complete soft/firm pairs, velocities
0.25 and 0.85, are PCM-identical. Flute Organ 8+4 is also PCM-identical at
pressure 0 and 0.8. Tremolo LFO Organ responds to its intentional upper-stop
route: pressure 0.8 raises held full-patch RMS **0.492 dB** and the narrowband
523.25 Hz magnitude **1.326 dB**. That full-mix band contains both the upper
stop and harmonics of the lower stop; it is not an isolated-rank measurement.

**Percussion and repeated keying.** Percussive Organ was held for 1.5 s,
released for 0.3 s, then struck again. Its full-patch octave magnitude falls
**24.171 dB** on the first hold and **23.094 dB** on the second, while the
fundamental changes by less than **0.001 dB**. The second onset's octave is
only **0.147 dB** below the first. This verifies separated-note retriggering
of the authored overtone. It does not establish physical contact keyclick
or a particular tonewheel organ's legato-percussion rules.

**Named tremolo.** Tremolo LFO Organ retains its quarter-second envelope
pattern: normalized quarter-second repeat error is **0.00732**, and the
half-period error is **26.62 times** larger. The waveform therefore retains
the authored four-pulse-per-second behavior under the same existing metric.

## Existing evidence reused and unresolved questions

The frozen-source [focused CTest receipt](../development/focused-ctest-receipt.json)
reports 31/31 passing tests, including `cs80_brass_organ_refinement_tests`,
`cs80_preset_voicing_tests` and `cs80_modern_preset_refinement_tests`.
These cover the fourteen traditional registrations' velocity, held sustain
and key damping, isolated/full-mix octave percussion, and the modern organ's
4 Hz pattern. A20 inspected and reused those results rather than rerunning
their unrelated family cases. The previous `.6` A24 review also has all-fifteen
soft/firm captures; exact parameter preservation plus the scoped PCM pairs
provide continuity without recreating that broad matrix.

No new source-bound Organ regression was found. Two concrete acceptance
questions remain outside this numeric review:

- **Relative registration loudness:** among these five examples, held single-note
  RMS spans **13.526 dB**, from Combo Organ to Tremolo LFO Organ. The measured
  eight-note Combo peak leaves **4.109 dB** of headroom. These differences are
  preserved from `.6`; whether transitions between those registrations need
  further balancing requires listening in the intended playing context.
  No equal-loudness or perceived-improvement claim follows from the numbers.
- **Musical realism and broader performance:** this checks one single-note pitch
  and one eight-note voicing. It supplies no worst-case register/voicing
  guarantee and no human approval of keying, chorus, tremulant or percussion.
  The sparse harmonic/circuit construction does not separately model measured
  pipes, a wind chest, tonewheels, contact clicks, a rotating speaker or a room.
  The registration descriptions are consistent with that authored scope.

No production source, tests, workflows, remote refs or existing programs were
changed. Native package qualification remains separate from these local core
measurements. Reproduce the evidence from the repository root with:

```sh
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python3 docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.7/verification/a20/reproduce.py
```
