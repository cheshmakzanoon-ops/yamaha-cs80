# A14 — Bass family and live string-body correction

**No Bass production change is requested before freeze.** A06's response correction survives the complete Engine path in representative bass programs, while the electronic control remains sample-identical. This is a numerical source-build review, not a listening or native-installation certification.

## All-program structural coverage

Checked **all 50 Bass programs**, including the original-address finishes and Drive Bass. All names, categories, host addresses and complete parameter vectors match the 2026.11.6 baseline. The [per-program classification](../verification/a14/inventory-review.json) records every name, body model, mix, material, size and affected rank:

- **18** have a standard bass/string body affected by A06. Bass is model 8; supporting steel, nylon and clav contacts use the same correction where enabled. All use standard material.
- **2** are brass-body/circuit hybrids outside A06: Brass Bass and Amber Bass 89.
- **30** are circuit-only programs. Their sine, pulse, resonance, octave/fifth, ring and driven identities remain intentional. Pedal Bass and Velvet Bass 90 retain velocity-neutral organ-like keying. Rubber Bass deliberately combines a synthetic scoop with a modest corrected bass-body accent; category membership does not imply an acoustic-bass claim.

No body amount, oscillator blend, filter, gain or other recipe value was changed for the comparison.

## Fresh complete-Engine PCM comparison

The [retained probe](../verification/a14/engine_probe.cpp) renders **Analog Bass, Pluck Bass, Fretless Glide and Upright Pluck** from the actual program library. Each has steady, upward-octave-and-return, downward-octave-and-return, and pressure scenarios, in both baseline and current builds: **32 stereo captures**, 48 kHz, MIDI 36, velocity 0.85, 2.3 seconds held plus 0.7 seconds released. The authored 16-foot foundation therefore sounds near **32.703 Hz**, and the downward octave reaches 16.352 Hz.

Ribbon ramps run 0.35–0.50 seconds, hold until 1.05 seconds, and return by 1.20 seconds. Pressure is 0.75 from 0.35 seconds in its separate scenario. Raw PCM remains temporary under `/tmp/cs80-a14`; hashes, measurements and executable/source provenance are retained in [pcm-analysis.json](../verification/a14/pcm-analysis.json). Baseline callers use archived baseline headers with the baseline library, avoiding the changed Engine/body ABI.

| Actual program | Current peak | Up-bend PCM difference / current RMS | Largest measured H2–H4 ratio change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Analog Bass | −19.565 dBFS | exactly zero | 0.000 dB |
| Pluck Bass | −27.585 dBFS | 7.404% | 2.427 dB |
| Fretless Glide | −26.551 dBFS | 7.336% | 2.689 dB |
| Upright Pluck | −26.045 dBFS | 10.162% | 2.682 dB |

Those nonzero output-spectrum changes demonstrate that the corrected body response is not erased by the authored oscillator blend or downstream filters. They do not establish a perceptual threshold. Analog Bass is **byte-identical in all four scenarios**. The three body programs' returned-note baseline/current differences fall below **−89.20 dB relative to current RMS**, consistent with the small static drift-related correction rather than a retained bend offset. Fitted fundamentals across steady/bent/returned windows stay within **0.545 cents** of the intended pitches.

All 32 renders are finite and unclipped. Current late-release RMS (2.8–3.0 seconds) is below **−128.16 dBFS** in every scenario. Fretless Glide's pressure window rises **1.611 dB**; the intentionally pressure-inert Pluck Bass and Upright Pluck remain unchanged. This does not qualify chord headroom or every keyboard register.
