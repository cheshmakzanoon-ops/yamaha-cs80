variation. This is a concrete coverage limit, not evidence that those programs

## What the three-second bank measurement establishes

The [matched-bank scope check](../verification/a34/matched-bank-scope.json)
verifies all 523 rows at **48 kHz, velocity 0.8, pressure zero**. The notes are
category-specific: **Bass 48; Winds and Leads 67; Bells 72; all other categories
60**. All rows are finite and have no samples over full scale. This is a
single-note, fixed-duration comparison of complete patches with their saved
levels and effects.

The [capture CLI](../../../../src/tools/preset_capture.cpp) normally chooses

`hold = clamp(max(active-rank saved VCA attack, saved VCF attack) + 1.5, 3, 10)`.

Its rank predicate is MIX below 0.99 for rank I and above 0.01 for rank II.
An explicit positive `--hold` returns immediately and bypasses that policy.
The root's `--hold 3 --tail 1` therefore intentionally standardizes capture
duration. Without that override, **50 programs** receive holds longer than
three seconds; the longest current default is **8.9 seconds**. Fifty is the
number receiving the additional margin, not the number whose attacks exceed
three seconds.

The CLI's reported body RMS is the final half-second **before its note-off**.
For the fixed run, that is 2.5–3 seconds, even if a filter entrance is still
in progress. `peak_window_seconds` marks the beginning of the loudest 50 ms
held RMS window, not the exact time of the largest individual sample. Neither
field should be labeled a completed, steady sustain without checking the
recipe's timing.

## Actual source timing, including the borderline programs

The [timing probe](../verification/a34/timing_probe.cpp) evaluates the exact
archived source functions for every program and both ranks: **1,046 rows** in
[all-rank-timing.csv](../verification/a34/all-rank-timing.csv). It includes all
eight voice cards, amplitude attack scaling at velocity 0.8, full velocity, and
the soft-velocity limiting value. The
[census](../verification/a34/timing-census.json) retains the full derivation.

For the source-performance engine, the accepted-note attack time is:

- **VCA:** saved attack × captured body articulation attack × macro time scale
  × card amplitude-envelope time ratio, with a 1 ms lower clamp.
- **VCF:** saved attack × macro time scale × card filter-envelope time ratio,
  with the same lower clamp.

The [Engine](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) sets these values at note-on.
[BodyArticulation](../../../../src/core/BodyArticulation.h) gives some breath and
bow models a velocity-dependent entrance; its attack factor does not depend
on pitch. [ToleranceModel](../../../../src/core/ToleranceModel.h) derives each
card's time ratio from its deterministic R×C draw, scaled by saved voice
dispersion. All 523 recipes have neutral macros, so the macro time scale is
one. A filter with zero IL and AL has no moving entrance span and is excluded
from the crossing count.

The [circuit envelope law](../../../../src/core/Dsp.h) charges toward a 1.5×
overshoot target and ends attack at the target comparator. Its nominal attack
endpoint is the mapped time above, subject to sample quantization; it is not
the old uniform-time/legacy-envelope approximation. Output latency is separate.
All 14 crossing programs have scanning, arpeggiation and unison voice mode
disabled. A fresh Engine resets its cyclic allocator to voice card zero, so

## Limits and reproduction

This review supplies **whole-bank timing coverage**, reuse of existing
whole-bank/family safety gates, and **five fresh selected scenes**. Among the
14 timing crossings, seven have current individual extended captures in the
reviewed evidence set; the seven remaining rows are explicitly identified
above. Their existing short-bank and structural coverage is not silently
promoted to a new individual long-hold pass. The late-peak pad examples also
show why an attack-time census alone cannot certify arbitrary sustained-stack
headroom.

The capture and timing probes compile against all `src` headers archived from
the frozen `.7` source and link the supplied `.7` core. No old-library/new-
header ABI combination is used. [Compilation provenance](../verification/a34/compile-provenance.json)
and [source/input/evidence hashes](../verification/a34/binding.json) bind the
result. Reproduce the five scenes with:

```sh
python3 docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.7/verification/a34/reproduce.py \
  --render --tmp /tmp/cs80-a34-1cee5a/reproduction
```

Raw PCM is temporary; the small measurements, manifests, source-bound harnesses
and exact scene definitions are retained. There is no listening-panel,
acoustic-equivalence, every-controller, all-sample-rate or native-plugin claim.
