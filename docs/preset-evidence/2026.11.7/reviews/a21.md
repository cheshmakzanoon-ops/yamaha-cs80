## Expression, slow envelope completion and stacked headroom

For MIDI 36, current held RMS gains from velocity 0.25 to 0.85 are **7.412 dB** for Choir Pad and **6.789 dB** for Dark Choir; their respective baseline gains are 7.473 and 6.836 dB. Pressure 0.8 adds **3.020 dB** and **2.810 dB**, within 0.001 dB of baseline. These are complete-patch touch responses, including the authored VCA and body paths.

Sweep Pad is held beyond both saved filter attacks (**2.75/3.85 seconds**). With no pressure assistance, its 4–4.5-second bloom is **13.321 dB** above the 25–125 ms opening; its 5.5–6-second window remains at **−28.982 dBFS**, **3.353 dB** below the bloom. The full hold and stack captures are **sample-identical** to baseline, confirming this slow synthetic control is unaffected by the choir correction.

This is headroom evidence for the stated scenes, not every possible voicing or controller gesture.
