## Release interpretation and reproducibility

Ring patches retain a quiet carrier after their voiced transient/tail decays.
For example, Metal Scrape's final second is **−67.298 dBFS RMS at 219 Hz**;
Space Sweep is **−68.886 dBFS at 31 Hz**, and UFO Landing is **−74.171 dBFS at
173 Hz**. Robot Voice/Hollow FX 141 settle to **−67.427 dBFS at 73 Hz**; the bell
pair settles to **−77.950 dBFS at 247 Hz**. The unchanged
[ring circuit](../../../../src/core/RingModulatorCircuit.h) explicitly models
carrier feedthrough through its multiplier offsets, and the unchanged
[Engine](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) continues clocking that carrier.
These floors agree with the programmed carrier frequencies. They must not be
described as silence, delay echoes, or proof of a stuck voice.

FX 141 produce exactly equal float32 samples. All 238 evidence checks pass.

files and every generated WAV. Raw WAVs stay in the named scratch directory;
