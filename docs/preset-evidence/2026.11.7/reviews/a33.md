# A33 — cumulative published `.4` → `.7` signal and parameter accounting

**PASS for this numerical accounting scope.** All **521 musical programs**
have cumulative signal-change evidence: **519** have different recorded audio
metrics in the matched default bank captures, and the **two pressure-required
Leads** have different waveforms when pressure is applied. All 521 also have
changed previously published non-master parameters: **493** extend beyond the
declared conservative level/pan exclusions, while **28** change active additive
rank-VCA baselines. The **two Init programs** retain equal measured signal
vectors and their published parameter values. These counts establish changes;
they do not establish perceptual improvement or listening approval.

## Level, spectrum and tail interpretation

For the **519 default-sounding musical programs**, four-second RMS decreases
in 455 and increases in 64. The median is **−1.01188 dB**, with the 10th–90th
percentiles **−2.87871 to +0.04983 dB**. The extremes are Choir Pad
**−9.62312 dB** and Kalimba **+4.07270 dB**. Peak changes range from
**−7.40467 to +8.52398 dB**, with median **−1.11762 dB**. Consequently,
cumulative changes are not a uniform bank-wide gain adjustment. Lower levels
are not inherently better, and an assessment of timbre preference would need
level-matched listening. [Exact distributions and extrema](../verification/a33/metrics-summary.json).

The recorded centroid is a **power-weighted, stereo, 4096-point Hann-window
measurement around each capture's own loudest 50 ms held window**. It is not
a whole-note timbre score. Centroid rises in 276 programs, falls in 242 and
is equal in one: median **+0.00289 Hz**, 10th–90th percentiles
**−8.33485 to +13.71792 Hz**. The extrema are Rain **−292.85815 Hz** and
Tubular Bells **+1060.56339 Hz**. The loudest-window start changes in 76
programs, so part of a spectral comparison can also reflect a different
selected moment. No systematic brighter/darker preference follows from the
near-zero bank median. [Measurement implementation](../../../../src/tools/preset_capture.cpp)
and [per-program values](../verification/a33/program-deltas.csv).

The CSV's “body” window is **2.5–3.0 seconds**, and its “tail” window is
**3.5–4.0 seconds**, after key-up at 3.0. Raw median changes are
**−0.57790 dB** and **−0.48534 dB**, respectively. Their raw extremes require
absolute levels: Agogo's tail moves from **−80.71579 to the −240 dBFS report
floor**, while Music Box moves from **−212.77992 to −87.74486 dBFS**. Such
large ratios of very quiet windows do not measure comparable changes in
heard note level. For the **238 pairs with both tail windows above −100
dBFS**, the median change is **−0.48361 dB**, range **−16.59630 to +17.26762
dB**. That cutoff is a transparent descriptive filter, not an audibility gate.
Across all 521 musical programs, late-tail windows above −60 dBFS number
**102 → 101**; above −80 dBFS, **181 → 173**. These counts do not establish
release duration, absence of quiet residue, or a fully developed envelope.
The fixed three-second hold can precede slow-attack completion; long-hold
review remains separate. [Full floor/threshold sensitivity](../verification/a33/metrics-summary.json).

## Why the 28 baseline-only cases still change touch response

A12 identifies 19 Bass, four Plucked, three Hardware Studies, one Brass and
one Keys program in this subgroup. Every program changes a VCA baseline on an
active rank with a nonzero initial-touch route. A33 adds only **two representative
causal checks**: the frozen `.7` Engine/current patch versus the same patch
with just its two `.4` VCA baseline values restored. Both arms use the same
`.7` headers and core library. These diagnostic restorations are not full
published `.4` audio reconstructions.

At velocity **0.25 versus 0.85**, pressure zero, 48 kHz and the category's
default note, the first 200 ms show:

| Representative | Firm-minus-soft span with restored `.4` baselines | Span with current baselines | Increase in span |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Analog Bass | 4.93422 dB | 8.38293 dB | +3.44870 dB |
| Funky Pluck I | 6.10111 dB | 8.98609 dB | +2.88499 dB |

A post-output scalar cancels from this firm/soft ratio, so it cannot reproduce
these changes. The one-second held-window measurements independently show
span increases of **3.44436 and 2.88320 dB**. This supports the additive-touch
interpretation in two representatives; it does not measure all 28 response
curves or establish that a wider relative span is preferable. Eight captures
total **12 seconds**, with zero nonfinite/over-full-scale samples and maximum
peak **−14.77476 dBFS**. [Causal results](../verification/a33/causal-velocity-summary.json) ·
[Source/header/core binding and PCM hashes](../verification/a33/causal-velocity-receipt.json).

## Probe bounds and reproduction

All **523 rows in each bank** report zero nonfinite samples and zero samples
over full scale. Published `.4`'s largest captured peak is **−13.53739 dBFS**
(Arp Bass); `.6` and `.7` both peak at **−13.40001 dBFS** (Bright FX 147).
These bounds apply only to these single-note probes. They do not cover dense
polyphony, every controller value, every rate, native plugin execution, or
other stress conditions. Whole-bank PCM was not retained; A33 consumes the
authenticated CSVs and does not claim new sample-by-sample whole-bank testing.
