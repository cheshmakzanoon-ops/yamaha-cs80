## Actual-program PCM measurements

`engine_gestures.cpp` rendered unchanged **Guitar II, Pressure Pluck, Harp, Koto and Banjo** at MIDI **48/60/72**, velocity **0.8**, **48 kHz stereo**, 2.2 seconds each. Guitar II exercises a full generic steel body; Pressure Pluck exercises its generic nylon body, steel contact and authored pressure route.

Four performances were compared: plain release at 1.0 seconds; wheel +2 semitones at 0.10 seconds, −2 at 0.35, return at 0.65; pressure 0.8 from 0.35–0.70 seconds; and sustain postponing damping until 1.70 seconds. There are **60 current and 60 baseline renders**. Each harness was compiled against its archive's matching headers; baseline headers came from `git archive 18711f93 src`, avoiding the changed Engine/body ABI. `binding.json` retains source, archive, executable and inventory hashes.

Results (`gesture-metrics.csv`, `summary.json`):

- **269 checks, zero failures**; finite, audible output throughout; largest current peak **0.04764**.
- Measured fundamental bend/return error versus matched plain windows stayed within **0.469 cents**.
- Generic current/baseline difference was **−98.1 to −80.5 dB** relative to plain-note RMS, versus **−33.5 to −22.0 dB** during bends. This shows the correction is active in the complete patches; it is not an independent formant-model oracle.
- Pressure Pluck gained **4.56–4.67 dB** during pressure. Zero-depth programs remained sample-identical to their plain-pressure controls.
- All 15 note/program combinations lost held energy and reduced spectral centroid. Held decay ranged from **−4.16 to −31.00 dB**; pedal advantage after key release ranged from **6.93 to 67.17 dB**, preserving both long Harp ringing and dry Banjo damping.
- **All 36 Harp/Koto/Banjo gesture captures were bit-identical to baseline**, preserving the .6 material behavior at this rate and these registers.

Existing full-program suites also passed: **PluckedMaterialPresetTests 124/0**, **BassPluckedRefinementTests 149/0**. These add touch, aftersound, material distinction and damping coverage beyond the five-program probe.
