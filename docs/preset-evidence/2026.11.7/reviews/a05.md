# A05 — rate-consistent body breath, contact and flutter

## Result and scope

Implemented the bounded correction identified in [A02](a02.md). `InstrumentBody::prepare()` caches two independent gains referenced to **96,000 Hz internal**: the 650/5200 Hz high-pass/low-pass noise band's exact stationary variance, and the 19 Hz flutter pole's variance. `tick()` uses those gains without changing the random generator, draw count, filter updates, envelopes, modal amplitudes or control timing. The 96 kHz noise-band multiplier is exactly 1 and the flutter depth is exactly its previous `0.012` constant.

Production edits are limited to prepare, the existing noise tick block, two cached doubles and a private variance helper. The concurrent piano-loss and string-formant changes in the same header belong to A04 and A06. No preset, parameter, saved identity, CMake entry, commit or remote state was changed by A05.

### Exact compensation

For low-pass coefficients `aL`, `aH` and poles `rL = 1-aL`, `rH = 1-aH`, the implemented high-pass first updates its state. Its cascade transfer function is:

`H(z) = aL*rH*(1-z^-1) / ((1-rH*z^-1)*(1-rL*z^-1))`.

The squared impulse-response sum simplifies to:

`V = 2*aL^2*rH^2 / ((1+rH)*(1+rL)*(1-rH*rL))`.

The helper evaluates the denominator as `(2-aH)*(2-aL)*(aH+aL-aH*aL)`. This avoids a near-unit pole-product subtraction and the pole-difference division in an equivalent partial-fraction expression. The stationary input variance cancels in `sqrt(V96k / Vrate)`. Representative gains are **1.3934522894 at 192 kHz**, **1.9581414546 at 384 kHz**, and **2.7611548607 at 768 kHz**. The 8 kHz–1.536 MHz legal range remains finite; a simple square-root-of-rate approximation would fail the low-rate tests.

Flutter independently uses the one-pole variance `aF/(2-aF)`. Its reference ratio is folded into `flutterDepth` in prepare. The shared random input is left unscaled, so neither process receives the other's compensation. Existing coefficient evaluation is deliberately retained to preserve the reference stream.

## Behavioral PCM evidence

[BodyNoiseRateTests.cpp](../../../../tests/BodyNoiseRateTests.cpp) uses only public calls. A requested fundamental above host Nyquist makes the existing harmonic-rejection path silence the modes while preserving normal note initialization and the complete random/filter/envelope path. At the 8 kHz/8 kHz limit, the public internal-frequency clamp lands at 0.49Fs, still above the complete rejection edge. A normal organ provides an exactly silent control. No private state is inspected or modified, and the tests do not implement the production variance formula.

The standard 48 kHz/2x flute reference is **0.000932057527 RMS** in the new three-seed capture. Corrected 48 kHz/4x and 8x values are **0.000930369344** and **0.000931292611**. Three seconds of steady PCM are pooled per rate, after the contact transient. Bowed, clarinet, oboe, bassoon and brass separately pass sustained-noise comparisons.

Contact measurements pool **96 seeds per model/velocity/rate**, integrating the first 25 ms including filter startup, onset fade and contact decay. Piano, tine, clav, steel and marimba are covered at velocities 0.2 and 0.9, with 88.2/192/384/768 kHz comparisons against 96 kHz. These are actual finite-onset energy measurements, not an inference that stationary compensation must also preserve every short transient.

Flutter is measured separately in sounded PCM. A carrier at host/4 leaves only the flute fundamental; its fundamental dark/bright weights coincide. Subtracting an identical-seed, harmonically rejected capture removes additive breath. Two carrier samples a quarter cycle apart recover the amplitude envelope without knowing oscillator phase. Three seeds and six pooled seconds measure envelope variance. The wider **0.8 dB** test tolerance reflects the much smaller number of independent observations in a 19 Hz process; corrected observed deviations range from −0.05602 to −0.36810 dB.

### Reference-stream preservation and negative control

The three permanent 4,096-sample noise fingerprints were captured **before A05 changed the header**, at 96 kHz internal / 48 kHz host, MIDI 60, velocity 0.8, colour 0.5, decay 1, seed 42, and a 48 kHz requested tick pitch. Flute, piano and steel hashes remain exactly `741fa1bfadfb90b0`, `c86def2df355d73f` and `43898cb5b1e5abab`. These fixtures constrain public PCM, including note-on RNG draws and contact decay, rather than reproducing the implementation formula.

For broader preservation and the negative control, a separate include tree was copied from the current shared source and **only A05's prepare/gain/helper/tick changes were removed**. A04/A06 changes remain in that control. The same new behavioral test built against that include tree fails 52 checks, while all three reference fingerprints still pass. The retained fingerprint probe additionally renders all 19 enabled models, notes 24/60/96 and three seeds, with a pressure change. Including the noise-only fixtures, **`cmp` confirms 1,413,120 float samples / 5,652,480 bytes are identical** between A05-scoped before/after captures. The initial historical snapshot differs for some nonexact onset pitches after A06's legitimate formant correction, which is why that snapshot is not used to attribute broad sample differences to A05.

The existing `InstrumentBodyTests.cpp` suite also passes **2,077 checks**, rendering **41,062,350 samples** across bodies, registers, touch and rates. It exercises headroom, damping, spectra and live expression beyond the new isolated noise measurements.

## Runtime cost and limits

The sample path adds **one double multiplication** to additive noise. Flutter replaces its old constant with a cached coefficient and adds no arithmetic operation. The two gains occupy **16 additional bytes per body**. Square roots, divisions and exponential reference calculations occur in prepare only; no new sample-time transcendental, allocation, lock, branch, or random draw is introduced.

A GCC 14.2 `-O3` microbenchmark records seven one-million-sample repetitions per model/rate, in before/after/after/before order. Concurrent-workspace timing varied substantially even for organ, whose noise block is inactive: its observed medians ranged from 18.14 to 32.85 ns/sample. Consequently these wall times do **not** support a precise overhead percentage. Raw logs are retained; the static one-multiply cost is the reliable claim. Dedicated CPU and full Engine integration checks are left to the assigned integration workers, rather than broadening this bounded change with unqualified timing claims.

This establishes rate consistency of the authored texture, not similarity to a recorded physical instrument or listening approval. It does not change or claim equivalence of the complete engine's anti-alias filters across quality modes.
