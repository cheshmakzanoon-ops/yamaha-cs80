## All-family source and routing review

The [25-program structural audit](../verification/a22/inventory-audit.json)
derives membership from the emitted bank and verifies it against both source
files. All 25 non-volume parameter vectors remain distinct. All body models,
mixes, variants and materials are zero: these are deliberately synthesized
circuit voices, so the `.7` piano, excitation-noise, string-body and choir fixes
do not supply their defining sound. Acoustic-replica criteria would be
inappropriate for this family.

| Programs | Retained defining source behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Poly Stab; Poly Brass Stab | Zero sustain on both ranks. The dry saw hit and the staggered brass-like filter crest have different onsets and decay lengths. |
| Poly Pluck | Zero sustain, a 3.4 s authored saw-body decay and a 0.19 s pulse-accent decay; separate 0.40/0.04 s releases. |
| PWM Poly; Hollow Poly; Square Poly | Independent pulse-width motion at 0.293/0.467 Hz; very shallow near-square motion; or fixed 50% pulses with zero PWM depth, respectively. |
| Resonant Poly; Poly Sweep | A brief resonant upper accent versus staggered filter attacks at 0.29/0.56 s over a sustained chord. |
| Detuned Poly; Wide Poly; Synthwave Chords | Direct 17-cent saw beating without chorus; separated saw/PWM stereo ranks with chorus; or broad saw layers with longer 1.55/0.94 s releases. |
| Velocity Poly; Poly Swell | Velocity Poly has strong initial-touch routes and no pressure routes. Poly Swell has small initial-touch levels and much larger pressure-level routes, 0.94/1.00. |
| Octave Poly; Fifth Poly | A quieter 4′ crown or 5⅓′ harmonic fifth, with separate upper filtering and shorter/lighter upper articulation. |
| Bright Poly; Dark Poly; Saw Pulse Poly; Sine Poly; Contour Poly | Open saw/pulse brightness; darker sustained color; distinct saw-body/pulse-accent roles; a dominant sine body; or deliberate keyboard brightness/level contours. |
| Ring Poly; Glide Poly; Tremolo Poly; Poly Sub Pulse | A 137 Hz ring carrier at restrained amount; 0.16 s polyphonic portamento; 5.4 Hz SUB/VCA motion; or a 4 Hz square SUB filter route. |
| Phased Polysynth | Saw-body/pulse-accent envelopes, phaser mix 0.32 and plate mix 0.07; short authored rank releases of 0.145/0.045 s. |

Sources: [Polysynth.inc](../../../../src/core/curated/Polysynth.inc),
[Modern.inc](../../../../src/core/curated/Modern.inc), and
[the recipe builder](../../../../src/core/CuratedLibrary.inc).
Times and rates in this table are authored controls, not inferred listening
judgments. Different vectors alone do not prove perceptual distinctness.

Twenty-one programs have authored pressure-to-brilliance and/or pressure-to-level
routes. **Poly Stab, Poly Brass Stab, Velocity Poly and Poly Pluck** have none;
all SUB pressure routes are zero throughout the family. All programs retain
initial-touch routes, with Poly Swell intentionally favoring pressure for its
largest dynamic change. The three chord-hit programs above have zero sustain
in both ranks; the remaining programs retain a sustained body.

MIDI **48, 55, 60, 64, 67, 72, 76, 79**, velocity **1**, pressure **1**, a
**six-second hold**, and a **four-second release**. Four current-only controls

active; there is no normalization. This adds **11 current and seven baseline
captures**, totaling **180 seconds** of audio, rather than a new all-bank matrix.

## Limits and disposition

The all-family claim is **25-program structural/identity coverage**, supported
by the existing whole-family refinement runs. The fresh long-chord result
applies to **seven selected programs**, one voicing, one sample rate and one
quality setting. It does not cover changing voicings, legato portamento,
per-note/MPE pressure, every controller gesture, native plugin hosting or
installation archives. No listening was performed; numerical equality and
headroom do not establish perceived quality or an acoustic likeness.

No production, test, script or workflow change is requested by this review.
Only the A22 review and its evidence were added; no commit or push was made.
