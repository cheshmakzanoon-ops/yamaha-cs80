## Two different routes share the tone names

The browser entries are complete, authored **PANEL performance programs**.
`OriginalCircuitLibrary.inc` derives each from the instrument template below,
applies its finish, then labels it Hardware Studies. Tone II swaps both channel
cards and all seven body controls, reverses the balance, and removes the
one-sided detune so the named rank stays at concert pitch. Twenty programs use
**39 active body ranks**; String 4 and Bass deliberately use circuit excitation.
All variants and materials are neutral. Source:
[library construction](../../../../src/core/OriginalCircuitLibrary.inc) and
[complete saved-value audit](../verification/a26/program-audit.json).

An original tone-button selection retains a different authority:

- `selectHardwareSource` sets the rank selector; it does **not** load the browser
  study. `readParameters` preserves that selector in `ControlFrame`, supplies the
  factory/memory control values, and leaves footage, pan and filter-bypass
  controls live. Ordinary complete-program recall resets both selectors to PANEL.
  [Actual plugin paths](../../../../src/plugin/PluginProcessor.cpp).
- In source-performance policy, `Engine::setControlFrame` compiles the selected
  original tab through `SourceFactoryTones`; the resulting wave, level, filter
  and envelope descriptors own its electrical route. The provisional
  `hardwareTonePatch` recipe alone is not this source-derived sound.
  [Engine](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) ·
  [source descriptors](../../../../src/core/SourceFactoryTones.h).
- Selector 11 recalls M1/M2 on ranks I/II; selector 12 recalls M3/M4. These
  memory selections and PANEL use the existing audio adapter, not a factory
  source column. The more detailed electrical memory/PANEL model in
  [MemoryPanels.h](../../../../src/core/MemoryPanels.h) explicitly remains
  **not wired into audio**. Its declared pot, follower and diode assumptions
  must not be reported as implemented physical-memory audio behavior.
- The [tone-button tooltip](../../../../src/plugin/CentreRow.cpp) identifies
  the matching revised browser study. It does not change this routing.

## Body suppression in the actual paths

The live Engine admits a body only when **source-performance policy AND selector
13 (PANEL)** are both true. Switching the selector can therefore leave dormant
body values in the main-panel state without admitting their sound. Separately,
`applyHardwareTone` resets all **seven selected-rank body controls**, including
Material, while retaining the other rank and global controls. The plugin uses
that helper to construct its factory-control tables; live selection is the
selector/ControlFrame path described above. This distinction avoids mistaking
a helper reset for an APVTS rewrite on every button press.
[Engine gate](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) ·
[helper](../../../../src/core/FactoryPresets.cpp) ·
[plugin](../../../../src/plugin/PluginProcessor.cpp).

The **unmodified `.6` A30 probe**, rebuilt against the current core, passes
**442 checks**. It exercises **28 rank/selector combinations** with a nonzero
tubular body versus body-off: all **26 non-PANEL combinations** (22 original
tabs plus four rank/memory combinations) are sample-identical; both PANEL
combinations differ. Every fixture is audible and finite. It also tests the
seven-control reset/preservation contract for **all 22 original tabs**.
[Probe source](../../2026.11.6/verification/a30/material-routing.cpp) ·
