# Sound explanation for final publication

This copy covers sound and scope only. The publication owner supplies the
separately accepted final-source downloads, native results and actual audition
archive details. Relative links below are valid from this file.

## Suggested user-facing copy

The existing library now carries the cumulative sound revisions across all
**521 musical programs**, with **all 523 names, categories and program
addresses preserved**. No presets were added. The two Init programs retain
their starting controls and match their earlier stereo output exactly in the
twelve tested comparisons. Compared with the published `.4` bank, 493 musical
programs change controls beyond the declared level/pan exclusions; another 28
change active additive VCA baselines that contribute to touch response.
[Catalogue and parameter evidence](../../reviews/a12.md) ·
[Init comparisons](../../reviews/a27.md).

The cumulative work gives the existing families more specific attacks,
independently decaying partials, body color, release and playing response. It
retains the dedicated tube, gong, bell, comb, tine, banjo, koto and harp
profiles, plus separate xylophone, steelpan and Ah/Oo variants. Deliberately
electronic presets retain their ring, PWM, pressure, motion and sequence
identities. These are authored synthesized instruments with documented
spectra and losses; their names do not certify exact acoustic replicas.
[Material and variant evidence](../../reviews/a41.md) ·
[All-family review](../../reviews/a54.md).

**The `.7` continuation preserves every `.6` preset value and corrects four
body-rendering behaviors:**

- **Piano tails:** the principal partials above harmonic six now receive the
  intended prompt damping, reducing the isolated upper ringing measured in
  low-note tails. The slower low-order courses remain. Try Stage Keys or Soft
  Keys on low held notes. [Piano mechanism](../../reviews/a04.md) ·
  [Complete-preset comparisons](../../reviews/a13.md).
- **Breath and contact texture:** separate compensation keeps the authored
  noise-band and flutter strength more consistent as internal rendering rate
  changes. Try sustained Flute or Solo Violin in realtime and HQ. This repairs
  texture energy; complete effects and anti-alias paths can still differ.
  [Rate evidence](../../reviews/a05.md) · [Brass effects boundary](../../reviews/a16.md).
- **String bends:** standard guitar, bass, clavichord and harpsichord harmonics
  move through a fixed body response, preserving the accepted note's decay
  and excitation history. Try a slow bend and return on Nylon Pluck or Steel
  Pluck. [String response](../../reviews/a06.md) ·
  [Plucked integration](../../reviews/a15.md).
- **Low choir detail:** Ah/Oo can use up to 48 harmonics, restoring upper
  voiced detail where the former 24-mode bank ended too soon. Try Choir Pad
  and Dark Choir across low and high registers. The deepest notes still reach
  the cap before their full upper vowel regions; a later downward bend keeps
  the onset bank. [Vocal coverage and limits](../../reviews/a35.md).

The measurements distinguish cumulative progress from this increment.
Relative to `.4`, **519** musical programs change the matched default audio
metrics, and **Pressure Lead** and **Electric Leads 136** change their measured
waveforms when aftertouch is applied. Relative to `.6`, **67** default metric
vectors change; the four mechanisms are structurally applicable to **256**
programs under the relevant pitches, gestures and rates. The bank probes use
category-specific MIDI notes 48/60/67/72, a three-second hold and one-second
tail. They measure changes, not listening preference. Focused longer notes,
chords and controller performances supplement that bank comparison.
[Complete signal accounting](../../reviews/a33.md) ·
[Long-note scope](../../reviews/a34.md).

The remaining limits are specific. These profiles do not solve complete
coupled hammer/string, soundboard, membrane, shell or vocal-tract mechanics,
and the evidence contains no human listening approval or demonstration that
every name is indistinguishable from a recorded instrument. Low choir also
uses more processing: the recorded shared-host eight-note Engine comparison
has paired median increases of about **15% for Ah and 17% for Oo** at 48 kHz/2×.
Those are bounded core measurements, not a DAW deadline guarantee.
[Mechanism limits](../../reviews/a41.md) · [Runtime evidence](../../reviews/a37.md).

To load the cumulative factory programming, open **Library → FACTORY LIBRARY**,
choose a program and press **LOAD / RELOAD**, then play fresh notes. Existing
projects retain their stored controls. Body-enabled saved patches still run
the corrected engine and can therefore sound different without a parameter
change. [Recall and saved-state behavior](../../reviews/a27.md).
