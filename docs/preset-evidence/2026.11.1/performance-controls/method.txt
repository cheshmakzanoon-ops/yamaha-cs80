# Performed controller roles — assignment 33

The final `2026.11.1` factory bank passes 60 bounded checks across five programs,
ten renders and 64 seconds of audio. No production source was changed.

## Scope and result

- Pressure Lead: the first unpressed held note produces exact digital zero.
  Full pressure reaches −26.90 dBFS RMS; easing pressure returns it to
  −81.26 dBFS before note-off, a 54.36 dB reduction.
- Wah Lead: pressure introduces rhythmic filter motion, with the observed
  main-rank low-pass spanning 1,107–2,429 Hz. The fitted audio brightness
  oscillation increases from 7.67 to 140.62 Hz peak-to-peak in the stated window.
  Oscillator pitch history remains identical to its unpressed control.
  The audio fundamental shows some phase modulation from the moving filter;
  this is reported separately from oscillator vibrato.
- Aftertouch Vibrato Lead: main-rank pitch reaches 47.94 cents peak-to-peak
  under full pressure, versus 0.14 cents of background variation in the control.
  Fundamental-demodulation analysis also observes the motion (44.58 cents
  peak-to-peak at the fixed nominal-rate fit), supporting the description of
  roughly a quarter-tone excursion.
- Velocity Keys: hard versus matched soft strike gives +10.53 dB onset and
  +10.47 dB held level. A following soft strike returns within 0.000003 dB of
  its all-soft control.
- Velocity Lead: the same comparison gives +11.41 dB onset and +11.48 dB held
  level. The following soft strike returns within 0.000001 dB of its control.

All captures are finite and below full scale; the highest sample peak is
−16.49 dBFS. Every sequence retires all voices. Pressure presets exercise
note-off both after pressure has returned to zero and while pressure remains
full. Full-pressure release excerpts fall below −118.85 dBFS RMS by 0.6–0.8 s
after note-off. There are no test, parameter, or acceptance-policy changes.

## Exact method

Use `events.csv` for every event. The `summary.json` method section records the
timeline, analysis windows and formulas. All notes are MIDI 60, at 48 kHz.
The pressure cases use velocity 0.8 and pressure ramps updated every 5 ms.
Matched controls alter only controller events; factory parameters are intact.
Raw output is stereo little-endian IEEE float32 with no normalization.

Rank telemetry is captured every 10 ms. Pitch and filter cutoff are actual
render-time rank diagnostics. Audio fundamental motion is estimated separately
by complex demodulation around MIDI 60 and a fifth-order 70 Hz lowpass applied
forward and backward. A sinusoidal least-squares fit with constant and linear
trend measures amplitude at the nominal modulation frequency. It does **not**
independently measure the exact modulation rate, especially because these
presets include pressure-to-SUB-speed routing.

Audio brightness uses the power-weighted centroid between 150 and 8,000 Hz,
with a 4,096-sample STFT and 240-sample hop. All modulation comparisons use
1.2–1.9 seconds, after the rising pressure ramp. Oscillator/filter modulation
and audio metrics are kept separate; a moving filter can change audio phase
without introducing oscillator pitch vibrato.

## Reproduce

From the repository root, with the final core target built:

```sh
mkdir -p build-w06-performance-controls
g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Isrc build-w06-performance-controls/probe.cpp build-audit/libcs80_core.a -o build-w06-performance-controls/probe
./build-w06-performance-controls/probe > build-w06-performance-controls/run.log
python build-w06-performance-controls/analyze.py > build-w06-performance-controls/analysis.log
```

The analyzer requires NumPy and SciPy. `summary.json` identifies every input,
source, render and trace by SHA-256. Large raw renders are disposable and can
be regenerated with the probe; preserve their hashes when omitting them from
a published dossier. Compact evidence comprises this file, `summary.json`,
`probe.cpp`, `analyze.py`, `captures.csv`, `events.csv` and the run log.

This is a representative software-behavior audit, not a listening-panel result,
a physical-CS80 calibration, a DAW compatibility test or coverage of all notes,
controllers and performance histories.
