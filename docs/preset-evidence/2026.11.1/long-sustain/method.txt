# Long-sustain headroom check — assignment 39/60

**PASS: 96/96 checks, 12 captures, 216 seconds of generated stereo audio.**
No production files or factory values were modified. The probe retains only
metrics, keeping its audio memory to a 128-frame block.

## Why these programs

The whole-bank audit and normal audition excerpts have bounded holds. This
matrix checks late envelope peaks using twelve representatives with at least
1.15 seconds of authored amplitude attack in one rank. It includes the longest
attack cases, several synthesis families, Sweep Pad's slow filter crest, and the
requested Slow Pad, Cathedral Pad and Ether Pad. The five recently widened
ensembles were excluded because they already have a separate chord audit.
This is not the entire slow-program roster.

## Conditions

All factory values are retained. At 48 kHz, eight notes—MIDI 36, 43, 48, 52, 55, 60, 64
and 67—begin simultaneously at velocity 0.9 on channel 1. Pressure stays zero until
one second, rises linearly to full pressure from 1–3 seconds in 10 ms events, and
remains full through release. All eight notes release at 10 seconds; capture ends
at 18 seconds. The early RMS window is 0–2 seconds, the late held window 7–10, and
the tail window 17–18. Quarter-second windows record the whole phrase.

The early/late comparison combines envelope development and pressure; it does
not isolate envelope gain. Sample peak and RMS use actual output values without
normalization. RMS is the square root of mean L/R sample power.

## Reproduce

Run from the repository root with the identified final calibrated core library:

```sh
g++ -std=c++20 -O2 -I src build-w08-long-sustain/probe.cpp build-audit/libcs80_core.a -o build-w08-long-sustain/probe
./build-w08-long-sustain/probe > build-w08-long-sustain/run.log
python build-w08-long-sustain/analyze.py
```

`summary.json` records exact input/output hashes, the source-state qualification,
selection and all per-program factory attack/release values. The analyzer guards
the original inventory/library hashes, checks unique programs and contiguous
windows, and verifies the numerical summary. An intentionally different build
requires new provenance rather than being labeled as this same measured binary.
No WAV or raw-float audio files were created.

This bounded deterministic chord does not establish universal no-clipping
behavior, arbitrary DAW/performance compatibility, acoustic-instrument identity,
physical-CS-80 equivalence or a listening verdict.
