# Assignment 30: character across sample rates

**PASS — 183 of 183 checks.** Six refined Circuit programs preserve their
specified body/detail roles at 44.1, 48, and 96 kHz in this representative-note
fixture. No production files were edited.

## Conditions

The frozen `capture-snapshot` executable is copied from the final calibrated
`build-audit/cs80_preset_capture`. `inventory.json` identifies the exact bank.
Each program is captured at its factory settings, velocity 0.8 and zero
pressure, as the complete patch and with rank I / II isolated through the actual
mix control. No other sound parameters are changed; there is no normalization.

| Programs | MIDI note | Held duration | Role window | Later comparison |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Flute, Breathy Flute | 67 | 0.75 s | 300–500 ms | 500–700 ms |
| Harp, Kalimba | 60 | 1.2 s | 4–20 ms | 800–1100 ms |
| Sine Bass, Upright Pluck | 48 | 1.2 s | 4–20 ms | 800–1100 ms |

There is no release tail. This matrix totals 54 captures / 56.7 seconds of
unnormalized stereo IEEE float audio. Weighted rank reconstruction uses the
engine's float32 subtraction before promoting weights to float64 for analysis.
Reconstruction must hold over each exact role window as well as the entire
capture. The role limits are the existing character suite's authored bounds;
its struck-note fixture uses velocity 0.9, while this matrix deliberately uses
0.8 as requested.

Breathy Flute retains more sustained air relative to its body than Flute at
every rate. All four struck voices retain a present but subordinate onset,
then lose at least 12 dB of relative upper-layer contribution while the key
remains held. The wind air remains sustained. The body remains above the
suite's silence floor in every measured role window.

- Highest complete-patch sample peak: **−19.296 dBFS**.
- Nonfinite captures and samples exceeding full scale: **zero**, including
  isolated-rank captures.
- Largest weighted reconstruction residual: **−142.758 dB relative RMS**.
- All 183 checks pass; no threshold was changed after execution.

These are bounded software behavior checks. They do not establish perceptual
identity across sample rates, arbitrary-register behavior, polyphonic headroom,
release behavior, or physical/acoustic instrument equivalence. Sample-rate
waveforms are not expected to be bit-identical.

## Reproduction and evidence

From the repository root, with Python, NumPy and SciPy available:

```sh
python build-w06-sample-rates/verify.py
```

The script keeps existing frozen input copies when rerun. To capture a different
build, intentionally use a new output directory or remove those two local input
copies first. `summary.json` records the executable, inventory and script hashes.
`measurements.json` includes every command, WAV hash, role window and assertion.
`capture.log` contains the capture tool's raw output; `run.log` contains the
aggregate result. WAVs are diagnostic intermediates and need not be committed.
