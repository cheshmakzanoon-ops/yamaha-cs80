# Rapid articulation check — assignment 40

**PASS: 96/96 checks, eight 12-second captures, 96 seconds of generated audio.**
No production source or factory parameters were changed. The revised Harp,
Kalimba, Sine Bass and Upright Pluck accept repeated strikes while prior notes
are releasing, retain headroom through the allocation stress sequence, and
retire every voice after the final release.

## Performed conditions

At 48 kHz, channel 1 and zero pressure, the unchanged factory presets perform:

1. Four same-note strikes at 0, 0.45, 0.90 and 1.35 seconds, alternating velocity
   0.3 and 0.95 and holding each for 120 ms.
2. A twelve-note arpeggio starting at 2.00 seconds, with 90 ms between onsets,
   35 ms holds, alternating velocities, and offsets
   0/4/7/12/7/4/0/7/12/16/12/7 semitones from the base note.
3. A separate sixteen-onset allocation stress burst starting at 3.60 seconds,
   with 10 ms between onsets, 6 ms holds and offsets
   0/2/4/5/7/9/11/12/14/16/17/19/16/14/12/11.
4. Individual note-offs finish at 3.756 seconds. A releasing All Notes Off at
   4.05 seconds is followed by uninterrupted rendering through 12 seconds.

The rapid burst is deliberately faster than a normal musical phrase. It tests
allocation while releases overlap. All four ordinary repeated strikes develop
finite, nonzero body and strike envelopes in their first 40 ms. Their running
mix levels are recorded in `onset-levels.csv`; those levels include earlier
tails and are not isolated velocity-transfer measurements.

## Matched boundary comparison

Each control begins with the same preset, engine history, rendering blocks and
events. Only the tenth burst onset, at 3.69 seconds, and its note-off are omitted.
That onset reuses an active, released slot in all four factory captures.

- Every preceding sample is bit-identical between the pair.
- The immediate event-sample residual is at most **3.7253 × 10⁻⁹**.
- The maximum residual during the first millisecond is **8.9594 × 10⁻⁶**.
- During that millisecond, the residual derivative is at least **60.35 dB**
  below the control waveform's own maximum derivative.
- The later residual within the first 6 ms is nonzero in every pair, showing
  that the additional onset actually contributes sound.

This establishes a bounded, causal onset comparison. It does not treat the
later, intended pick/ping/click transient as a defect or claim a universal
perceptual absence of clicks. Allocation histories intentionally diverge after
the omitted onset; later differences are not interpreted as a local boundary
test.

## Reproduction and provenance

Run from the repository root, using the final calibrated core library:

```sh
mkdir -p build-w08-rapid-strikes
c++ -std=c++20 -O2 -Isrc build-w08-rapid-strikes/probe.cpp build-audit/libcs80_core.a -o build-w08-rapid-strikes/probe
./build-w08-rapid-strikes/probe > build-w08-rapid-strikes/run.log 2>&1
python build-w08-rapid-strikes/summarize.py
```

`summary.json` includes the exact input, executable and evidence hashes, all
per-capture statistics, envelope checkpoints and boundary measurements. The
probe hashes raw little-endian float32 audio in memory using the repository's
SHA-256 helper, then discards it. No raw WAV or PCM files were written.

The local library was compiled before publication with the final sound data.
Its inventory, library and executable hashes identify the tested artifact;
this is not an execution of a GitHub Actions-produced plugin or an external
DAW. Retirement is observed at render-block boundaries, at most 64 host frames
apart. These results do not establish acoustic-instrument identity or a
listening-panel quality verdict.
