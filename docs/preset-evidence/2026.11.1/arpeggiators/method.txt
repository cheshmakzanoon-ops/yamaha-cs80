## Performance and measurement

Each capture uses 48 kHz, ordinary Circuit, pressure zero, and all factory
parameters. Arp Bells receives MIDI 60/64/67; the other programs receive
48/52/55. Root/third/fifth velocities are respectively 0.45/0.7/0.9. Source
notes enter in fifth/root/third order before audio begins, independently
testing pitch sorting and preservation of source velocity/channel identity.

The source chord holds for six seconds. CC64 goes down at 4.5 seconds, all
source keys release at six seconds, and CC64 goes up at 7.5 seconds. Rendering
ends at nine seconds. Generated gates never become pedal-held notes. Releasing
all source notes stops generation immediately despite the pedal, and pedal-up
does not restart it. Every program retires all voices by **6.153542 seconds**.
All samples are finite and below full scale. Arp Bells still has a delay tail,
with final half-second RMS **−77.655 dBFS**; voice retirement is not a claim of
digital silence.

The probe observes public `VoiceInfo` records after every host sample. These
are actual generated note identities and held gates, not an estimate from a
parameter dump. It also measures and hashes the generated audio in memory.
No WAV or raw PCM files are written. The generated MIDI notes should not be
confused with acoustic fundamental estimates: rank registrations still apply.

Timing bounds were declared before execution: interval error at most 0.5 ms,
and gate-fraction error at most 0.002. No acceptance bound was relaxed after
execution. This is a bounded chord/phrase test, not a listening verdict,
external DAW test, host-tempo qualification, or arbitrary-performance guarantee.
