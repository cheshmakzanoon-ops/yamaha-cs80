  with 15 overlaps, reach 44 programs in this increment. The 521 cumulative
  musical cases comprise 493 with another old sound control changed beyond
  base rank VCA and 28 with base-VCA/touch-balance changes only. No new programs

- **C4 precision:** historical body checks preserve exact MIDI-note weights
  through C4 and traces for all 18 other standard body models; complete-preset

  correlated pairs. Both features improved in all 24 at uniform 15%/20%
  alignment; raw 10% gave 23/24 because of the A4 pp preattack-noise marker.
  The original raw rows and onset plots are unavailable after the reset.

  RMS changes **−3.832 to +5.542 dB**; the candidate chord peak was **−9.482 dBFS**
  with maximum chord RMS increase **4.175 dB**. These are historical observations,
  not an archived `.8` dataset or a universal headroom guarantee.

  Tine16 model. It remained roughly **40–42% more expensive than the old bank**.
  The retimed cached Stage Keys medians were still **1.127/1.097× audio duration**

  perfection claims.
