# A27 — all 56 Leads and their performance controls

## Identity and programmed roles

Compared all **56 Leads** with the published `.4` inventory: **24 original-address
programs and 32 curated programs**, including the two Modern leads. Every ordered
identity and address is preserved. Every program changes **5–37 previous
non-master controls**; all 56 non-master sound vectors are distinct. Six use
instrument bodies and fifty deliberately retain circuit excitation. New Material
selectors remain neutral. These are cumulative `.5` plus `.6` results, not a claim
that this review edited the recipes.

Source review distinguishes the pure sine/whistle roles from square and narrow
pulse, moving PWM, resonant and metallic sideband voices. The two interval leads
retain separate fifth/octave ranks. Glide, stepped glissando, ribbon, onset scoop,
trill and pressure vibrato each have explicit programming rather than a shared
large pitch wobble. Brass/Reed lead bodies remain supporting musical colors;
Drive Lead and Space Lead keep their intended effects. Original-address voices
inherit specific curated recipes with their authored finish. A lead name alone
does not imply a forced mono/duo voice mode, oscillator sync or an acoustic
instrument replica; those were not inferred from the category.

The suite checks Pressure Lead's intentional silence without pressure and its
near-independent key velocity at fixed pressure. Its enormous dB ratio to

Complementary captures cover **all 26 remaining Leads**: the 24 original-address
voices and Drive Lead/Space Lead. There are **55 complete-Engine captures**,
**99 seconds** at 48 kHz, MIDI 67, with a 1.3-second hold and 0.5-second tail.
Twenty-five programs receive velocities 0.25/0.85 at zero pressure. Electric
Leads 136 instead receives five pressure/velocity controls, because aftertouch
is necessary for that voice to sound. All 55 captures are finite and unclipped;
the largest peak is **−15.876494 dBFS**.

The ordinary soft/firm onset range is **4.838773–9.883079 dB**. The lower result
belongs to Velvet Leads 130, which inherits the pressure-led Aftertouch Vibrato
Lead role; it is consistent with that recipe's subordinate velocity response.
Electric Leads 136 is silent at zero pressure (the meter's −240 dBFS floor),
gains **10.638965 dB** between pressure 0.3 and 1, and changes **0 dB** between
soft/firm key velocities at fixed full pressure. Its required-controller behavior
is preserved rather than incorrectly classified as an inaudible preset.

## Scope and outcome

The structural comparison covers all 56 Leads; the existing suite's thirty
voices plus the complementary twenty-six collectively cover every program with
fresh rendered audio. Only the documented focused subset receives interval,
glide, ribbon or pressure-vibrato discrimination. Unique parameter vectors and
finite output do not establish perceptual uniqueness or a listening assessment.
