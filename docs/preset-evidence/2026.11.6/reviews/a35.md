## Scope and source review

[Engine](../../../../src/core/Engine.cpp) captures Model, Decay, Size, Variant
and Material through the accepted note's `InstrumentBody::noteOn`. Its running
body reads live Mix and Brightness while retaining the captured generator.
The body uses a private excitation seed and does not consume the circuit RNG.
The complete-Engine comparisons above verify selected consequences of those
paths, including both ranks; they do not establish every possible controller
trajectory or every longer voice-stealing history.

[MemoryEditing](../../../../src/plugin/MemoryEditing.h) intentionally copies
the 26 historical programmer controls. Its PANEL-to-PANEL and memory actions
exclude all software body extensions, matching their stated panel-row scope.
That behavior is separate from the complete authored rank swap tested here.
Original electrical source isolation and tab resets are covered by A30; this
assignment does not repeat those whole-source tests.

These are core runtime and production-block tests. They do not execute the
native host, APVTS, AU/VST3 wrapper or GUI. The native committed-source suites
remain responsible for host automation, saved-state and UI behavior. Waveform
identity or difference here establishes control isolation, not acoustic
instrument similarity.
