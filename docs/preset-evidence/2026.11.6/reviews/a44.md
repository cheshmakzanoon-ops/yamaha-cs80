## New tests measure rendered behavior, with limited reference claims

| Coverage | Observable contract | Reference limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Piano courses | Late-tail phase measurements, repeated-strike tuning stability, coherent partial displacement and varying excitation | The existing authored stiffness law supplies the unison reference; this is not an independently fitted piano or a stiffness-accuracy test. |
| Wind/brass response | Moving-note spectra compared with independently started destination notes, followed by return-to-onset response | It tests consistency of the authored response through a gesture. It does not establish that the response itself fits a measured instrument. Clarinet has the narrower return/onset cohort. |
| Direct material bodies | Modal spacing, relative decay, touch, finite output, selector fallback and high-mode attenuation | Sparse authored spectral targets are regression contracts, not complete acoustic spectra. |
| Complete bell/plucked presets | Material-only controls inside the running Engine, normalized spectra, contact/decay, touch and damping | The plucked distance alone cannot distinguish every compatible Material assignment; the separate strict inventory map must also pass. |
| State and inventory | Old IDs/ranges/defaults, actual next-note changes, held-note preservation, exact factory identities and required Material mappings | Structure or state correctness alone is not a listening-quality result. |

The six new C++ tests use public `InstrumentBody` or `Engine` calls and measured
output; they do not read private mode arrays or merely compare internal
coefficient literals. Some spectral target frequencies necessarily encode the
authored profile being protected. Their usefulness is regression detection,
not proof of an acoustic replica. [A37](a37.md) separately demonstrates that
uniform output gain cannot satisfy the nine material identity decisions and
explains the numerical sensitivity of an almost absent generic reference.
Its large mode ratios should not be reported as perceptual quality gains.

The reviewed negative-control record is substantive: preceding-source piano
geometry and moving-wind failures, disabled plucked response correction,
incorrect rank reconstruction, the strict inventory's structural/material
mutations, omitted modal cutoff, incorrect Material swapping, and gain-only
controls each target a specific causal claim. These controls are retained in
[A05](a05.md), [root wind](root-wind.md), [A09](a09.md), [A15](a15.md),
[A16](a16.md), [A32](a32.md), [A35](a35.md) and [A37](a37.md). They were inspected
as evidence; no broad duplicate execution was necessary for this audit.

## Registration and release-gate integrity

All **six new C++ tests plus the new comparison-contract test** are registered
directly under `CS80_BUILD_TESTS`, without an OS-specific condition. Each has a
bounded timeout. The two complete-preset tests link the actual `cs80_core`
Engine library. The source audit records their names, scopes and timeouts.

The command bodies of the four preceding macOS validator lanes are unchanged.
The fifth lane retains strictness 10, in-process validation, GUI coverage,
the exact `0x3c99748` seed, `pipefail`, and a required terminal `SUCCESS` line.
Neither fixed-seed lane depends on the preceding GUI lane succeeding: both run
after an attempted GUI lane, including a failure. Neither ignores its own
failure. Packaging has no status override, so a later passing seed does not
turn a failed earlier lane into a package-producing success path. The new lane
and its output are also covered by the retained workflow mutation checks.

The current [A51](a51.md) and [A52](a52.md) helpers correctly keep consistency
and parsing separate from qualification. Their synthetic tests reject missing
or incorrect fifth Mac lanes and ambiguous execution markers; the archive
binder pins the reviewed parser and the actual inspected ZIP/member/API
identities. Their deliberately false release/outcome flags must not be
rewritten into successful native results. Original authenticated job exits,
attempt/upload binding, package-to-validated-payload equivalence, actual native
test inventories, audition acceptance and public retrieval remain required.
The new compiled inventory must still undergo the strict `.4 → .6` comparison;
an unchanged synthetic fixture does not substitute for that step.
