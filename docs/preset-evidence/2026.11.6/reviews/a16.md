# A16 — Harp and Kalimba character-coverage review

## Finding and correction

Removing the two entire cases from the older `struckDetail()` test also removed useful coverage: full-voice and window-local rank reconstruction, the absolute primary-body audibility floor, and relative contact decay. The new `PluckedMaterialPresetTests` independently checks touch, sustained loss, gain-independent material spectrum and sustain-pedal damping, but it does not replace those reconstruction or absolute-body checks.

Added `materialContact()` for Harp and Kalimba, retaining the preceding acceptance limits:

| Contract | Retained limit |
| --- | --- |
| Weighted ranks reconstruct the complete voice | Relative RMS error below 1% |
| Reconstruction in 4–20 ms contact and 0.8–1.1 s held windows | Relative RMS error below 1% in each window |
| Primary body audible in both windows | RMS above 0.001 |
| Contact does not dominate the primary body | Contact/body RMS below 0.5 at onset |
| Contact recedes while the key is held | Late contact/body ratio below one quarter of its onset ratio |

Only the old requirement that an unrelated accent supply at least 8% of the onset is retired. The new recipes intentionally put the named material in both ranks. The material suite's identical-patch, material-disabled control checks that the intended spectrum survives the complete Engine independently of uniform gain. The Sine Bass and Upright Pluck gates and all breath/stereo thresholds are unchanged.

The replaced comment also no longer claims that the material suite proves tuning: it probes spectral shape at the nominal pitch, rather than making a separate pitch-error assertion.

## Actual verification

Compiled the complete character suite using C++20 and A11's isolated core library containing the final plucked recipes. It rendered **31.4 seconds at 44.1 kHz**: **128/128 checks passed**. The primary-body onset/held floors passed. Harp and Kalimba onset contact/body ratios measured **−27.2 dB** and **−36.1 dB**, explaining why the old −21.9 dB minimum no longer fits their intended roles. Their later ratios were −124.7 and −130.0 dB. All six reconstruction windows were approximately −143 dB relative error.

A temporary negative-control copy adds 0.10 to the body reconstruction weight only in `materialContact()`, leaving audio generation and all limits unchanged. It fails exactly the four local Harp/Kalimba reconstruction checks: **124/128 pass, exit 1**. This establishes that the restored local checks reject an incorrect reconstruction rather than accepting any material output.
