## Limits and conclusion

No production correction was warranted. The observations protect articulation, repeated slot reuse, mode/velocity identity and normal stopping. They are not sample-exact acoustic onset measurements, a listener preference result, acoustic-instrument equivalence, all registers or pressure gestures, or native-host qualification. Intentional ringing and delay can outlast generated-note ownership. The internal floating-point clock and callback-resolution observations do not establish sample-exact tempo timing. The descriptive audio digests are not retained PCM byte comparisons.
