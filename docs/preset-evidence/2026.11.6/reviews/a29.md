## Named roles and source review

The [current FX recipes](../../../../src/core/curated/Fx.inc) distinguish
naturalistic, mechanical and deliberately electronic roles:

| Role | Reviewed mechanism and intended behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Wind | Independently paced smooth random level/filter motion; airy onset precedes the lower wind bed. It avoids using the minimum-rate SUB pulse as a gust generator. |
| Surf | One-shot swelling surge and later spray; separate envelopes let bright foam drain ahead of the lower wash. |
| Thunder | Short broad crack, delayed low rumble, independent smooth random intensity and spectral movement, falling held-note tail. |
| Rain | Continuous noise layers with raised-cosine random motion; fine upper texture does not switch the entire bed in sample-and-hold steps. |
| Clockwork | Six-per-second retriggered zero-sustain envelopes produce ticks and gaps. The disabled square LFO does not masquerade as struck articulation. |
| Breath FX / Robot Voice | Pressure changes exhalation level; the robot's authored Ah body remains audible through the intentional ring/filter chain. |
| Siren / Alarm / Helicopter / Telemetry | Periodicity is intentional: defined pitch alternation, wail, rotor pulse or dry signaling. Randomizing these roles would undermine their descriptions. |
| Space Sweep / Deep Space / UFO Landing | Separate slow layer entrances and shared ring gestures; the UFO description correctly explains that a fresh sweep needs all notes and sustain released. |
| Laser / Scrape / Downer / Riser / Glitch | Distinct impact, downward release, long upward contour, sideband and rapid stepped-filter roles. Slow entries are not evaluated as immediate strikes. |

The [original-bank mapping](../../../../src/core/OriginalCircuitLibrary.inc)
inherits the intended source voices and applies distinct finishes. Its covered
Thunder variation, Dark FX 148, retains the explicit 165 Hz low-pass and level
overrides needed to preserve its low roll. No production correction was found
necessary during this review.

## Limits

Wind, rain, surf and thunder remain authored, keyboard-filtered noise models.
Their contour evidence establishes improved irregularity, layering and decay;
it does not establish identity with a field recording, room, weather event or
physical machine. Robot Voice is an intentional synthesized vowel and ring
effect. No listening-panel or acoustic-indistinguishability result is claimed.
Native host validation and release archives remain the coordinator's separate
gates. No production code, presets, test thresholds, commits or public links
were changed by this assignment.
