# A19 — Complete Brass family review

## Scope and identity

Reviewed all **59 Brass programs**: **33 curated and 26 original-bank**. The candidate inventory retains every name, category and ordered address from published 2026.11.4. Every program changes **2–8 previously existing non-volume controls**; the comparison does not count the appended material selectors or volume as improvement. **48** programs enable an instrument body; **11** retain deliberate circuit excitation. The original collection inherits individually finished existing voicings, including French Horn from Covered Horn, and does not add new programs.

[Per-program inventory differences and input hashes](../verification/a19/inventory-review.json) retain the exact comparison. The candidate is A15's local final-recipe development inventory, not a published native artifact.

## Named roles and mechanism review

Read the entire [Brass recipe source](../../../../src/core/curated/Brass.inc), the [original-bank mappings/finishes](../../../../src/core/OriginalCircuitLibrary.inc), and [Delay Brass](../../../../src/core/curated/Modern.inc), together with the earlier [brass effort review](../../2026.11.5/reviews/a14.md).

- **Covered Horn / French Horn:** darker, larger resonant body, very little detuning, rounded onset and level-led pressure. **Muted Trumpet:** smaller, brighter body and filtered nasal circuit edge, fast tongue-like contact, short breath release. Their role distinctions survive the shared brass generator.
- **Tuba / Low Brass / Trombone Glide:** low footage and larger bodies preserve the low foundation. Tuba uses one dry audible rank; Trombone keeps an intentional scoop and slide. Those pitch gestures are appropriate authored roles, rather than accidental modulation shared by every voice.
- **Sforzando / Stab / Crescendo / Swell:** separate onset, sustained and release contours remain explicit. Stab dies while held; Sforzando relaxes into its body. Long pad swells keep their longer rise and release.
- **Reed Brass:** the secondary oboe body is an explicit hybrid edge. **Synth Horns, Wah, Ring Breath, Tremolo, Fifth and octave voices:** retain their advertised electronic coloration and interval structure. Making these acoustic-only would erase their intended identities.
- The brass body's onset weights and moving-pitch correction use the same resonant function. Its resonances remain in Hz at the captured body size while harmonics move through them; gain is bounded and returning to onset pitch restores the original response. This is specifically relevant to Trombone and Brass Glide, not just a static patch-level comparison.

## Conclusion and limits

No production defect requiring a change was identified. All family identities and substantive programming changes are accounted for; the inspected recipes express distinct horn, mute, low-brass, accent, swell and electronic roles, with current complete-Engine checks supporting their touch and articulation. No production files, test thresholds or programs were changed. These are local signal/structure results. They do not establish listening-panel approval, measured acoustic replicas, or native release qualification.
