# A25 — complete Pads-family audit

## Preserved bank and authored roles

The cumulative published `.4` → candidate `.6` comparison preserves all **63
Pads identities and addresses**: 31 original-address programs and 32 curated
programs. Every program changes **4–54 previous non-master controls**; all 63
non-master sound vectors are distinct. The new Material selectors remain
neutral throughout this family. Five programs use vocal bodies, while the
other 58 deliberately remain electronic pads.

The reproducible [inventory audit](../verification/a25/audit.py) compares
binary32 values against the hash-pinned published inventory, checks complete
ordered identities, and records each program's changed controls. All current
core source bytes match committed source
`fcc3c8e055223fe4f786ca162ff46076c33471c8`.
[Per-program evidence and source hashes](../verification/a25/inventory-audit.json)

The source distinguishes staggered warm-body/upper-rank blooms, octave and
fifth registrations, slow filter sweeps, pressure-led swells, noise layers,
SUB breathing, ring drift and wide chorus. These are intentionally electronic
identities: replacing every pad with an acoustic body would remove those
roles. Original-address variants inherit the appropriate revised recipe and
retain their individual finish controls. Plate Pad adds a restrained plate
tail; LFO Sweep Pad uses a ten-second triangle-filter cycle and a separate
slower irregular pan movement.

Choir Pad uses Ah variants on both ranks; Dark Choir uses Oo variants with
the existing 16-foot/8-foot registration. Their body size, entrances, rank
balance and effort differ. Full body mixtures and broad panel filters expose
the vowel spectra; live fixed-frequency response keeps the vowel resonances
from moving wholesale with pitch. These are authored wordless singing
profiles, not fitted singers, phonetic articulation or a measured choir.
The 24-harmonic source also limits high-formant coverage at very low notes.
