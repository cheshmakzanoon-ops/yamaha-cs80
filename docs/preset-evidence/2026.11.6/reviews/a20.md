## Named roles checked in the recipes

| Existing programs | Distinguishing implementation |
| --- | --- |
| Flute; Breathy Flute | Flute harmonic body with separate sustained air; Breathy Flute has darker body, greater air contribution and a softer entrance. |
| Pan Flute; Recorder | Short independent chiff/tongue rank, with a sustained pipe body. Recorder keeps nearly straight pitch and a shorter release. |
| Clarinet; Bass Clarinet | Odd-harmonic/register-dependent clarinet profile. Bass Clarinet uses 16-foot registers and size 2.0, with a restrained upper reed rank. |
| Oboe; English Horn | Double-reed formants. English Horn increases body size to 1.35/1.30 and softens brightness/articulation without transposing its played note. |
| Bassoon | Separate low-frequency body/formant profile, 16-foot ranks and compact tongue/release. |
| Soprano Reed | Bright oboe-derived profile with smaller size 0.82, a narrow-pulse upper contribution and a restrained onset scoop. |
| Ocarina; Piccolo | Ocarina uses dark, nearly pure 4-foot voicing with trace air; Piccolo uses bright 2-foot ranks and much quicker articulation. |
| Whistle; Shakuhachi | Whistle keeps its second rank out of the mix and uses restrained glide; Shakuhachi has a stronger aspirated transient that settles and an authored onset bend. |

The low fixed amplifier floors leave playing effort responsible for most of
the level. Aftertouch, onset and release are programmed separately rather than
being identical settings applied to all fourteen voices. These distinctions
are present in source and in the resulting parameter inventory; their quality
as acoustic imitations has not been assessed by a listener in this review.

## Moving-pitch response

Re-ran `cs80_wind_body_formant_tests`: **957 checks, zero failures**. Maximum
normalized harmonic error is **0.202533 dB** against the unchanged **0.23 dB**
destination-response limit. The destination matrix covers flute, oboe,
bassoon and brass at three rates, three sizes and three pitch intervals.
Clarinet participates in the bounded bend/return-to-onset checks; it is not
part of the fresh-destination matrix because its accepted register/fingering
profile remains captured. This distinction matters when describing coverage.

The fixed-Hz response prevents an onset spectrum from simply transposing with
every bend. Returning to the accepted note restores the initial body response.
The correction retains the existing storage and control-rate processing.

## Limits and disposition

No new production defect or required code change was identified. The eight
flute-based voices share an authored harmonic generator, then use different
filtering, register, excitation, air and contour programming. Ocarina is not a
separately fitted vessel model; Recorder, Pan Flute and Shakuhachi are not
separately measured bores. Likewise, Soprano Reed deliberately specializes the
existing oboe profile. The engine does not claim a measured nonlinear
air-jet/reed-and-bore solution for these programs. These are remaining model
scope limits, not evidence of acoustic indistinguishability or a failed
listening assessment.

This assignment changed only review/evidence files. No presets, production
code, thresholds, public links or remote state were changed.
