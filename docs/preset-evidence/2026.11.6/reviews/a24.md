# A24 — complete Organ-family audit

## Identity and programming

Reviewed all **15 Organ programs**, all at curated-library addresses: the fourteen registrations in `Organ.inc` plus Tremolo LFO Organ in `Modern.inc`. Every ordered identity/address is preserved relative to published `.4`, every program has **4–7 changes to previous non-master controls**, and all fifteen non-master parameter vectors are distinct. Fourteen use harmonic instrument bodies; **Combo Organ deliberately keeps circuit pulse excitation**. The appended material selectors remain neutral.

The registrations have concrete differences: 8′+4′ and 16′+8′ flute combinations, a separate decaying 4′ percussion rank, same-footage reed color, different 16′ principal/flute pipe entrances, 8′+2′ bright/chapel stops, a subordinate 5⅓′ quint, and explicit tremulant/vibrato motion. Mellow Organ uses a single dark 8′ rank. Straight registrations use zero authored detune/drift/dispersion rather than a detuned lead substitute. Percussive Organ keeps its sustained lower rank while the upper percussion decays. The modern LFO registration retains its 4 Hz amplitude pattern and upper-stop aftertouch route.

The core fixes organ strike excitation and its seed, and the recipes leave velocity routes at zero. Sustained organ modes have no struck-body partial loss; the rank envelope performs key damping. Body decay values therefore do not create a hammer-like fade in these programs. This behavior is appropriate to the stated registration roles.

## Remaining scope

The body is an authored sparse harmonic registration, blended with circuit oscillators and filters. It is not a separately measured pipe, tonewheel, contact-keyclick, wind-chest, rotating-speaker or room model. Fast keying and the percussive rank do not establish a physical contact-click simulation. The supplied descriptions generally use registration and color language consistent with this scope. No human listening approval or equivalence to a particular acoustic/electric organ is claimed.

No production code, tests, gates, preset identities, new programs or public links changed. Native package qualification remains a separate release gate.
