# A15 — cumulative factory identity and material contract

The new `scripts/compare-preset-response-2026-11-6.py` verifies the cumulative
**published 2026.11.4 → candidate 2026.11.6** inventory. The existing `.5`
comparator is unchanged. The baseline bytes are authenticated against SHA-256
`0545f9a6ede82c72f4bbecc0e032cbc08f4de257b496f6b763b1911d607f9611`.
The matching baseline is retained at
`docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.5/baseline/inventory.json` and
`docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.4/continuation/final-inventory/inventory.json`.

## Observed candidate

An isolated Release build at `/tmp/cs80-a15-final` exported the final calibrated
nine-material recipes, including Gong body brightness 1. The complete capture,
executable digest and core source file hashes are retained in
[the binding record](../verification/a15/capture-binding.json).

- **523 ordered names, categories, inventory positions and host addresses are
  preserved:** 222 original programs followed by 301 curated programs. Library
  membership and local address are derived from that unchanged host partition;
  the native inventory does not contain a separate library field.
- All **521 musical programs** change at least one of their previous 200
  non-master-volume sound controls at IEEE-754 binary32 precision relative to
  `.4`. This includes the prior `.5` work; it does **not** mean 521 new recipe
  edits were made again in `.6`.
- **Init Patch and Init Circuit** retain their original 200 values, including
  master volume, and use neutral zeros for the four appended selectors.
- The original 200 IDs remain ordered; `.5` variant IDs stay at 200/201;
  material IDs append at **202/203**, for **204** total. A separate comparison
  to the retained `.5` inventory confirms its entire 202-ID prefix.
- All nine required material programs select their intended material on both
  active, compatible ranks. The remaining programs retain Standard material.
  Nonzero variant/material collisions, inactive selections and missing choices
  are rejected.
- **272 programs** enable instrument bodies; **251** remain circuit-only,
  including the two Init programs. There are no duplicate non-volume vectors.

## Negative controls and integration

`scripts/test-compare-preset-response-2026-11-6.py` passes **seven test groups**,
including **43 structural mutations**, two volume/material-only nonqualification
cases, duplicate JSON keys, nonfinite numbers, baseline digest mismatch and
output/input preservation. The CLI also accepts a valid baseline-derived
structural fixture, then rejects a changed identity without replacing its
previous report. Test fixtures are not production preset recipes.

[Retained test output](../verification/a15/negative-tests.log)

Recommended CTest registration:

```cmake
add_test(NAME cs80_preset_response_2026_11_6_comparison_tests
         COMMAND ${Python3_EXECUTABLE}
                 ${CMAKE_CURRENT_SOURCE_DIR}/scripts/test-compare-preset-response-2026-11-6.py)
set_tests_properties(cs80_preset_response_2026_11_6_comparison_tests PROPERTIES TIMEOUT 30)
```

This evidence proves structural coverage and the saved factory selections.
Parameter ranges, defaults and host normalization are absent from the inventory
and are independently tested by [A08](a08.md). A changed sound control need not
be on an audible route; this comparison does not prove perceptual improvement,
acoustic indistinguishability, native host validation or release qualification.
The retained candidate is an uncommitted snapshot. Re-run the comparator on the
committed CI audition inventory before binding a published archive.

No commits, pushes, workflow edits or unrelated source edits were made.
