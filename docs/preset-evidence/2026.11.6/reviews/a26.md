None belongs to the original 222-address collection. Every ordered name, category, index and host address matches published 2026.11.4. Every program changes **5–8 previous non-master controls** versus that published inventory, cumulatively including the retained 2026.11.5 work. All 25 non-master sound vectors are distinct.

All 25 are deliberately **circuit-only electronic instruments**. Their purpose is expressive synthesizer chords, not an acoustic-instrument replica. No material selector or new body model is necessary to distinguish their named roles. This review does not treat different parameter vectors alone as proof of perceptually distinct or superior sound.

## Programming review

The recipes use a common starting point but explicitly assign distinct rank and controller roles:

| Named role | Implemented distinction |
| --- | --- |
| Poly Stab, Poly Brass Stab, Poly Pluck | Both ranks have zero sustain, separate short upper accents and longer chord bodies; their filter crests and decay lengths differ. |
| PWM Poly, Hollow Poly, Square Poly | Independent moving pulse widths, shallow near-square movement, or unmodulated 50% pulse widths respectively. |
| Resonant Poly, Poly Sweep | A short resonant accent versus staggered filter opening over an already established chord. |
| Detuned Poly, Wide Poly, Synthwave Chords | Direct 17-cent beating without chorus, unequal stereo/PWM layers with chorus, or broad saw chords with a longer retro release. |
| Velocity Poly, Poly Swell | Velocity dominates the former; substantial aftertouch gain and brightness dominate the latter. |
| Octave Poly, Fifth Poly | A quieter 4-foot crown or a stable harmonic-fifth rank, with register-specific filtering and reduced upper sustain. |
| Bright, Dark, Sine, Saw Pulse, Contour Poly | Different wave mixtures, brightness, accent lengths, sustained body, release and keyboard contours. |
| Ring, Glide, Tremolo, Poly Sub Pulse | Modest ring sidebands; sustained portamento; a 5.4 Hz VCA route; or a 4 Hz square filter-color switch. |
| Phased Polysynth | Rhythmic saw/pulse layers with short upper decay, phaser movement and a short plate tail. |

## Fresh complete-Engine captures

Rendered **50 captures** covering every program at MIDI 60 and velocities 0.25/0.85, pressure zero, 48 kHz, a two-second hold and half-second release excerpt. Two additional captures apply pressure 1 at velocity 0.85 to **Poly Swell** and **Velocity Poly**. Total generation is **52 captures / 130 seconds**. These runs retain metrics without exporting WAV files.

- Every capture is finite and unclipped.
- Maximum pressure-zero peak is **−14.842320 dBFS**; including the pressure control, maximum peak is **−13.652377 dBFS**.
- The 24 velocity-led voices have **7.969756–9.244013 dB** onset contrast.
- Poly Swell has **3.888935 dB** onset velocity contrast, consistent with its deliberately pressure-led role.
- At the same firm velocity, Poly Swell gains **15.635573 dB** in the held-body measurement under pressure. Velocity Poly's matching control gains **0 dB**, preserving their different performance roles.
