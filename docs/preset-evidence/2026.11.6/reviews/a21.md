# A21 — complete Bass-family review

## Coverage and identities

Reviewed all **50 Bass programs**: 25 original addresses and 25 curated entries,
including Drive Bass outside the 24-source-recipe Bass file. Every ordered name,
category and host address matches the published 2026.11.4 inventory. Every program
has **1–15 changes in the previous 200 non-master controls**; all 50 non-master
sound vectors remain distinct. **20 programs use instrument bodies and 30 retain
circuit excitation.** The new material selectors do not redefine this family.

This is cumulative programming coverage, including the preceding 2026.11.5
work, rather than a claim that 50 recipes changed again in 2026.11.6. The
[structural audit](../verification/a21/structural-audit.json) lists each program
and each changed previous control. It is reproducible with the retained
[audit script](../verification/a21/audit_bass.py).

## Recipe and mechanism review

- Pluck Bass and Picked Bass combine independent string partial losses with a
  brief upper contact. Their outer contours and key release do not substitute
  for the body decay. Picked Bass retains its deliberately longer held string.
- Fretless Glide has a soft onset, longer main decay, restrained pressure motion
  and short portamento. Upright Pluck has a larger resonant-size setting, darker
  main string and brief finger/noise contact. Their differences are explicit
  recipe controls, not a claim of distinct measured instruments.
- Analog, resonant, pulse, sub, fifth and octave programs retain their intended
  electronic registers and spectra. Pulse Bass has shallow independent PWM;
  Sub Bass retains a stable sine; Rubber Bass has a short initial pitch scoop
  and a fully decaying contour. Those identities should not be converted to
  acoustic basses solely to satisfy the category name.
- The original-address finishes preserve the short contact when extending the
  main body, restrain resonance on acoustic-body layers, and retain low-register
  balance. Pedal Bass and its inherited Velvet Bass 90 keep organ-like velocity
  neutrality while using controlled keying and release.

The earlier [Bass/Plucked review](../../2026.11.5/reviews/a15.md) records the
baseline/candidate causal checks for aftersound, key damping and touch, including
43 failures of the revised 149-check suite on its .4 baseline. The coordinator's

repeat that long suite or recount it as a fresh A21 execution.

## Fresh whole-family low-register captures

Rendered **all 50 programs twice**, at velocities **0.25 and 0.85**, MIDI **36**,
48 kHz, pressure zero, **1.3 seconds held + 0.5 seconds released**, through the
complete Engine. Authored footage remains active; a 16-foot rank is therefore
below the played MIDI register. No WAV normalization, output rescaling or WAV
files were used: the capture tool computed metrics from its rendered samples.

**All 100 renders are finite and unclipped.** The largest firm-note peak is
**−17.246 dBFS**, in Neon Bass 92. The 48 touch-sensitive programs have early-window
firm/soft contrasts of **8.698–11.658 dB**. Pedal Bass and Velvet Bass 90 both
measure **0.000 dB**, consistent with their deliberately neutral keying.

## Realism limits and decision

Fingered, picked, upright and fretless patches still share an authored harmonic
bass-body model, shaped by their distinct contact, size, brightness, filtering,
loss and playing controls. It does not separately solve fret contact, a
measured pickup, bridge mechanics or an acoustic instrument's coupled cavity.
The Standard Bass spectral response is captured at onset during live pitch
changes; the new fixed-Hz material correction currently covers the new plucked
materials, not Standard Bass. These are concrete source-level simplifications,
not a failed listening test or evidence that every bass sounds interchangeable.

The all-program low-register check found no nonfinite, clipping or touch-role
regression requiring a production change. No source, acceptance threshold,
identity, native result or public link was changed by this review. No human
listening assessment or indistinguishability claim is made.
