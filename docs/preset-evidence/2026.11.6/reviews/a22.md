## Distinct authored mechanisms

- Marimba retains a fourth-tuned wooden spectrum, faster upper-mode loss and
  a softer contact. Xylophone uses its separate third-tuned bar variant in
  both ranks, with compact losses and a harder high-register excitation.
- Steel Drum selects the steelpan shell variant, retaining an octave and
  twelfth rather than the vibraphone's fourth-tuned bar. Vibraphone has its
  own longer metal losses, tremolo and key damper response.
- Glockenspiel and Celeste Bells share a bar family but differ in footage,
  excitation brightness, filtering, contact and damping. These are authored
  distinctions, not separately fitted physical instruments.
- Ring Bell, Crystal Bell, Ring Sweep Bell, Metallic Keys and Ring Mallet
  deliberately keep electronic ring coloration. Bell Pad and Ambient Bells
  intentionally sustain a softer bed. Fifth/octave courses in Bell Choir,
  Clock Bells and Chimes retain their stated musical roles.
- A10's five new material recipes use compatible specialized profiles in both
  ranks. Tubular ratios are source-derived; their weights/losses and the
  other profiles are authored. The gong's delayed upper modes approximate
  blooming metal resonance; they do not solve nonlinear mode coupling.

The source varies strike phases/contact without randomizing these bar-mode
ratios. This review did not perform a new repeated-note perceptual assessment.

The retained [2026.11.5 mallet review](../../2026.11.5/reviews/a12.md) documents

## Fresh register and bounded-output checks

Added **36 complete-Engine metric captures** of the retained eighteen programs
at MIDI **84**, velocities **0.25/0.85**, pressure zero and 48 kHz. Each holds
for 0.55 seconds and releases for 0.15 seconds. All samples are finite and
below full scale. Early soft/firm contrast spans **6.713–8.179 dB** and the
largest peak is **−19.797 dBFS**. This extends the focused retained-recipe
coverage upward; it is not a long-tail or all-key listening test.

An independent direct-gong probe covers **216 cases**: six notes from 12 to
127, three host sample rates, both resonant-size endpoints, both partial-decay
endpoints and three seeds, at full strike. Brightness alternates across its
complete range while delayed modes enter. Across **345.6 generator seconds**,
there are zero finite/bound failures and maximum absolute sample **0.612346113**.

The gong's full-strike modal normalization gives a conservative modal bound
of 0.80. The contact coefficient is at most 0.017; the two-pole noise state's
simple conservative bound is 2, rather than assuming it is constrained to 1.
Consequently **0.834 plus numerical tolerance** is a defensible conservative
generator bound. The probe uses 0.8341; it does not certify polyphonic final
mix headroom. A10 separately records the actual factory eight-voice peak at
−15.275 dBFS. No limiter or gate relaxation is involved.
