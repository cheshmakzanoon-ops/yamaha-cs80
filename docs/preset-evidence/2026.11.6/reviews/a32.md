# A32 — material boundaries and sanitized execution

Reviewed the nine material specializations at committed source
`fcc3c8e055223fe4f786ca162ff46076c33471c8`. The three included production headers
match that commit byte-for-byte. No production code or existing acceptance
threshold was changed.

## Executed coverage

The [direct generator probe](../verification/a32/extremes.cpp) passes
**3,236 checks, zero failures**, observing **11,223,296 rendered samples**.
Maximum absolute output is **0.715956986**. The same executable is instrumented
with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer; it exits zero without
sanitizer diagnostics.

| Cohort | Cases | Conditions and required result |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Combined boundaries | 1,440 | All nine materials; 44.1/48/96/192 kHz host rate with twice-rate internal processing; MIDI 0/24/60/96/127; velocity 0.01/1; size 0.5/2.5; decay 0.25/4. Alternate brightness and pitch at every 16-sample boundary, including a far-above-band pitch. Every sample must be finite and below the conservative 0.8341 bound; reset must disable the body and return exact silence. |
| Selector fallback | 211 | All twenty models, each incompatible material and the adjacent invalid choices −1/10. Neutral and unsupported selections must produce sample-identical output during pitch movement. |
| Host-rate modal cutoff | 32 | Eight materials without sub-fundamental modes, at all four host rates. Raise pitch until all their modes lie above the host cutoff; require finite bounded transitions and less than 1e−15 late output after the contact transient has decayed. |
| Malformed controls | 27 | All nine materials with NaN, positive infinity and negative infinity at onset and live controls, plus invalid prepare rates. Require finite bounded defaults, exact silence for rejected frequencies, and an unchanged next accepted sample after rejected-frequency calls. |

The common amplitude bound follows the largest material ceiling, Gong's 0.80,
plus a conservative noise-state allowance of twice its 0.017 contact
coefficient, with numerical tolerance. Other new materials have lower combined
ceilings. [A22](a22.md) separately examines the Gong bound and longer delayed
mode entrances.

Tubular bell is deliberately excluded from the blanket cutoff-silence cohort:
its low sub-nominal modes can remain below Nyquist at the clamped control
frequency. It participates in every combined-boundary and malformed-control
cohort. The cutoff check establishes functional attenuation of the named modal
path; it is not a full spectrum/alias measurement of the oversampled Engine or
its decimator.

## Negative control

A temporary copied header replaces only
`const double antialias = t * t * (3.0 - 2.0 * t);`
with `const double antialias = 1.0;`. The copied-header probe exits **1** with
**exactly 32 failures**, all belonging to the above-host-cutoff assertions.
The other 3,204 checks pass. Production headers are untouched. This verifies
that the cutoff assertions detect the disabled mechanism, rather than merely
accepting bounded oscillators.

## Reproduction and scope

From the repository root, compile the probe against the frozen source:

```sh
c++ -std=c++20 -O1 -g -fsanitize=address,undefined -fno-omit-frame-pointer -Isrc docs/preset-evidence/2026.11.6/verification/a32/extremes.cpp -o /tmp/cs80-a32-extremes
ASAN_OPTIONS=detect_leaks=0 /tmp/cs80-a32-extremes
```

Compiler: GCC 14.2.0. LeakSanitizer is disabled; the result does **not** establish
absence of leaks or callback allocations. It does establish successful
AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer execution for the exercised new
branches. The negative control uses the same probe and `-std=c++20 -O2`, with a
temporary include directory containing the copied and deliberately mutated
header plus the unchanged Material/Variant headers. The exact mutation, source,
harness and executable hashes are retained in the receipt.
