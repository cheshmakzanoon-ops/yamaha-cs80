## Conclusion

The work has concrete signal-path, recipe and dynamic-response improvements. The available evidence does **not** establish exact named-instrument reproduction, human-rated improvement for every program, or a numerical realism score. This is not merely a general qualification: several named acoustic presets deliberately use shared model approximations with identifiable missing behavior. Native test and validator success cannot close that acoustic-evidence gap.

No additional routing regression requiring a source change was found in this review. Do not invent parameter changes to conceal these model limits or restart the frozen builds for speculative acoustic adjustments.

## Controls that should not be credited as audible changes

`Partial decay` is inactive for sustained bowed, wind, brass and organ bodies because their modal loss is zero. Non-neutral literals in those recipes must not be counted as audible improvements. At body mix 1, oscillator waveform, sine and VCF-output-level literals are replaced by the body signal; panel HP/LP filters, envelopes, touch and rank output remain active. Counting every edited source literal would overstate audible work.

## Exact claim boundaries

- All **521 existing musical recipes** have non-volume programming changes relative to the preceding bank, while both Init programs and all 523 program identities/order/addresses remain preserved. This is parameter coverage.
- The historical matched rendering at **59d489d** shows changed spectral and relative-envelope metric groups for all 521 under the documented fixture, including the two pressure-only fixtures. Metric change is not a listening result or universal improvement score; small changes can clear rounding tolerance without established audibility.
- The tine and bowed continuation corrections have narrower, meaningful direct-model and complete-preset regression evidence. Their configured model reach is **68 existing programs**; do not call that 68 detailed listening tests.
- The audition contract is **138 WAVs / 74 programs**, with a complete inventory. It is not all 523 programs listened to and not a reference-comparison panel. Actual final artifact qualification remains the release owner's task.
- Native builds, CTest, headroom gates and plugin validation establish their stated compatibility/numerical properties. They do not validate instrument realism.

## Suggested concise release wording

> Reprogrammed all 521 existing musical programs while preserving the complete 523-program bank and its host/MIDI identities. The revision corrects body-filter routing, improves register-dependent piano attacks and sustained effort response, and adds focused fixes for tine-piano dynamics and bowed-body pitch response. In the documented tine fixtures, soft-to-hard body contrast increases by about 4 dB while hard-strike endpoints remain unchanged. The acoustic-style presets remain authored synthesized interpretations: they are not calibrated reproductions of particular recorded instruments, and shared-model limits remain in presets such as Kalimba, Xylophone and Steel Drum. The linked audition files provide concrete sounds to evaluate; automated signal and compatibility tests do not establish indistinguishability or a universal realism score.

This wording reports substantial implemented work without falsely declaring the user's “EXACTLY” / perfection objective proved.
