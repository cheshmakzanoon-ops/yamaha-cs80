## Remaining work and decision

The [existing feasibility review](continuation-2026-10-31/cpu-feasibility-followup.md)
identifies whole-filter and oscillator work as the material held-note regions.
Its older instruction profile attributes 29.92% to the full filter path and
27.32% to oscillator ticking. Within the filter path, previous convergence
shortcuts already make further Newton/Jacobian work small in that fixture.
These are instrumented instruction shares, not native-time shares or a proof
that a target is impossible.

The [idle follow-up](qualification-2026-11-01/cpu-idle-follow-up.md) identifies
stable readout/controller bookkeeping as a credible next investigation. Source
readouts already skip unchanged memoryless circuit solves; much remaining cost
constructs and compares drive records and advances observable envelope clocks.
Touch charge, RNG history, oscillator phase, tails, and event-set controller
restoration remain live. Omitting them because no voice is audible would
change later sound or diagnostic state.

A fresh review considered an exact zero-state shortcut in `TouchVoice`, but
did not implement or evaluate it: the old profile attributes only 5.05% of
whole idle instructions to the complete touch loop and wrappers, of which a
shortcut could remove only a subset. Held initial-touch capacitors remain
nonzero after ordinary notes, and signed-zero, caller FP-mode, coefficient,
acquisition and wake transitions need their own prospective contract. That
bounded opportunity does not justify claiming either CPU target closed or
delaying integration of the already qualified work.

The larger proposed route remains independent-lane vectorization of complete
filter/oscillator kernels with the frozen scalar oracle, per-lane convergence,
state restoration, original mix/RNG order, and scalar fallbacks on unqualified
targets. Component arrays already present in `Engine.h` are groundwork for
that route; they are not evidence that lane SIMD has been implemented. Both
original CPU budgets remain listed as open without changed tolerances.
