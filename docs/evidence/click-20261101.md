## Proven mechanism and repair

The selected PR6 engine stops mixing a rank once both amplitude envelopes are
Idle. Its later `relaxIdle` call decays the stored VCA coupling state without
contributing that capacitor's signed output to the mix. With the existing
model, zero upstream drive does not imply zero downstream output:

```
c[n] = c[n-1] * exp(-1 / (0.1 * internal_rate))
y[n] = -c[n]
```

The source/assumption boundary is unchanged: M41 and service p52 identify the
IG00151 input/control connections; the **downstream** 1 uF / 100 k coupling,
offset and feedthrough magnitudes are declared inferences in
[IG00151_VCA_MODEL.md](../IG00151_VCA_MODEL.md), not hardware measurements.
A different downstream network, or an actual downstream mute switch, would
require a different independently sourced model. This correction makes the
current declared network internally consistent. It changes no time constant,
pedestal value, attack/release law, waveform, phase choice or output gain.

Published audio correction:
`b75b4d653b6ed6f0aa5ef8ee99b670d83e975250`.
Its `src` subtree is `a153b5ec6ca3b64331488af7d32fa13d8d29a7ec`, identical to
locally qualified commit `754b4cccc6f0e9a37c8e98d221757b0deec19f30`.
The separate published V2 pin is
`fb43c04e68e91eea120add3a89e6866ef1e64e01`.

The fix keeps the zero-drive VCA recurrence and its signed output connected
at each internal sample. Oscillator and filter idle scheduling are unchanged;
the old decimated VCA discharge is removed to avoid counting time twice. The
original `1e-30` numerical zero floor is retained. Unlike the earlier PR7
candidate's `1e-8` shortcut, small residual charge continues decaying instead
of freezing. Ordinary note-off retains the discharge. Explicit MIDI All Sound
Off clears the VCA and restores its cold-LI convention on the next note.

The primitive oracle compares the closed path with both the analytic 0.1 s
exponential and the existing full `process(..., envelope=0)` path at every
sample, with the unchanged `1e-7` absolute truth and `1e-6` handoff bounds.
The engine oracle independently advances the observed charge by real host
elapsed time, so restoring a second decimated discharge cannot pass it.
The initial nonzero-charge assertions prevent a silent or zero-pedestal fixture
from passing vacuously.

window, chord/reference denominator and **score <= 1** limits are unchanged.
The physical-PANEL Bass requirement remains open at 48/96 kHz, as do the Funky

than at a demonstrated zero-time state replacement. The new capacitor repair
therefore must not be presented as closing those raw-score requirements.

The source limits and alternatives for the earlier cold-envelope and cold-time
repairs, and the independently mutation-qualified exposed-state event gate,
remain in [CLICK_EVENT_CONTINUITY.md](../CLICK_EVENT_CONTINUITY.md).
Further changes need source evidence or another demonstrated numerical defect;
the remaining ratios do not justify an output fade or envelope/pedestal fit.

## CPU measurement scope

Three `cs80_source_factory_benchmark` rounds were completed **before** this
cohesive audio correction and three after it. Every round used the unmodified
baseline suite, all 11 tone pairs plus moving and idle controls, host 48 kHz,
256-frame blocks and 128 held callbacks: 27 cases / 51 rows per round.
No `--quick` filter was used because that option forces a 128-frame block.
Every round completed with zero nonfinite output samples.

Concurrent root integration builds/validation occurred on this shared cloud
host. The archived wall/thread CPU measurements are diagnostics; they establish
neither a speedup nor a production CPU budget result. In particular, their wide
within-phase variation prevents attributing a before/after difference to this
repair. The dedicated serialized CI timing matrix remains the appropriate CPU
comparison. No 0.15–0.20x realtime or <=2 percent idle completion is claimed here.
